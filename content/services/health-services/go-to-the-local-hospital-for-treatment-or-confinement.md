# Go to a Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement — San Pablo City

A guide to hospital care, admission, and confinement in San Pablo City, Laguna.

> **For life-threatening emergencies, call 911 immediately or go directly to the nearest emergency room.**

## What

This service covers **hospital treatment, emergency care, and inpatient confinement** at San Pablo City public and private hospitals. It includes:

- Emergency room consultations
- Outpatient consultations and laboratory tests
- Inpatient admission and confinement
- Surgery and specialized procedures
- PhilHealth claims processing
- Medical assistance applications (for indigent patients)
- Hospital referrals to higher-level facilities

## Who can be admitted

Hospital admission is open to:

- **Anyone in need of medical care** (regardless of residency)
- **PhilHealth members** for covered benefits
- **Indigent patients** who can apply for Medical Assistance to Indigent and Financially Incapacitated Patients (MAIFIP)
- **Senior citizens (60+)** with 20% discount privilege under RA 9994
- **PWDs** with discount privileges under RA 10754
- **Walk-ins** for emergency cases (priority based on triage)

## When to seek hospital care

| Situation | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Severe bleeding, chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, seizure** | Call **911** immediately |
| **Persistent high fever (>3 days), severe vomiting/diarrhea** | Go to ER same day |
| **Scheduled procedures** | Follow doctor's appointment date |
| **Routine consultations** | Follow OPD schedule (typically Mon–Fri) |
| **Maternal labor signs** | Go to your designated birthing facility / hospital |

## Why use a city / district hospital

- **PhilHealth-accredited** — Most local hospitals are PhilHealth providers
- **Lower out-of-pocket cost** — Government hospitals offer subsidized rates
- **Senior / PWD discounts** — Mandated by national law
- **MAIFIP support** — For indigent patients via City Social Welfare
- **Familiar local network** — Easier coordination with barangay health centers

## How to admit / be treated

### Step 1 — For emergencies, dial 911
For life-threatening situations, call **911** or go directly to the ER. CDRRMO can also dispatch ambulance services: **0998 540 7171**.

### Step 2 — Bring required identification and records
- **Valid ID** of patient and companion
- **PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record (MDR)**
- **Senior citizen / PWD / Solo Parent ID** (if applicable)
- **Referral slip** (from RHU or another doctor)
- **Existing prescriptions, lab results, imaging**
- **List of current medicines and allergies**
- **Emergency contact numbers**

### Step 3 — Register at the ER or Admissions desk
- Provide patient information
- Indicate emergency contact
- Present PhilHealth and benefit IDs

### Step 4 — Follow triage and assessment
The ER team will assess urgency. Priority goes to the most critical cases. Be prepared for:
- Vital signs and history taking
- Laboratory or imaging tests
- Doctor's evaluation and treatment plan

### Step 5 — Process admission (if needed)
If admitted:
- Sign consent forms
- Admissions desk will process billing
- Coordinate room assignment
- PhilHealth and benefits will be applied to billing

### Step 6 — Visit the social services desk for assistance
If you cannot afford the costs, ask the **hospital social services unit** about:
- **MAIFIP** (Medical Assistance to Indigent and Financially Incapacitated Patients)
- **DSWD assistance** through the Crisis Intervention Section
- **PCSO / Office of the Mayor** medical assistance referrals
- **PhilHealth Z-Benefit** for catastrophic illness packages

### Step 7 — Discharge and follow-up
Before discharge:
- Settle the final billing
- Get prescriptions and follow-up instructions
- Schedule follow-up consultations
- Request a medical certificate if needed

## Important notes

- **Verify hospital details** before transporting — bed availability changes daily
- **Bring photocopies** of all documents — saves time at admissions
- **Coordinate with your barangay health center** for referrals and follow-up
- **Keep all receipts** for PhilHealth claims and reimbursements
- **Senior / PWD discounts** apply to the patient's portion of the bill (post-PhilHealth)
