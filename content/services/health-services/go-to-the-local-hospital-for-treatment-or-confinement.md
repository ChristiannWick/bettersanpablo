# Go to a Local Hospital for Treatment or Confinement - San Pablo City

For emergencies, call **911** or go to the nearest emergency room. This page helps residents prepare for hospital treatment, confinement, or referral in and around San Pablo City.

Services, bed availability, fees, emergency capacity, and PhilHealth handling can change. Call ahead when possible or ask your barangay health center or City Health Office for referral guidance.

## What to Bring

- valid ID of patient and companion
- PhilHealth ID or Member Data Record, if available
- senior citizen, PWD, solo parent, or other benefit ID, if applicable
- referral slip or prescription
- laboratory results, imaging, and previous medical records
- list of current medicines and allergies
- emergency contact details

## Admission Flow

1. Go to the emergency room or admissions desk.
2. Provide patient information and emergency contact details.
3. Present PhilHealth and benefit IDs.
4. Follow triage, assessment, laboratory, and billing instructions.
5. Ask the social service desk about medical assistance if the patient cannot afford costs.
