# Join Health Programs - San Pablo City

San Pablo City residents can ask their barangay health center or City Health Office about community health programs such as nutrition, dengue prevention, tuberculosis treatment, vaccination, and health education.

## Programs to Ask About

### Nutrition

- growth monitoring for children
- nutrition counseling for parents and pregnant women
- feeding or supplementation programs when available
- Barangay Nutrition Scholar activities

### Dengue Prevention

- clean-up drives
- search-and-destroy campaigns for mosquito breeding sites
- reporting of suspected dengue clusters
- health education in schools and barangays

### Tuberculosis Care

- TB screening and referral
- sputum testing instructions
- DOTS treatment support
- contact tracing and follow-up

### Vaccination and Health Campaigns

- routine child immunization
- school-based vaccination when scheduled
- adult vaccination campaigns when available
- health fairs and outreach missions

## How to Join

1. Visit your barangay health center and ask for the program schedule.
2. Bring your ID, health card, and any medical records.
3. Register with the assigned health worker.
4. Attend all follow-up visits, especially for TB treatment or vaccine series.
5. Watch city and barangay announcements for outreach schedules.
