# Join Health Programs (Nutrition, Dengue, TB) — San Pablo City

A guide to community health programs run by the San Pablo City Health Office and barangay health workers, covering nutrition, dengue prevention, tuberculosis treatment, and ongoing campaigns.

## What

This service covers participation in **public health programs** that target community-wide health concerns:

- **Nutrition Program** — Growth monitoring, supplementation, feeding programs
- **Dengue Prevention** — Search-and-destroy mosquito breeding, education
- **TB DOTS** — Tuberculosis screening, treatment, follow-up
- **Vaccination Campaigns** — School-based, adult, mass immunization
- **Health Education** — Community lectures, brochures, household visits
- **Mental Health Programs** — Counseling and referral services (when available)

## Who can join

Open to all **San Pablo City residents**, with priority for:

- **Children 0–5 years** for nutrition / growth monitoring
- **Pregnant and lactating women** for nutrition support
- **Households in dengue hotspots** for vector control
- **TB-suspect cases and confirmed patients** for DOTS
- **School-age children** for school-based campaigns
- **Adults with comorbidities** (diabetes, hypertension)
- **Senior citizens** for special health programs

## Why participate

- **Free or low-cost** — Most programs are funded by the city / national government
- **Prevention is cheaper** — Avoid hospitalization through early screening
- **Community impact** — Programs work best with high participation
- **Lifelong benefits** — Vaccinations prevent disease decades later
- **Civic responsibility** — Reduce community-wide health risks
- **Family protection** — Especially for households with vulnerable members

## Programs in detail

### 🍎 Nutrition Program

**Services:**
- Growth monitoring for children 0–5
- Nutrition counseling for parents and pregnant women
- Feeding / supplementation programs (when available)
- Barangay Nutrition Scholar (BNS) home visits
- Operation Timbang Plus (OPT+) annual weighing

**Who runs it:** City Nutrition Office + Barangay Nutrition Scholars

### 🦟 Dengue Prevention

**Services:**
- Clean-up drives in barangays
- Search-and-destroy missions for mosquito breeding sites (4S strategy)
- Reporting of suspected dengue clusters
- Health education in schools, barangays, workplaces
- Vector control / fogging in confirmed outbreak areas

**Who runs it:** City Health Office + Barangay Health Workers

### 🫁 Tuberculosis Care (TB DOTS)

**Services:**
- TB screening for symptomatic individuals
- **Sputum testing** instructions and referral
- **Directly Observed Treatment, Short-course (DOTS)** therapy support
- Free TB medicines for the duration of treatment (6+ months)
- Contact tracing among household members
- Follow-up monitoring

**Who runs it:** City Health Office TB Program + Barangay TB Workers

### 💉 Vaccination and Health Campaigns

**Services:**
- Routine child immunization (EPI)
- School-based vaccination (HPV, MR for adolescents)
- Adult vaccination campaigns (when available)
- Health fairs and outreach missions
- Mass deworming for school-age children

**Who runs it:** City Health Office + DOH Region IV-A

## How to join

### Step 1 — Visit your barangay health center
Ask the barangay health worker (BHW) or midwife about:
- Active programs in your barangay
- Schedule for clean-up drives, vaccination, screenings
- Eligibility and how to register

### Step 2 — Bring required documents
- **Valid government ID**
- **Proof of residency** (utility bill, barangay clearance)
- **Health record card** (yours and family members')
- **Existing medical records** for screening / referral
- **Child's vaccination card** for nutrition / immunization

### Step 3 — Register with the assigned health worker
- Provide name, age, contact, and program of interest
- For TB DOTS — sign treatment agreement
- For nutrition — submit child's growth data
- For dengue — register your household for monitoring

### Step 4 — Attend all scheduled activities
**Especially critical for:**
- TB DOTS — Missing doses can cause drug resistance
- Vaccine series — Skipped doses reduce effectiveness
- Nutrition follow-ups — Missing weighing breaks the monitoring cycle

### Step 5 — Watch for outreach announcements
- City Information Office bulletins
- Barangay loudspeakers
- City Facebook page
- BHW house visits

### Step 6 — Help spread the word
- Refer neighbors who would benefit
- Volunteer for clean-up drives
- Share information with your community

- **Barangay Health Worker (BHW)** is your first contact for all programs
- **TB treatment must be completed** — don't stop even if symptoms improve
- **Dengue 4S** — Search and destroy, Self-protection, Seek early consultation, Support fogging
- **Nutrition tracking** continues until child reaches school age
- All programs are **free of charge** for registered participants
