# Access Maternal Care and Child Immunization - San Pablo City

Pregnant residents and parents can start with their barangay health center or the City Health Office for prenatal care, family planning, newborn care, and routine child immunization.

## Maternal Care Services to Ask About

- prenatal check-ups
- tetanus vaccination for pregnant women
- iron, folic acid, and nutrition counseling
- blood pressure and weight monitoring
- family planning counseling
- referral to birthing facilities or hospitals when needed

## Child Immunization Services to Ask About

- BCG, Hepatitis B, pentavalent, OPV/IPV, PCV, MMR, and other routine vaccines under DOH schedules
- child growth monitoring
- nutrition advice and vitamin supplementation
- catch-up vaccination if doses were missed

## What to Bring

- mother's valid ID
- prenatal record or mother's book
- child's birth certificate or health card
- vaccination card
- PhilHealth details, if available

## Steps

1. Register at your barangay health center as early as possible during pregnancy.
2. Attend scheduled prenatal visits and keep your records.
3. Ask where to deliver and what documents are needed for referral or admission.
4. After birth, keep the child's vaccination card and follow the health center schedule.

For emergencies such as severe bleeding, seizure, difficulty breathing, or high-risk pregnancy symptoms, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency facility immediately.
