# San Pablo City: Free Check-ups, Basic Medicines, and Vaccines

San Pablo City residents can ask their nearest barangay health center or the City Health Office about consultations, basic medicines, vaccination schedules, PhilHealth Konsulta referral, and health outreach activities.

The official San Pablo services portal lists **Health Services** for city health centers, medical assistance programs, and health insurance enrollment. That service is marked **Coming Soon** on the portal, so residents should confirm current procedures through barangay health workers or city hotlines.

## Services to Ask About

| Service | Where to Start | What to Bring |
| --- | --- | --- |
| General check-up | barangay health center or City Health Office | valid ID, proof of residence, old prescriptions or lab results |
| Basic medicines | health center or City Health Office pharmacy/dispensing point | prescription or referral, health record |
| Routine vaccines | barangay health center | vaccination card, child's health record, parent/guardian ID |
| Anti-rabies or animal bite care | nearest animal bite treatment facility or hospital | ID, details of bite incident, vaccination history |
| Medical certificate or referral | health center or City Health Office | ID and clinical records, if available |

## Steps

1. Go to your barangay health center during clinic hours or ask your barangay for the correct schedule.
2. Bring your ID, proof of residency, and health records.
3. Register at the health center desk and wait for assessment.
4. Follow the doctor's or health worker's instructions for medicines, laboratory tests, referrals, or follow-up.

## Emergency Reminder

For emergencies, call 911 or go directly to the nearest emergency facility. You may also contact San Pablo CDRRMO at 0998 540 7171.
