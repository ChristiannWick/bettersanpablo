# Get Free Check-ups, Basic Medicines, and Vaccines — San Pablo City

A complete guide to accessing free primary healthcare services through San Pablo City's barangay health centers and the City Health Office.

## What

San Pablo City residents have access to **free or subsidized primary healthcare services** through barangay health stations and the **City Health Office (CHO)**. Services include:

- General medical consultations
- Basic essential medicines (subject to availability)
- Routine vaccinations and immunizations
- Maternal and child health services
- TB and dengue program support
- Anti-rabies post-exposure care
- Health certifications and referrals

## Who can avail

Open to all **San Pablo City residents** with proof of residency:

- **Children** (especially under 5 years old) for the Expanded Program on Immunization
- **Pregnant women** for prenatal and maternal care
- **Senior citizens (60+)** under RA 9994
- **PWDs** under RA 10754
- **Solo parents** under RA 11861
- **General residents** for primary consultations and basic care
- **PhilHealth Konsulta** members for primary care benefits

## When services are available

| Service | Typical Schedule |
|---|---|
| **General consultations** | Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (varies by barangay) |
| **Routine immunization** | Wednesdays / scheduled days at most barangays |
| **Maternal check-ups** | Tuesdays / Thursdays at most barangays |
| **Senior citizen days** | Designated weekly schedule by barangay |
| **TB DOTS schedule** | Daily during clinic hours |
| **Special outreach missions** | Announced via barangay halls |

> Always confirm the exact schedule with your specific barangay health center as schedules may vary.

## Where to go

| Where | What to expect |
|---|---|
| **Barangay Health Station** (in your barangay) | First point of contact for most services |
| **City Health Office** (San Pablo City Hall complex) | For specialized programs, certifications, supervision |
| **PhilHealth Konsulta provider** (designated facilities) | For PhilHealth members' primary care benefits |
| **Hospital referrals** | When secondary or tertiary care is needed |

San Pablo has **80 barangays**, each with a designated barangay health station. Find the one nearest you.

## Why use these services

- **Free or low-cost** — Most services are free; medicines may have minimal fees
- **Close to home** — Barangay health centers are accessible within walking distance
- **Managed by qualified staff** — RHU midwives, BHWs, and visiting MDs
- **PhilHealth-accredited** — Through PhilHealth Konsulta program
- **Continuity of care** — Records stay in the barangay health system
- **Public health programs** — Dengue, TB, nutrition campaigns

## How to access services

### Step 1 — Locate your barangay health center
Ask your **Punong Barangay** or check with the **Barangay Hall** for your designated health station's location and hours.

### Step 2 — Prepare what to bring
- **Valid government ID** of patient and parent/guardian (for minors)
- **Proof of residency** (utility bill, barangay clearance)
- **Health record card** or **child's vaccination card**
- **Old prescriptions** or laboratory results
- **PhilHealth card / MDR** if a member
- **Senior citizen / PWD / Solo Parent ID** if applicable

### Step 3 — Register and wait for assessment
- Register at the health center desk
- Provide basic information (name, age, complaint)
- Wait for vital signs taking and queue number
- Consult with the assigned RHU MD, midwife, or BHW

### Step 4 — Follow medical advice
Depending on your case, follow:
- Prescription and medicine instructions
- Vaccination schedule (return for next dose)
- Laboratory referrals
- Hospital referrals (with referral slip)
- Follow-up appointment dates

### Step 5 — Keep your health records
Health records, vaccination cards, and prescription notes should stay with you for future visits and referrals.
