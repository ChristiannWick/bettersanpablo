# Request Special Waste Collection for Hazardous Materials and Electronics - San Pablo City

Hazardous and electronic waste should not be mixed with ordinary garbage. These items may need special handling, drop-off drives, or referral to an accredited hauler or recycler.

## Examples of Special Waste

- batteries and power banks
- busted bulbs and fluorescent lamps
- old phones, computers, chargers, and appliances
- paint, thinner, oil, pesticides, and chemicals
- expired medicines and medical sharps
- printer cartridges and small electronic parts

## What to Do

1. Keep the item sealed, labeled, and away from children or pets.
2. Do not pour chemicals into drains, canals, lakes, or soil.
3. Ask your barangay hall if there is an upcoming special collection or clean-up drive.
4. Contact the City Information Office for referral to the proper office or disposal channel.
5. For business-generated hazardous waste, coordinate with DENR-accredited service providers and keep disposal records.

## Information to Provide

- your name and contact number
- address or business location
- type of waste
- estimated quantity or photos
- whether there is leakage, odor, breakage, or immediate risk
