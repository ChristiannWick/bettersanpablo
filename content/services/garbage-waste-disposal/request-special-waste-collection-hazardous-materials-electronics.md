# Request Special Waste Collection (Hazardous Materials, Electronics) — San Pablo City

A guide to disposing of hazardous waste, electronic waste (e-waste), and other special items in San Pablo City.

## What

**Hazardous and electronic waste** must NOT be mixed with ordinary garbage. These items require **special handling, drop-off drives, or referral to accredited haulers or recyclers**. Special waste includes:

- **Batteries and power banks** (lead-acid, lithium, alkaline)
- **Busted bulbs and fluorescent lamps** (mercury content)
- **Old phones, computers, chargers, appliances** (e-waste)
- **Paint, thinner, oil, pesticides, chemicals**
- **Expired medicines and medical sharps**
- **Printer cartridges** and small electronic parts
- **Asbestos** (rare, but very hazardous)
- **Pressurized containers** (aerosol cans)

## Who must dispose of special waste properly

- **Households** — for everyday hazardous items
- **Schools** — for science lab waste, old equipment
- **Clinics and hospitals** — for medical sharps and waste
- **Auto repair shops** — for oil, chemicals, batteries
- **Electronics shops** — for e-waste returns
- **Salons and barbershops** — for chemicals and sharps
- **Construction sites** — for asbestos and chemicals
- **Industrial operations** — for chemical and hazardous waste

## When to dispose

| Situation | Action |
|---|---|
| **Old batteries / bulbs** | Set aside until special collection drive |
| **Broken electronics** | Find authorized e-waste drop-off |
| **Expired medicines** | Don't flush! Return to pharmacy or DENR drive |
| **Paint / chemicals** | Properly seal and contact CENRO |
| **Medical sharps** | Use puncture-proof container; bring to clinic |
| **Spillage / leak** | Emergency — call CDRRMO immediately |

## Where to dispose / drop off

| Type | Where |
|---|---|
| **Batteries / bulbs / electronics** | Special collection drives announced by city |
| **Medical waste (sharps)** | Hospitals, clinics, or DOH-licensed handlers |
| **Auto oil / lubricants** | Auto shops, DENR-accredited haulers |
| **Industrial chemicals** | DENR-accredited treatment, storage, disposal facilities |
| **Expired medicines** | Pharmacies (return programs), DENR drives |
| **Paint** | Air-dry first, then dispose; or seal containers |
| **Pressurized containers** | Empty completely, then dispose carefully |

## Why dispose properly

- **Health protection** — Hazardous materials cause illness if mishandled
- **Environmental protection** — Lakes, soil, and air are protected
- **Legal compliance** — RA 6969 (Toxic and Hazardous Waste Act)
- **Resource recovery** — Some e-waste contains recyclable metals
- **Avoid contamination** — Proper disposal prevents lead, mercury leaks
- **Wildlife protection** — Toxic waste harms aquatic life
- **Future generations** — Proper disposal protects long-term land/water

## Examples of special waste

### 🔋 Batteries and electronics
- Cell phones, smartphones
- Laptops, tablets, computers
- Chargers and cables
- Power banks
- Lead-acid car batteries
- Lithium-ion batteries
- Alkaline batteries
- Small appliances (microwaves, fans, etc.)

### 💡 Lighting
- Fluorescent tubes and CFL bulbs (contain mercury)
- LED bulbs
- Incandescent bulbs (less hazardous but still special)

### 🎨 Chemicals and paints
- Paint cans (water-based and oil-based)
- Thinner, solvents
- Pesticides, insecticides
- Cleaning chemicals (concentrated)
- Pool chemicals
- Used motor oil
- Adhesives and glue

### 💊 Medical
- Expired medications (pills, liquids)
- Used syringes (sharps)
- Medical bandages with body fluids
- Old prescription glasses (donate instead)

### 🖨️ Office / printer waste
- Printer cartridges
- Toner cartridges
- Floppy disks, CDs, DVDs

### 🚗 Auto
- Used motor oil
- Brake fluid, transmission fluid
- Coolant / antifreeze
- Old tires (separate handling)

## How to dispose properly

### Step 1 — Identify the type of special waste
Use the categories above to classify what you have.

### Step 2 — Store safely until disposal
- **Keep sealed and labeled**
- **Away from children and pets**
- **In a cool, dry place**
- **Not near food or water sources**
- **Original container** is best when possible

### Step 3 — DO NOT do these
- ❌ **Don't pour chemicals** into drains, canals, lakes, or soil
- ❌ **Don't burn** any hazardous waste
- ❌ **Don't break** lights or electronics intentionally
- ❌ **Don't throw with regular trash**
- ❌ **Don't leave outside** unsecured

### Step 4 — Find a proper disposal channel

#### For households:
1. **Ask your barangay hall** about upcoming special collection drives
2. **Contact City Information Office** (049) 561-1483 for referral
3. **Wait for announced drop-off events**
4. **Check with electronics stores** — many accept old batteries / phones

#### For businesses:
1. **Coordinate with DENR-accredited service providers**
2. **Keep disposal records** for compliance audits
3. **Implement waste reduction** at source
4. **Train staff** on proper handling
5. **For medical waste:** contact DOH-licensed handlers

### Step 5 — Provide information when disposing
When dropping off or requesting pickup:
- Your **name and contact number**
- **Address or business location**
- **Type of waste** (be specific)
- **Estimated quantity**
- **Photos** if useful
- **Whether there is leakage, odor, breakage, or immediate risk**

### Step 6 — Track if needed
For business/industrial:
- **Keep manifests** of waste handed off
- **Confirm DENR registration** of haulers
- **File records** for COA / DENR audits
- **Renew accreditations** annually

## Information to provide when reporting

- **Your name and contact number**
- **Address or business location**
- **Type of waste** (be specific)
- **Estimated quantity / size**
- **Photos** if useful
- **Risk level** (leaking, dangerous, etc.)
- **Special handling needs** (heavy, fragile, etc.)

## Emergency situations

Call **911** or **CDRRMO 0998 540 7171** if:

- 🔥 **Hazardous waste is on fire**
- 💧 **Major chemical leak or spill**
- ☣️ **Toxic gas detected**
- 💥 **Pressurized container ready to explode**
- 🏥 **Medical waste accident** (sharps injury, body fluid spill)
- 🌊 **Hazardous waste entering waterway**
