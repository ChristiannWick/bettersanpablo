# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup — San Pablo City

A guide to garbage collection schedules, route information, and special pickup requests in San Pablo City.

## What

Garbage collection in San Pablo City is **coordinated by barangays and the city's waste management teams**. Services include:

- **Regular household waste collection** (varies by barangay)
- **Bulk waste pickup** for special items
- **Construction debris coordination**
- **Tree branch / yard waste** removal
- **Holiday and emergency advisories**
- **Citywide clean-up drives**

Schedules can change during **holidays, typhoons, vehicle breakdowns, or special clean-up operations**.

## Who can request pickup

Open to:

- **All San Pablo City households** (regular waste)
- **Businesses** (with proper waste contracts)
- **Schools and institutions**
- **Event organizers** (post-event cleanup)
- **Construction projects** (debris coordination)
- **Property owners** (for vacated property cleanup)

## When collections happen

| Type | Frequency |
|---|---|

| **Holiday adjustments** | Schedules shift around major holidays |
| **Typhoon recovery** | Special collection after disasters |

| **Year-end clean-up drives** | November–December |

## Why follow schedules

- **Avoid scattered garbage** — set out only on collection day
- **Pest control** — uncollected waste attracts rats, flies, mosquitoes
- **Cleaner streets** — proper schedules reduce litter
- **Drainage protection** — improper disposal blocks drains, causes flooding
- **Lake protection** — Seven Lakes are vulnerable to runoff
- **Compliance** — RA 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act)

## How to manage your waste

### Step 1 — Know your schedule
Visit your **Barangay Hall** or call (049) 561-1483 to confirm:
- Collection days
- Collection time
- Route changes during holidays
- Special collection days

### Step 2 — Segregate properly before collection
- **Biodegradable** (food scraps, leaves)
- **Recyclable** (bottles, cans, paper)
- **Residual** (contaminated packaging)
- **Special waste** (batteries, electronics — see below)

### Step 3 — Set out waste at the right time
- **Set out only during allowed time** to avoid scattered garbage
- Use **secure bags or containers**
- **Keep sharp objects wrapped** and labeled
- **Don't mix** batteries, chemicals, medical waste, paint, oil, electronics with regular waste

### Step 4 — Request special pickup if needed
For bulky waste, construction debris, tree branches, or large volume:

1. **Contact your barangay hall first**
2. Provide:
   - Your **name** and **exact address**
   - **Waste type** and **estimated volume**
   - **Photos** if available
   - **Preferred pickup date**
3. Ask if **barangay or city office** can schedule collection
4. Or get **direction to approved disposal options**

### Step 5 — Cooperate with collectors
- **Pay collection fees** (if any) per barangay rules
- **Don't block** the collection truck path
- **Bring waste out promptly** when truck arrives

### Step 6 — Adjust during emergencies
- Follow **CDRRMO and barangay** instructions during typhoons
- **Hold waste indoors** during severe weather
- **Don't put waste near flooded areas**

## Pre-collection checklist

- [ ] **Segregate** by type (biodegradable, recyclable, residual, special)
- [ ] **Drain wet waste** before disposal
- [ ] **Flatten boxes and bottles** to save space
- [ ] **Wrap and label sharp items** and broken glass
- [ ] **Set aside special waste** — don't put with regular garbage
- [ ] **Use secure bags / containers**
- [ ] **Set out at proper time** only
