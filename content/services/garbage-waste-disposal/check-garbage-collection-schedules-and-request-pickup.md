# Check Garbage Collection Schedules and Request Pickup - San Pablo City

Garbage collection schedules are usually coordinated by barangays and city waste management teams. Schedules can change during holidays, typhoons, vehicle breakdowns, or special clean-up operations.

## How to Check Your Schedule

1. Ask your barangay hall for the current collection day and route.
2. Check barangay announcements and official city information channels.
3. For citywide advisories, contact the City Information Office at (049) 561-1483.
4. During emergencies or blocked roads, follow CDRRMO and barangay instructions.

The official city services portal lists **Environmental Services** for waste management, tree planting programs, and environmental protection initiatives. That portal entry is currently marked **Coming Soon**, so barangay schedules and City Information Office advisories remain the practical first checks.

## Before Collection Day

- Separate biodegradable, recyclable, residual, and special waste.
- Put waste out only during the allowed time to avoid scattered garbage.
- Keep sharp objects wrapped and labeled.
- Do not mix batteries, chemicals, medical waste, paint, oil, or electronics with regular household waste.

## Requesting Special Pickup

For bulky waste, construction debris, tree branches, or unusual waste volume:

1. Contact your barangay hall first.
2. Provide your name, exact address, waste type, estimated volume, and photos if available.
3. Ask whether the barangay or city office can schedule collection or direct you to an approved disposal option.
