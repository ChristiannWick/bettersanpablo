# Report Illegal Dumping and Waste Management Violations - San Pablo City

Illegal dumping, open burning, dumping into waterways, and improper disposal of hazardous waste can harm public health, drainage, and San Pablo City's lakes and environment.

## What to Report

- garbage dumped on roadsides, vacant lots, canals, creeks, or lake areas
- open burning of waste
- repeated non-segregation or blocked collection points
- construction debris dumped without approval
- chemical, medical, or hazardous waste disposal
- businesses disposing waste outside authorized channels

## How to Report

1. Take photos or video only if it is safe.
2. Record the date, time, exact location, and landmarks.
3. Note plate numbers, business names, or identifying details if visible.
4. Report first to your barangay hall for immediate local action.
5. Escalate to the City Information Office or relevant city office if the issue is recurring or hazardous.

## Emergency Situations

Call 911 or CDRRMO at 0998 540 7171 if the dumped material is burning, leaking, toxic, blocking a major road, or threatening a waterway.

## Local Environmental Context

Reports near Sampaloc, Palakpakin, Mohicap, Yambo, Pandin, Calibato, or Bunot Lake should include the nearest landmark, photos if safe, and whether waste is entering water or drainage.
