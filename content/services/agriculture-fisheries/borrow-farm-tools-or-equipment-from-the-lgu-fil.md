# Mag-borrow ng Farm Tools o Equipment sa LGU — Lungsod ng San Pablo

Gabay sa pag-borrow o pag-access ng shared farm tools, machinery, at equipment sa San Pablo.

## Ano ito

May available na **farm tools at shared equipment** sa pamamagitan ng:

- **Mga barangay** — local-level shared equipment
- **Farmer associations** — cooperative-owned tools
- **Cooperatives** — member-shared resources
- **City Agriculture Office** — city-managed equipment
- **Provincial agriculture programs** — regional shared equipment
- **National agriculture support projects** — DA-distributed equipment

Karaniwang available na equipment:

- **Hand tractor** at small farm machinery
- **Thresher, mill, dryer**
- **Harvester at combine** (bihira, sa appointment)
- **Pump at irrigation equipment**
- **Hand tools** (sprayer, plow, harrow)
- **Post-harvest equipment**
- **Aquaculture equipment** (lake-specific)

## Sino ang puwedeng mag-borrow

Bukas sa:

- **Mga rehistradong magsasaka** sa RSBSA / mangingisda sa FishR
- **Mga miyembro ng farmer association**
- **Cooperative members**
- **Mga indigent na magsasaka** na may barangay endorsement
- **MSMEs sa agriculture**
- **Mga lake-area aquaculture operator**
- **Senior citizen farmer** (priority para sa ilang programa)

## Kailan mag-borrow

| Aktibidad | Pinakamabuting Panahon |
|---|---|
| **Land preparation (tractor)** | Pre-planting season |
| **Threshing / drying** | Post-harvest |
| **Irrigation pumping** | Dry season |
| **Special programs** | Per DA / city schedule |

> Maaaring kailangan ang reservation nang maaga sa peak farming periods.

## Saan magtanong

| Source | Available |
|---|---|
| **Barangay Hall** | Barangay-level shared tools |
| **City Agriculture Office** | City-managed equipment |
| **Farmer Cooperatives** | Member equipment |
| **DA Region IV-A** | Special programs at equipment |
| **City Information Office** | Citywide announcements |

## Bakit mag-borrow / mag-share ng equipment

- **Affordable access** — Karamihan libre o murang halaga
- **Walang malaking puhunan** — Iwas pagbili ng mahal na equipment
- **Scalable farming** — Gamitin kung kailangan
- **Pagbuo ng komunidad** — Pinapalakas ang cooperatives
- **Government support** — Nag-iinvest ang DA sa community equipment
- **Sustainable** — Binababa ang overall machinery footprint

## Ano ang itanong

- **Kung available ang equipment** sa inyong barangay
- **Iskedyul ng pag-borrow** at reservation process
- **Operator requirements** at safety rules
- **Gasolina, transport, deposito, o maintenance** responsibilidad
- **Pinsala, late return, o cancellation** rules
- **Kinakailangang dokumento**
- **Bayad** (kung mayroon)

## Paano mag-borrow

### Hakbang 1 — Tukuyin ang kailangan
- **Hand tractor** — para sa land preparation
- **Thresher** — para sa palay / mais
- **Pump** — para sa irrigation
- **Sprayer** — para sa pest / fertilizer
- **Iba pa** na specific equipment

### Hakbang 2 — Magtanong sa tamang opisina
- **Una** — **Barangay Hall**
- **Pangalawa** — inyong **cooperative** (kung miyembro)
- **Pangatlo** — **City Agriculture Office**
- **Pang-apat** — **DA / provincial** offices

### Hakbang 3 — I-confirm ang availability at rules
Itanong tungkol sa:
- **Available dates**
- **Gaano katagal** maaaring gamitin
- **Operator requirements** (may certified operator ang ilan)
- **Fuel responsibility** (madalas sa borrower)
- **Transport** papunta/mula sa farm
- **Deposit** (kung mayroon)
- **Damage rules**

### Hakbang 4 — I-submit ang dokumento
| Dokumento | Layunin |
|---|---|
| **Valid ID** | Identity verification |
| **Proof of residency** | Local resident verification |
| **Farmer registration** (RSBSA) | DA registration |
| **Barangay endorsement** | Lokal na validation |
| **Farm location details** | Service area |
| **Cooperative endorsement** | Kung applicable |

### Hakbang 5 — Mag-reserve
- **Mag-reserve nang maaga** — mabilis mapuno sa peak periods
- **I-confirm ang araw at oras** sa written form kung kaya
- **Mag-iskedyul ng transport** papunta sa farm
- **May operator** kung hindi sila ang nagpo-provide
- **Maghanda ng gasolina** kung kailangan

### Hakbang 6 — Gamitin ang equipment
- **Sundin ang safety rules**
- **Igalang ang iskedyul**
- **Huwag ipahiram sa iba** (karaniwang prohibited)
- **Idokumento** ang paggamit at anumang issue
- **Magdagdag ng gasolina** kung kailangan

### Hakbang 7 — Ibalik on time
- **Ibalik na malinis** — hugasan ang putik at debris
- **I-report agad ang anumang pinsala**
- **I-settle ang fees o replacement costs**
- **Pumirma ng return form**

## Karaniwang requirements

- **Valid government ID**
- **Proof of residency**
- **Farmer registration** (RSBSA)
- **Barangay endorsement**
- **Farm location** at **petsa**
- **Association o cooperative endorsement** (kung kailangan)
- **Authorized operator info** (para sa ilang equipment)

## Mga contact ng opisina

- **City Hall main line:** (049) 562-0111
- **City Agriculture Office** — sa City Hall main line
- **City Information Office:** (049) 561-1483

## Mahahalagang paalala

- **Karamihan libre o murang halaga** ang pag-borrow
- **Mag-reserve maaga** — mabilis mapuno
- **Operator** ay maaaring kailangan para sa safety-critical equipment
- **Fuel costs** ay karaniwang responsibility ng borrower
- **Damage costs** ay maaaring sa borrower
- **Late returns** ay nakakaapekto sa iba — ibalik on time
- **Cooperative members** ay madalas may priority
