# Ask for Free Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, or Fingerlings — San Pablo City

A guide for San Pablo City farmers and fisherfolk to access free or subsidized agricultural inputs through the city, DA, BFAR, and partners.

## What

San Pablo has **rural barangays, agricultural areas, and lake communities**. Free or subsidized **farm and fisheries support** is offered through:

- **City Agriculture Office**
- **Department of Agriculture (DA)** Region IV-A
- **Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR)**
- **Provincial Agriculture Office** (Laguna)
- **Cooperatives and farmer associations**
- **Partner organizations** (NGOs, foundations)

Common assistance includes:

- **Seeds and seedlings** (rice, vegetables, coconut, fruit trees)
- **Fertilizers** (organic and inorganic)
- **Fingerlings** for fishpond stocking (tilapia, bangus, hito)
- **Coconut seedlings** for replanting
- **Vegetable starter kits**

## Who can apply

Open to:

- **Filipino farmers** registered with DA-RSBSA
- **Fisherfolk** registered with BFAR (FishR system)
- **Cooperatives and farmer associations**
- **Lake-area residents** for aquaculture livelihoods
- **MSMEs** in agriculture
- **4Ps beneficiaries** (priority for some programs)
- **Indigent farmers** for need-based programs
- **Senior citizen farmers** (priority)

## Why apply

- **Reduces farming costs** — free or subsidized inputs
- **Improves yield** — quality seeds and proper fertilizers
- **Supports food security** — local production
- **Recovery support** — post-typhoon assistance
- **Lake livelihood** — fingerlings for aquaculture
- **Climate adaptation** — drought-resistant varieties
- **Community engagement** — connect with cooperatives

## What to ask

- **Seed or seedling distribution schedules**
- **Fertilizer support programs**
- **Fingerling distribution or lake-related livelihood programs**
- **Farm input validation requirements**
- **Cooperative or farmer/fisherfolk registration**
- **Post-disaster recovery aid**
- **Special category programs** (women in agriculture, youth farmers)

## How to apply

### Step 1 — Register as a farmer or fisherfolk
- **DA RSBSA** (Registry System for Basic Sectors in Agriculture) — for farmers
- **BFAR FishR system** — for fisherfolk
- **City Agriculture Office** — for local registration
- **Cooperative membership** — for cooperative-based programs

### Step 2 — Watch for announcements
- **City Information Office** announcements
- **Barangay halls** for local distribution
- **Cooperative meetings**
- **DA Bayanihan** and **BFAR campaigns**
- **PCA coconut programs**
- **Post-typhoon Yolanda-style** rehabilitation drives

### Step 3 — Prepare required documents
| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Valid government ID** | Identity verification |
| **Proof of San Pablo City residency** | Eligibility |
| **Barangay certification** | Local endorsement |
| **Farm or fishery location details** | Service mapping |
| **Farmer / fisherfolk / cooperative ID** | Registration verification |
| **RSBSA / FishR confirmation** | DA / BFAR registration |
| **Post-disaster damage report** | For recovery aid |

### Step 4 — Submit application
- Visit your **Barangay Hall** for endorsement
- Submit application to **City Agriculture Office**
- Or attend **distribution event** with documents

### Step 5 — Receive your inputs
- Pick up at **distribution venue**
- Sign **acknowledgment receipt**
- **Plant or use promptly** for best results
- **Document use** (photos, dates) for follow-up programs

### Step 6 — Follow up and report
- **Maintain records** of inputs received
- **Report yields** if requested
- **Stay registered** for future programs
- **Pay it forward** — share knowledge with neighbors

## For lake area / aquaculture

San Pablo City's seven lakes — **Sampaloc, Pandin, Bunot, Yambo, Palakpakin, Calibato, and Muhikap (Mohicap)** — support active fisheries and aquaculture communities.

Lake-side barangay residents should ask:
- **Fingerling distribution schedules** (tilapia, bangus, hito)
- **Lake-related livelihood programs**
- **Aquaculture training**
- **Lake protection / sustainability** programs

## Important notes

- **All government agricultural inputs are FREE or subsidized**
- **Beware of brokers** asking for processing fees
- **Stay registered** with RSBSA and FishR
- **Maintain records** — useful for future support
- **Cooperatives** offer additional benefits
- **Climate change adaptation** is a priority — ask about resilient varieties

## Local context

San Pablo City's mix of:
- **Coconut groves** (PCA replanting programs)
- **Vegetable farms** (DA support)
- **Lake aquaculture** (BFAR programs)
- **Hillside farming** (Mt. Cristobal foothills)

means support varies by area. Connect with the **City Agriculture Office** to find programs that fit your specific situation.
