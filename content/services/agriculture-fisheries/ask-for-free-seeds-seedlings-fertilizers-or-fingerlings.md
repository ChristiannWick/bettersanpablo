# Ask for Seeds, Seedlings, Fertilizers, or Fingerlings - San Pablo City

San Pablo has rural barangays, agricultural areas, and lake communities. Farm and fisheries support may be announced through city agriculture offices, barangays, DA, BFAR, provincial offices, cooperatives, or partner organizations.

## What to Ask

- seed or seedling distribution schedules
- fertilizer support
- fingerling distribution or lake-related livelihood programs
- farm input validation requirements
- cooperative or farmer/fisherfolk registration

## Common Requirements

- valid ID
- proof of San Pablo City residency
- barangay certification
- farm or fishery location details
- farmer, fisherfolk, cooperative, or association ID if available

## Contacts

- Barangay hall: first point of validation
- City Information Office: (049) 561-1483
- City Hall main: (049) 562-1234

## Local Context

San Pablo City's seven lakes — **Sampaloc, Pandin, Bunot, Inya, Palakpakin, Calibato, and Mabini** — support active fisheries and aquaculture communities. Residents in lakeside barangays should ask their barangay and the City Agriculture Office about fingerling distribution and lake-related livelihood programs.
