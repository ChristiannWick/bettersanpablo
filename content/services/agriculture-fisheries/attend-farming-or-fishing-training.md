# Attend Farming or Fishing Training - San Pablo City

Training may be offered through the city government, barangays, DA, BFAR, DTI, TESDA, cooperatives, schools, or livelihood partners.

## Training Topics to Watch For

- crop production and farm inputs
- coconut, fruit, and vegetable production
- aquaculture and lake livelihood practices
- food processing and packaging
- cooperative formation and MSME basics
- climate-resilient farming and disaster preparedness

## How to Join

1. Watch city, barangay, DA, BFAR, DTI, and TESDA announcements.
2. Confirm the venue, date, target participants, and documents.
3. Register with your barangay, association, or organizer.
4. Bring ID, notebook, and farm or livelihood details.
