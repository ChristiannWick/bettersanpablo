# Attend Farming or Fishing Training — San Pablo City

A guide to free agricultural and fisheries training programs available to San Pablo City residents.

## What

Free or subsidized **farming and fishing training** is offered through:

- **City Government** (City Agriculture Office)
- **Barangays** (local-level training)
- **Department of Agriculture (DA)**
- **Bureau of Fisheries and Aquatic Resources (BFAR)**
- **Department of Trade and Industry (DTI)**
- **TESDA** (TVET — Technical Vocational Education and Training)
- **Cooperatives and farmer associations**
- **Schools and PLSP**
- **Livelihood partners** and NGOs

## Who can join

Open to:

- **Farmers and fisherfolk** of San Pablo City
- **Out-of-school youth** wanting to enter agriculture
- **MSMEs in agriculture** seeking to expand
- **Cooperative members**
- **Lake-area residents** for aquaculture
- **Women in agriculture** (priority programs available)
- **Senior farmers** wanting to update skills
- **Returning OFWs** seeking livelihood

## Why attend training

- **Free or low-cost** — most programs are FREE
- **Improve productivity** — modern techniques
- **TESDA certifications** — recognized nationally / abroad
- **Network with peers** — meet fellow farmers
- **Access support programs** — registered participants get priority
- **Climate adaptation** — learn resilient methods
- **MSME pathways** — training leads to enterprise opportunities
- **Government partnership** — DA / BFAR programs reward trained farmers

## How to join

### Step 1 — Watch for announcements
- **City Information Office** bulletins
- **Barangay Hall** announcements
- **DA, BFAR, DTI, TESDA** announcements
- **Official city Facebook page**
- **Cooperative meetings**

### Step 2 — Confirm details
For each training:
- **Venue** and **date**
- **Target participants** (farmers, fisherfolk, etc.)
- **Documents required**
- **Slots available**
- **Fees** (most are free)
- **Duration** of training

### Step 3 — Register early
- **Slots are limited** — register early
- **Apply through your barangay**, association, or organizer
- **Confirm receipt** of your application

### Step 4 — Prepare for training
Bring:
- **Valid government ID**
- **Notebook and pen**
- **Farm or livelihood details**
- **Specific questions**
- **Photos of issues** you want addressed
- **Open mind for learning**

### Step 5 — Attend all sessions
- **Don't miss sessions** — most certificates require full attendance
- **Take notes**
- **Ask questions**
- **Network with peers**
- **Provide feedback** for improvement

### Step 6 — Apply what you learned
- **Implement new techniques** on your farm
- **Document results** for future reference
- **Share learnings** with neighbors and family
- **Stay connected** with trainers for follow-up

### Step 7 — Pursue certifications
- **TESDA NC II / NC III** for vocational training
- **Cooperative officership** training
- **Specialized BFAR / DA** certifications
- **Use credentials** for grants, jobs, or scholarships
