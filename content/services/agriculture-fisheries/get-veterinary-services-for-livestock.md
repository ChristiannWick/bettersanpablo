# Get Veterinary Services for Livestock - San Pablo City

Livestock owners should coordinate with their barangay and the city or provincial veterinary service for vaccination, animal disease reports, livestock movement, and emergency animal health concerns.

## Services to Ask About

- livestock vaccination schedules
- animal disease reporting
- anti-rabies or animal bite referral for humans
- animal movement or transport requirements
- farm biosecurity advice

## What to Prepare

- owner's valid ID
- barangay and farm location
- animal type and number of animals
- photos or symptoms if disease is suspected
- vaccination or treatment record if available

## Contacts

- Barangay hall: first point of local reporting
- City Information Office: (049) 561-1483
- City Hall main: (049) 562-1234
- Emergency hotline: 911 for immediate public safety threats
