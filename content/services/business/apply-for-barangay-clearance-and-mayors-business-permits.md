# Apply for Barangay Clearance and Mayor's Business Permit - San Pablo City

Use this guide when starting or registering a business in San Pablo City, Laguna. The official services portal lists **Business Permits & Licensing** as an active online service and links it to the San Pablo BPLO portal.

The city forms hub says downloadable forms are still under development and advises residents to visit or contact the respective offices directly for form requests.

## Before You Apply

Prepare the documents that normally apply to your business type:

- valid government ID of the owner or authorized representative
- DTI business name registration for sole proprietors, or SEC/CDA registration for corporations, partnerships, or cooperatives
- barangay business clearance from the barangay where the business is located
- lease contract, tax declaration, or proof of business address
- BIR registration or TIN details
- fire safety, sanitary, zoning, and other clearances if required for the business activity

## Steps

1. Register or confirm your business name with DTI, SEC, or CDA.
2. Secure a barangay business clearance from your barangay hall.
3. Use the BPLO portal or go to the Business Permits and Licensing Office if the transaction requires in-person processing.
4. Submit the unified application form and required attachments.
5. Complete inspection or endorsement steps required by the city.
6. Pay the assessed local fees and taxes through the authorized channel.
7. Claim or download the Mayor's Permit or business permit release document.
