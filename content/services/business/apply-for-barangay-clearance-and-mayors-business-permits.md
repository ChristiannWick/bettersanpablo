# Apply for Barangay Clearance and Mayor's Business Permit — San Pablo City

A complete guide for starting or registering a business in San Pablo City, Laguna using the **5 W's & H** framework.

## What

The **Mayor's Business Permit** is the official authorization issued by the City Government of San Pablo allowing you to legally operate a business within city limits. It is supported by a **Barangay Business Clearance** issued by the barangay where the business is located.

## Who can apply

This service applies to:

- **Sole proprietors** registered with DTI
- **Corporations and partnerships** registered with SEC
- **Cooperatives** registered with CDA
- **Authorized representatives** with proof of authorization (SPA)
- Both **new businesses** and **existing businesses renewing** their permits

## When to apply

| Action | Period |
|---|---|
| **New business** | Before commencing operations |
| **Annual renewal** | January 1 – January 20 each year |
| **Late renewal** | After January 20 (penalties + 25% surcharge + 2% monthly interest may apply) |
| **Quarterly payments** | Optional — Q1 by Jan 20, Q2 by April 20, Q3 by July 20, Q4 by Oct 20 |

## Why you need it

- **Legal compliance** — Operating without a permit violates the Local Government Code (RA 7160)
- **Tax legitimacy** — Required for BIR registration and Official Receipts
- **Credit access** — Banks, suppliers, and lenders require a valid permit
- **Customer trust** — Posted permits build credibility with clients
- **Government contracts** — A current permit is mandatory for public procurement

## How to apply

### Step 1 — Register your business name
Choose the right registry:
- **DTI** — for sole proprietors
- **SEC** — for corporations / partnerships
- **CDA** — for cooperatives

### Step 2 — Get a Barangay Business Clearance
Visit the barangay hall where your business will operate. Bring:
- Valid government ID
- DTI / SEC / CDA certificate
- Proof of business location (lease contract, tax declaration, or owner's consent)
- Pay the barangay clearance fee

### Step 3 — Complete supporting clearances (as required)
- **Fire Safety Inspection Certificate** (BFP)
- **Sanitary Permit** (City Health Office)
- **Zoning / Locational Clearance** (City Planning & Development Office)
- **Occupancy Permit** (City Engineer's Office) — for new structures

### Step 4 — Apply at BPLO
Submit your unified application form online via [bplo.sanpablocity.gov.ph](https://bplo.sanpablocity.gov.ph) or in person at City Hall. Required documents:
- Valid government ID of owner / authorized representative
- DTI / SEC / CDA registration
- Barangay Business Clearance (current year)
- Lease contract or proof of business address
- BIR registration / TIN
- All supporting clearances from Step 3

### Step 5 — Pay assessed fees
Once BPLO assesses your business, pay through:
- City Treasurer's Office (cashier)
- Authorized partner banks / online payment channels (when available)

### Step 6 — Claim your Mayor's Permit
Pick up at BPLO or download from the portal once issued. **Display the permit prominently at your place of business.**

- Renewal fees are based on gross receipts of the previous year
- Display your permit in a visible area within your business premises (legal requirement)
