# Join Trade Fairs, Business Expos, or Tourism Promotions — San Pablo City

A guide for San Pablo City businesses, MSMEs, cooperatives, farmers, and tourism operators on participating in city-led and partner trade events.

## What

This service covers participation in **city-organized and partner-led trade events** including:

- **Trade fairs and seasonal bazaars** organized by the City Government
- **Tourism promotions** tied to San Pablo's Seven Lakes and local festivals
- **DTI Negosyo Center** trainings and MSME assistance programs
- **PESO job fairs** and livelihood programs
- **Coco Festival** market events (January 9–15 annually)
- **Market matching events** for local food, crafts, agriculture, and tourism

## Who can join

Open to San Pablo City–based:

- **Sole proprietors, partnerships, and corporations** with active Mayor's Permits
- **Cooperatives and associations** registered with CDA
- **MSMEs** registered with DTI / Negosyo Center
- **Farmers, fisherfolk, and food producers**
- **Artisans and crafts producers**
- **Tourism operators** (resorts, tour guides, transport, food vendors)
- **Home-based businesses** with proper city registration

## Why participate

- **Direct sales** — Reach high-traffic event audiences
- **Brand exposure** — City-promoted events draw media coverage
- **Market matching** — Connect with buyers, distributors, and suppliers
- **Government incentives** — Reduced fees, subsidized booths, capacity-building programs
- **Tourism leverage** — Tap into Seven Lakes and Coco Festival visitor traffic
- **Networking** — Meet fellow MSMEs, partners, and government agencies

## How to apply

### Step 1 — Prepare your business documents in advance
Keep these ready before any call for exhibitors opens:

- **Current Mayor's Permit** or business permit
- **DTI / SEC / CDA / cooperative registration**
- **BIR registration** or official receipt details
- **Product photos**, price list, and short business profile
- **Food safety / sanitary / FDA / BFAD** documents (for food businesses)
- **Booth staffing plan**, inventory, and payment options (cash, GCash, etc.)
- **Tourism Operator's Permit** (for tourism businesses)

### Step 2 — Watch for the call for exhibitors
Check the City Information Office, official city website, and DTI Negosyo Center regularly. Calls are usually released 4–8 weeks before the event.

### Step 3 — Submit application via official channel
Use only the channel specified in the call (city portal, email, or in-person submission). Include all required documents.

### Step 4 — Attend orientation
Approved exhibitors typically attend a pre-event orientation covering:
- Booth assignments and setup schedules
- Sanitation and safety protocols
- Payment terms and event rules
- Marketing and promotion guidelines

### Step 5 — Pay booth fees (if any)
Pay only through official channels or to the designated event organizer.

### Step 6 — Set up and operate
Follow event rules, keep your booth clean, and coordinate with organizers throughout the event.

## Sample inquiry message

## Important notes

- Submit applications **only through official city, DTI, or event organizer channels** — beware of unauthorized brokers
- Some events prioritize **first-time exhibitors** or specific product categories
- Tourism-related calls often reference the **Seven Lakes** (Sampaloc, Palakpakin, Bunot, Pandin, Yambo, Muhikap, Calibato)
- Keep records of all event participation for marketing portfolios
