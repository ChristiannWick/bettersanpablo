# Join Trade Fairs, Business Expos, or Tourism Promotions - San Pablo City

San Pablo City businesses, MSMEs, cooperatives, farms, and tourism operators can watch for trade fair, livelihood, and tourism promotion opportunities from the city government, DTI Laguna, PESO, and local partner organizations.

## Typical Opportunities

- city-led trade fairs and seasonal bazaars
- tourism promotions tied to San Pablo's Seven Lakes and local festivals
- DTI Negosyo Center trainings and MSME assistance
- job fairs and livelihood programs through PESO
- market matching events for local food, crafts, agriculture, and tourism businesses

## How to Prepare

Keep these ready before a call for exhibitors opens:

- current Mayor's Permit or business permit
- DTI, SEC, CDA, or cooperative registration
- BIR registration or official receipt details, if applicable
- product photos, price list, and short business profile
- food safety, sanitary, FDA, or BFAD-related documents when applicable
- booth staffing plan, inventory, and simple payment options

## Where to Watch for Calls

- City of San Pablo website: https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph
- City services portal: https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph/services
- City Information Office: (049) 561-1483
- City Hall main / forms inquiries: (049) 562-1234
- DTI Laguna and Negosyo Center announcements
- barangay and local chamber announcements

San Pablo's official city profile identifies tourism, enterprise growth, and protection of natural resources as part of its published mission. Tourism-related calls may reference the Seven Lakes, especially Sampaloc, Palakpakin, Mohicap, Yambo, Pandin, Calibato, and Bunot.

## Message Template

> Good day. We are a San Pablo City-based [business type] offering [product/service]. We would like to ask about available trade fair, expo, or tourism promotion slots for [event/date]. Please let us know the requirements, booth fees, deadlines, and orientation schedule.

Submit applications only through official city, DTI, or event organizer channels.
