# Rent Stalls in Public Markets — San Pablo City

A guide to renting a stall, booth, or selling space in San Pablo City public markets and city-authorized market events.

## What

This service covers the **rental of stalls or booths** in San Pablo City–operated public markets, public terminals, and city-authorized commercial spaces. Rentals may be:

- **Permanent** (annual or multi-year contract)
- **Temporary** (event-based, seasonal, or short-term)
- **Special events** (fairs, bazaars, holiday markets)

## Who can apply

This service is open to:

- **Filipino citizens** of legal age (18+)
- **San Pablo City residents** (preferred for some markets)
- **Sole proprietors, partnerships, and corporations** with proper business registration
- **Cooperatives and associations** registered with CDA
- **Farmers, fisherfolk, and producers** for designated agriculture / wet market sections
- **MSMEs** for designated dry goods / merchandise sections

## When stalls become available

| Event | Typical Period |
|---|---|
| **Permanent stall vacancies** | When existing stallholders surrender, retire, or are evicted |
| **Annual market events** | During Coco Festival (January 9–15), city anniversaries, Christmas season |
| **Bidding for prime stalls** | Announced via city website and barangay halls |
| **Temporary booth rentals** | Holiday markets, weekend tiangge, special promotions |

## Why operate in a city market

- **Foot traffic** — Public markets serve high-volume daily customers
- **Lower overhead** — Rents are typically below private commercial spaces
- **Established customer base** — Markets have built-in regulars and loyalty
- **Government oversight** — Standardized fees, sanitation, and safety
- **Community presence** — Visibility within established commercial hubs

## How to apply

### Step 1 — Inquire about availability
Contact the City Market Administrator at City Hall to ask about:
- Available stalls in your preferred market or section
- Stall sizes, location, and rental rates
- Whether the rental is by direct application or competitive bidding

### Step 2 — Get the application form / checklist
The Market Administrator's Office issues the official application form and lists exact requirements.

### Step 3 — Gather required documents
- Valid **government ID**
- Proof of **San Pablo City residency** (if required)
- **Business name registration** (DTI / SEC / CDA), if applicable
- **Barangay clearance**
- Description of **product or merchandise** to be sold
- **Contact number** and emergency contact
- **Photos or sketch** of stall setup (for temporary booths)
- **Sanitary Permit** for food vendors

### Step 4 — Submit the application and wait for assessment
The Market Administrator's Office will:
- Review your documents
- Verify product compatibility with the market section
- Assess applicable fees (rent, deposit, sanitation, garbage)

### Step 5 — Pay through authorized city channels
Pay only at:
- City Treasurer's Office cashier
- Authorized payment partners (if available)
- **Never pay third parties or in-person to anyone outside official channels**

### Step 6 — Sign the lease and start operating
Keep copies of:
- Receipts
- Stall assignment / lease contract
- Mayor's Permit (if separate business permit is required)

## Important notes

- Some stalls require **competitive bidding** — watch for posted notices
- **Sub-leasing** stalls is typically prohibited and may cause cancellation
- **Sanitation and food safety** rules are strict for wet market and food sections
- Display your stall assignment and Mayor's Permit visibly inside your stall
- Comply with city ordinances on operating hours, garbage, and noise
