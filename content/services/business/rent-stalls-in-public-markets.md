# Rent Stalls in Public Markets - San Pablo City

Use this guide if you want to inquire about a stall, booth, or selling space in a San Pablo City public market or city-authorized market event.

## Where to Start

- Ask the market administrator or the city office managing the public market.
- Confirm whether the stall is permanent, temporary, seasonal, or event-based.
- Check whether you also need a barangay clearance, business permit, sanitary permit, or other clearances.

## Common Requirements

- valid government ID
- proof of San Pablo City residency, if required
- business name registration, if applicable
- barangay clearance
- product or merchandise description
- contact number
- photos or sketch of stall setup for temporary booths, if requested

## Steps

1. Ask City Hall or the market administrator if stalls are available.
2. Get the official application form or checklist.
3. Submit complete documents and wait for assessment.
4. Pay only through authorized city payment channels.
5. Keep copies of receipts, permit, contract, and stall assignment.
