# Apply for Socialized Housing or Relocation Projects — San Pablo City

A guide to applying for socialized housing and relocation programs through CSWDO and partner agencies in San Pablo City.

## What

San Pablo City assists qualified low-income residents with **access to socialized housing and relocation programs** coordinated by the city government in partnership with:

- **NHA (National Housing Authority)**
- **DHSUD (Department of Human Settlements and Urban Development)** — formerly HUDCC
- **SHFC (Social Housing Finance Corporation)**
- **CSWDO (City Social Welfare and Development Office)**

Programs include relocation, lot awards, Community Mortgage Program, and housing subsidies.

## Who may qualify

Socialized housing and relocation programs typically target:

- **Informal Settler Families (ISFs)** in danger zones
- **Residents displaced** by infrastructure projects or hazardous area clearing
- **Indigent families** who don't own land and can't afford commercial housing
- **Households along waterways**, flood-prone areas, unstable slopes
- **Tenants of vulnerable structures** (sub-standard housing)
- **4Ps beneficiaries** (priority for some programs)
- **Senior citizens, PWDs, solo parents** (priority access)

## When to apply

| Situation | When to Act |
|---|---|
| **You receive demolition notice** | Immediately — within notice period |
| **Your area is declared danger zone** | Following barangay/CDRRMO advisory |
| **Major infrastructure project** | When announced (typically 6–12 months ahead) |
| **Open application drives** | Watch for CSWDO / NHA announcements |
| **Disaster recovery** | Post-disaster as part of rehabilitation |

## Where to apply

| Office | Role |
|---|---|
| **Barangay Hall** | Initial endorsement, validation |
| **CSWDO** (City Hall) | Coordinates social preparation, beneficiary listing |
| **CPDO** (City Planning Office) | Land use, zoning verification |
| **NHA** | National-level housing programs |
| **DHSUD Region IV-A** | Regional housing coordination |
| **SHFC** | Community Mortgage Program |
| **Mayor's Office** | Special programs and endorsements |

## Why apply

- **Affordable housing** — Below-market rates and amortization terms
- **Safety** — Move from danger zones to safer locations
- **Legal tenure** — From informal to formal settler status
- **Family security** — Stable home for the family
- **Access to services** — Resettlement areas typically have water, electricity, schools
- **Long-term investment** — Builds family asset base
- **Community building** — New neighbors, support network

## Types of housing assistance

| Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Resettlement / Relocation** | Transfer to designated relocation site with housing units |
| **Lot-only award** | Award of lot; beneficiary builds their own house |
| **Community Mortgage Program (CMP)** | Collective ownership through community organization (SHFC) |
| **Cash subsidy / housing vouchers** | Assistance under specific national programs |
| **Rent-to-own** | Through partner developers |
| **In-city relocation** | Stay within San Pablo City when possible |
| **Off-city relocation** | Move to designated provincial sites if needed |

## How to apply

### Step 1 — Verify your eligibility
- Talk to your **Barangay Captain** about whether your household is:
  - In a **danger zone**
  - Tagged for **relocation** under a city or national program
  - Eligible for housing assistance

### Step 2 — Register at the CSWDO
The CSWDO coordinates **social preparation and beneficiary listing** for relocation programs.
- Visit CSWDO at City Hall
- Submit basic information for initial intake

### Step 3 — Submit required documents
| Document | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Valid IDs** | All adult household members |
| **Proof of residency** | Utility bill, barangay certificate |
| **Household profile** | Names, ages, relationships, income |
| **Income declaration** | For income-qualifying programs |
| **Demolition notice** | If applicable |
| **Barangay certification** | Endorsing your application |
| **Photos of current dwelling** | For danger zone documentation |

### Step 4 — Attend orientation
Beneficiaries are briefed on:
- **Relocation site location** and amenities
- **Costs** (if any) — amortization, utilities
- **Terms and conditions** of the program
- **Construction / repair** rules
- **Barangay integration** at new site

### Step 5 — Validation
The city validates your eligibility through:
- **Home visit** by social worker
- **Document verification**
- **Income assessment**
- **Cross-checking with barangay records**
- **Interview** if needed

### Step 6 — Endorsement and award
After validation:
- City endorses to **NHA or partner program**
- Receive **award notice** with details
- **Sign agreements** for amortization, etc.
- Schedule **transfer to relocation site**

### Step 7 — Move and establish residence
- **Pack important documents** safely
- **Coordinate transport** through program
- **Register at new barangay** for services
- **Pay amortization** on time (if applicable)
- **Comply with rules** of the relocation site

## Common requirements

- **Valid government ID** (all household heads)
- **Barangay certificate or certificate of indigency**
- **Proof of San Pablo City residency**
- **Household composition data** (names, ages, relationships)
- **Income statement** (for need-based programs)
- **For danger zone residents:** documentation of current dwelling location
- **Demolition or eviction notice** (if applicable)
- **Photos** of current housing situation

## Important notes

- **All government housing programs are processed FREE** — no fees to apply
- **Beware of fixers** offering guaranteed slots — these are scams
- **Don't pay anyone** claiming to reserve a relocation slot
- **Keep copies** of all notices, claim stubs, submitted documents
- **Attend all required activities** to maintain eligibility
- **Income verification** is rigorous — be honest in declarations
- **Programs change** — verify current options with CSWDO
