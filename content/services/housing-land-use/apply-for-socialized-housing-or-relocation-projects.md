# Apply for Socialized Housing or Relocation Projects - San Pablo City

San Pablo City's services portal lists **Housing & Urban Development** for housing programs, building permits, zoning clearances, and urban development plans. The portal entry is currently marked **Coming Soon**, so residents should verify active housing or relocation programs with City Hall.

## Common Requirements

- valid ID
- proof of San Pablo City residency
- barangay certificate or certificate of indigency, if required
- household profile
- proof of income or employment, if required
- documents related to demolition, danger area, or relocation status, if applicable

## Steps

1. Ask your barangay whether there is an active city, provincial, or national housing intake.
2. Confirm the target beneficiaries and required documents.
3. Submit only through official barangay or city channels.
4. Attend validation, site inspection, interview, or orientation if scheduled.
