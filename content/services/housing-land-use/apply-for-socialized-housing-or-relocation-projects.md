# Apply for Socialized Housing or Relocation Projects — San Pablo City

San Pablo City assists qualified low-income residents with access to socialized housing and relocation programs coordinated by the city government in partnership with the National Housing Authority (NHA) and the Housing and Urban Development Coordinating Council (HUDCC).

## Who May Qualify

Socialized housing and relocation programs typically target:

- **Informal settler families (ISFs)** living in danger zones (near waterways, flood-prone areas, unstable slopes)
- **Residents displaced by demolition** due to infrastructure projects or hazardous area clearing
- **Indigent families** who do not own land and cannot afford commercial housing

## Types of Housing Assistance

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Resettlement / Relocation** | Transfer to a designated relocation site with housing units or lots |
| **Lot-only award** | Award of a lot in a resettlement area where beneficiaries build their own house |
| **Community Mortgage Program (CMP)** | Collective ownership of land through community organization; administered by SHFC |
| **Cash subsidy / housing vouchers** | Assistance for qualified beneficiaries under specific national programs |

## Steps to Apply

1. **Ask your Barangay Captain** whether your household is in a danger zone or tagged for relocation under a city or national program.
2. **Register at the CSWDO** — The City Social Welfare and Development Office coordinates social preparation and beneficiary listing for relocation programs.
3. **Submit required documents:**
   - Valid ID (all adult household members)
   - Proof of residency (utility bill, barangay certificate)
   - Household profile and income declaration
   - For demolition-related relocation: demolition notice or barangay certification
4. **Attend orientation** — Beneficiaries are briefed on the relocation site, costs (if any), and amortization terms.
5. **Validate through the CSWDO** — The city validates eligibility before final endorsement to the NHA or housing program.

## Common Requirements

- Valid government ID (all household heads)
- Barangay certificate or certificate of indigency
- Proof of San Pablo City residency
- Household composition data
- For danger zone residents: documentation of current dwelling location
