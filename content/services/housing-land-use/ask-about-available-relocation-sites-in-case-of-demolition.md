# Ask About Available Relocation Sites in Case of Demolition — San Pablo City

A guide to verifying relocation options if your household receives a demolition notice or is in a danger zone in San Pablo City.

## What

If your household **receives a demolition notice** or is told that your area is unsafe, you have rights and options. This service helps you:

- **Verify the legitimacy** of demolition orders
- **Identify available relocation sites**
- **Coordinate with city and national agencies**
- **Understand your timeline** and entitlements
- **Avoid scams** by self-proclaimed brokers

## Who can ask

Any San Pablo City resident facing:

- **Formal demolition notice** from city or national agency
- **Eviction proceedings** (court-ordered or otherwise)
- **Declared danger zone** advisory (CDRRMO, DENR)
- **Infrastructure project displacement**
- **Post-disaster relocation** needs
- **Voluntary relocation** for safety

## When to act

| Situation | Timeframe |
|---|---|
| **Receive demolition notice** | Act immediately — within notice period (typically 30 days) |
| **Verbal advisory of unsafe area** | Within 1 week — verify formally |
| **Pending demolition (3 days notice)** | URGENT — know your rights |
| **Disaster aftermath** | Within emergency period |
| **Project announcement** | Within 6–12 months pre-displacement |

## Where to verify and seek help

| Office | When to Contact |
|---|---|
| **Barangay Hall** | First — for written referral and validation |
| **CSWDO** (City Hall) | For social preparation, beneficiary listing |
| **CPDO** (Planning Office) | For zoning, land use, infrastructure plans |
| **City Information Office** | For program clarifications |
| **PNP / Court** | For legal disputes, restraining orders |
| **Free legal aid** (PAO) | Public Attorney's Office for legal assistance |
| **NHA** | National Housing Authority — for resettlement |

## Why verify first

- **Avoid scams** — Fake brokers claim relocation slots
- **Know your rights** — UDHA (RA 7279) protects ISFs
- **Timeline awareness** — Know exactly when you must move
- **Compensation eligibility** — Some demolitions include cash assistance
- **Safe relocation** — Verify destination is safe and serviced
- **Family preparation** — Plan transport and adjustment
- **Documented process** — Protects against future disputes

## How to verify and apply

### Step 1 — Receive and review the notice
- **Read the demolition notice** carefully
- Identify the **issuing authority**
- Note the **deadline** clearly
- Save **all related documents** in a folder

### Step 2 — Report to your barangay hall
- Bring the **demolition notice**
- Ask for a **written referral**
- Request **endorsement** to housing programs
- Get **information** about local relocation sites

### Step 3 — Confirm jurisdiction
- Is the relocation **city, provincial, or national** government-led?
- Who is the **lead office** for your case?
- What is the **next step** (validation, orientation)?

### Step 4 — Visit CSWDO at City Hall
Bring all documents. CSWDO will:
- Verify your case
- Check eligibility for programs
- Schedule **validation visit**
- Provide a **document checklist**

### Step 5 — Prepare required documents
| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Demolition notice or written advisory** | Proof of need |
| **Valid IDs** of household members | Identity verification |
| **Proof of residency** | Establish time at location |
| **Barangay certificate** | Endorsement |
| **Household profile** | Family composition |
| **Contact numbers** | For coordination |
| **Senior citizen / PWD / Solo Parent IDs** | Priority status |
| **Medical documents** | If special needs |

### Step 6 — Attend validation and orientation
- **Validation visit** by social worker
- **Cross-check** with barangay records
- **Income assessment** if applicable
- **Orientation** on relocation site, terms, costs

### Step 7 — Receive endorsement and act
- Receive **award notice** with site details
- **Sign agreements** for relocation
- **Schedule move** with city assistance
- **Don't pay third parties** for slots

## Critical safety reminders

### ⚠️ DO NOT
- ❌ Pay anyone claiming to reserve a relocation slot
- ❌ Abandon your home before validation
- ❌ Accept verbal-only promises
- ❌ Submit originals unless required
- ❌ Refuse to validate due to fear
- ❌ Confront demolition team — let authorities handle

### ✅ DO
- ✅ Get everything in writing
- ✅ Keep copies of all notices and stubs
- ✅ Verify identities of all officials
- ✅ Coordinate with PAO (Public Attorney's Office) if disputed
- ✅ Attend all required activities
- ✅ Report scams to PNP and barangay

## Timeline rights under UDHA (RA 7279)

The **Urban Development and Housing Act (RA 7279)** provides these protections:

- **Adequate notice** of demolition (typically 30 days)
- **Adequate consultation** with affected residents
- **Adequate relocation** (preferably in-city)
- **Just compensation** for property
- **Special protection** for vulnerable groups
- **No demolition without court order** in most cases

## Important notes

- **Document everything** — Photos, receipts, written notices
- **Get legal aid** if disputing — PAO is free for indigents
- **Stay informed** — Don't sign anything you don't understand
- **Know your barangay rights**
- **Special protections** for vulnerable groups (seniors, PWDs, kids)
