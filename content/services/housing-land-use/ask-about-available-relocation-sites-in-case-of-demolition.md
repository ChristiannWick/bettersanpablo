# Ask About Available Relocation Sites in Case of Demolition - San Pablo City

If your household receives a demolition notice or is told that your area is unsafe, verify relocation options through your barangay and the city office handling housing, social welfare, or urban development.

## What to Bring

- demolition notice or written advisory, if available
- valid IDs of household members
- proof of residency
- barangay certificate
- household profile and contact numbers
- senior citizen, PWD, solo parent, or medical documents, if relevant

## Steps

1. Report to your barangay hall and ask for a written referral.
2. Confirm whether the relocation is city, provincial, or national government-led.
3. Ask for the validation schedule and document checklist.
4. Do not pay anyone claiming to reserve a relocation slot.
5. Keep copies of all notices, claim stubs, and submitted documents.
