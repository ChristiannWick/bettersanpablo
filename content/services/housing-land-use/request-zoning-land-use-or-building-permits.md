# Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits — San Pablo City

A complete guide to securing zoning clearances, land-use approvals, building permits, and occupancy permits in San Pablo City.

## What

For **construction, renovation, occupancy, business location clearance, or land-use questions**, coordinate with the appropriate city offices. Permits include:

- **Locational / Zoning Clearance** — verifies land use compliance
- **Building Permit** — authorization to construct or renovate
- **Occupancy Permit** — clears a structure for use after construction
- **Demolition Permit** — for tearing down structures
- **Fence Permit** — for permanent fencing
- **Electrical / Mechanical / Plumbing Permits** — for utility installations
- **Subdivision / Condominium Approval** — for development projects

## Who needs these permits

- **Property owners** building or renovating
- **Contractors and developers**
- **Business establishments** changing locations or constructions
- **Subdivision developers**
- **Schools, hospitals, religious organizations** building new structures
- **Homeowners** building fences, additions, or extensions
- **Industrial operations** with land use needs
- **Anyone changing a property's use** (e.g., residential to commercial)

## When to apply

| Permit | When |
|---|---|
| **Zoning / Locational Clearance** | Before any construction/renovation |
| **Building Permit** | Before breaking ground |
| **Occupancy Permit** | Before occupying / opening a new structure |
| **Demolition Permit** | Before tearing down a structure |
| **Renewal of permits** | Per validity period (typically annual) |

> Plan **at least 30–60 days** of lead time for permits, especially for major projects.

## Why secure permits

- **Legal compliance** — National Building Code (PD 1096) requires permits
- **Safety standards** — Permits ensure structural safety
- **Insurance protection** — Most insurers require permits
- **Property value** — Permitted structures are properly assessed
- **Avoids penalties** — Building without permits = fines + demolition risk
- **Future transactions** — Real estate sales require permit history
- **Disaster resilience** — Code-compliant structures survive better
- **Resale value** — Buyers and banks check permits

## Permit types in detail

### 📋 Zoning / Locational Clearance
Issued by **CPDO** (City Planning and Development Office)
- Verifies compliance with the City Zoning Ordinance
- Required before applying for building / business permits
- Confirms land use matches city plan

### 🏗️ Building Permit
Issued by **OBO** (Office of the Building Official)
- Authorization to construct or renovate
- Required for any new structure or major renovation
- Includes electrical, mechanical, plumbing reviews
- Validity: **1 year**, extendable

### 🏢 Occupancy Permit
Issued by **OBO** after construction
- Required before occupying / using a new structure
- Confirms construction matches approved plans
- Required for business permit issuance

### 🔨 Demolition Permit
Issued by **OBO**
- Required before tearing down a structure
- Includes safety, debris management plan
- Coordinates with neighboring properties

### 🏘️ Special Use Permits
- **Subdivision approval** — CPDO + DHSUD
- **Industrial use** — CPDO + DENR
- **Heritage site modifications** — National Historical Commission

## How to apply

### Step 1 — Determine which permits you need
Use this guide:
- **New construction** → Zoning + Building + Occupancy
- **Renovation / addition** → Zoning + Building Permit
- **Just fencing** → Fence Permit
- **Demolition** → Demolition Permit
- **Business location change** → Zoning + Locational Clearance

### Step 2 — Prepare required documents
| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Valid ID** | Identity verification |
| **Lot title** | Proof of ownership |
| **Tax declaration** | Updated tax record |
| **Lease contract** | If renting (with owner's consent) |
| **Authorization documents** | If applying via representative (SPA) |
| **Location plan / vicinity map** | Site identification |
| **Building plans** | Architectural, structural, electrical, mechanical, plumbing |
| **Project description** | Summary of intended works |
| **Barangay clearance** | Local endorsement |
| **Business details** | For business location clearance |

### Step 3 — Get Zoning / Locational Clearance first
- Visit **CPDO** at City Hall
- Submit application with documents
- Wait for **review and inspection**
- Receive **Locational Clearance**

### Step 4 — Apply for Building Permit
- Visit **OBO** at City Hall
- Submit Locational Clearance + Building Permit application
- Submit signed-and-sealed plans (architect / engineer)
- Pay assessed fees at **City Treasurer's Office**
- Wait for permit issuance

### Step 5 — Construct per approved plans
- Begin construction
- Follow **approved plans strictly**
- **Keep permits** on-site
- **Allow inspections** by OBO

### Step 6 — Get Occupancy Permit (after construction)
- Apply for Occupancy Permit
- Submit **as-built plans** if there were changes
- Pass **final inspection** by OBO
- Receive Occupancy Permit
- Permit is needed for **business permit / utility connections**

### Step 7 — Maintain permits
- **Renew expired permits** before expiration
- **Update** if you make further changes
- **Keep records** for property transactions

## Common requirements

- **Valid ID**
- **Lot title, tax declaration, lease, or authorization** documents
- **Location plan, sketch, or vicinity map**
- **Building plans** or project description (signed-and-sealed)
- **Barangay clearance** or endorsement
- **Business details** for location clearance
- **Updated real property tax payments**
- **Photos** of existing site

## Important notes

- **Always get permits before construction** — retroactive permits are difficult and costly
- **Use licensed architects / engineers** — required for plan signing
- **Keep permits on-site** during construction
- **Allow OBO inspections** during construction phases
- **Forms hub** is being developed; visit offices directly for now
- **Comply with the Zoning Ordinance** — major land use changes need amendments
- **Pay only at City Treasurer's Office** — never to fixers
