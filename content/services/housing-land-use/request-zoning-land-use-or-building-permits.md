# Request Zoning, Land Use, or Building Permits - San Pablo City

For construction, renovation, occupancy, business location clearance, or land-use questions, coordinate with the appropriate city planning, zoning, engineering, or building office.

## Official Portal Status

The San Pablo forms hub lists **City Planning & Development** and **Building Official** among the offices planned for downloadable forms. The forms portal is currently under development and advises residents to visit or contact the respective offices directly.

## What to Prepare

- valid ID
- lot title, tax declaration, lease, or authorization documents
- location plan, sketch, or vicinity map
- building plans or project description, if applicable
- barangay clearance or endorsement, if required
- business details for location clearance, if applicable
