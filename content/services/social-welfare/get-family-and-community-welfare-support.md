# Get Family and Community Welfare Support — San Pablo City

A guide to family welfare, crisis intervention, and community welfare services through the City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO).

## What

The City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) provides **family and community welfare assistance** to San Pablo City residents in crisis or special circumstances. Services include:

- **Crisis Intervention** (AICS — Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations)
- **Counseling** for couples, families, and individuals
- **Child protection** referrals (abuse, abandonment, neglect)
- **Women's protection** (VAWC cases — RA 9262)
- **Indigency certification** for medical, burial, educational assistance
- **Burial assistance** for indigent families
- **Medical assistance referral** to PCSO, DSWD, hospital social services
- **Coordination with national agencies** (DSWD, PCSO, DepEd, etc.)

## Who can avail

Open to **San Pablo City residents** in need:

- **Indigent families** for burial, medical, educational assistance
- **Women and children** experiencing abuse or violence
- **Senior citizens, PWDs, solo parents** for special programs
- **Displaced or homeless individuals** for temporary shelter
- **Families affected by disasters** for emergency aid
- **Persons in crisis** (death of breadwinner, sudden illness, accidents)

## When to seek help

| Situation | When to Act |
|---|---|
| **Domestic violence / abuse** | Immediately — contact PNP (049) 562-8765 or 911 |
| **Child abuse / neglect** | Immediately — report to barangay + CSWDO |
| **Death in the family + no funds** | Within days of death for burial assistance |
| **Sudden medical emergency, no funds** | Anytime during the emergency |
| **Loss of livelihood / breadwinner** | Within 30 days of incident for AICS |
| **Mental health crisis** | Immediately — call 911 or NCMH 1553 |

## Why seek help early

- **Crisis Intervention is FREE** — no payment required
- **Confidential handling** — your case is protected
- **Connects to national programs** — DSWD, PCSO, AICS funding
- **Legal protection** — VAWC and child protection laws are enforced
- **Prevents escalation** — early help avoids worse outcomes
- **Community support** — barangay-level network responds first

## How to access services

### Step 1 — Start at the barangay
For most non-emergency concerns, your **Barangay Hall** is the first point of contact:
- Get a **Barangay Certificate of Indigency** (for medical/burial/educational assistance)
- Request a **referral letter** to CSWDO or higher offices
- Report safety issues to **Barangay Tanods** or BCPC

### Step 2 — Visit CSWDO at City Hall
Bring required documents:
- **Valid government ID**
- **Proof of residency** (utility bill, barangay clearance)
- **Barangay Certificate of Indigency**
- **Documents related to your case:**
  - Birth, marriage, death certificates
  - Medical records or hospital bills
  - Police blotter (for VAWC / abuse)
  - School records (for educational assistance)
- **Contact details** of the person needing assistance

### Step 3 — Undergo intake assessment
A CSWDO social worker will:
- Conduct an interview
- Verify your case
- Determine eligibility for specific programs
- Coordinate with appropriate agencies

### Step 4 — Receive assistance
Depending on your case:
- **Direct cash assistance** (AICS) for emergencies
- **Goods/in-kind** assistance for relief
- **Counseling sessions** for family / mental health
- **Legal referral** for VAWC, child protection
- **Medical / burial referral** to PCSO, DSWD
- **Shelter** referral for displaced individuals

### Step 5 — Follow-up
For ongoing cases (counseling, child protection, crisis intervention):
- Attend scheduled follow-ups
- Update CSWDO on your situation
- Cooperate with assigned social worker

## Common documents to prepare

- **Valid ID** (government-issued)
- **Proof of residency** (barangay clearance, utility bill)
- **Barangay Certificate** or referral
- **Birth, marriage, death certificates** (PSA)
- **Medical, hospital, police, or school documents** (relevant to case)
- **Photos / written narrative** of the incident or condition

## Important notes

- **All CSWDO services are free** — no fees, no fixers
- **Privacy is protected** under data privacy laws
- **VAWC reporting** is mandatory for healthcare providers and police
- **Child abuse cases** trigger the BCPC and DSWD response system
- **Walk-in consultations** are accepted during office hours
