# Get Family and Community Welfare Support - San Pablo City

Family and community welfare support can include referrals for counseling, crisis assistance, child or women protection, indigency certification, medical or burial assistance, and coordination with national agencies.

## Start Here

1. Go to your barangay hall for initial referral and certification.
2. Ask which city office handles the specific concern.
3. Bring IDs, proof of residence, and documents related to the case.
4. For urgent safety issues, contact emergency responders immediately.

## Common Documents

- valid ID
- proof of residency
- barangay certificate or referral
- birth, marriage, death, medical, or police documents depending on the case
- contact details of the person needing assistance
