# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters — San Pablo City

A guide for San Pablo City residents on how to receive relief goods, cash aid, or post-disaster assistance after typhoons, fires, floods, or other emergencies.

## What

When disasters strike, San Pablo City coordinates **relief distribution and cash aid** through the **CDRRMO**, **CSWDO**, **barangay halls**, and partner agencies (DSWD, NDRRMC, PCSO). Assistance includes:

- **Food packs and water** — immediate relief
- **Hygiene kits** — for affected households
- **Cash aid** (AICS — Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations)
- **Temporary shelter** in evacuation centers
- **Medical assistance** for injuries / illness
- **House repair / construction materials** (for typhoon / fire damage)
- **Burial assistance** for casualties
- **Livelihood recovery** assistance

## Who can receive assistance

Open to **affected San Pablo City residents** who are:

- **Evacuated** to designated evacuation centers
- **Whose homes are damaged or destroyed**
- **Who lost livelihood** due to the disaster
- **Indigent or low-income households**
- **Senior citizens, PWDs, pregnant women, children, solo parents** (priority groups)
- **Residents of officially declared affected barangays**

## When relief is distributed

| Phase | Timing |
|---|---|
| **Pre-emptive evacuation** | Before disaster strikes (e.g., typhoon landfall) |
| **Immediate response** | First 24–72 hours after disaster |
| **Initial relief distribution** | 24–96 hours after disaster |
| **Validation period** | First week post-disaster |
| **Cash aid release** | 1–4 weeks (depending on validation speed) |
| **Recovery / rehabilitation** | 30+ days post-disaster |

## Where to register and receive aid

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| **Evacuation centers** | Designated barangay schools, gyms, multi-purpose centers |
| **Validation registration** | At the evacuation center or barangay hall |
| **Relief distribution** | Evacuation centers + barangay-level distribution points |
| **Post-disaster cash aid** | CSWDO at City Hall (after validation) |
| **Disaster coordination** | CDRRMO at City Hall |
| **Damage assessment** | Visit by city / barangay assessors |

## Why register early

- **Get on the validated list** — Aid follows the official roster
- **Avoid duplicates** — One registration per household per program
- **Receive priority** — Vulnerable groups get first distribution
- **Access multiple programs** — DSWD, AICS, PCSO can all reference your registration
- **Document your situation** — For house repair / livelihood claims

## How to receive assistance

### Step 1 — Follow evacuation orders
- Listen to barangay loudspeakers, official city announcements, and CDRRMO advisories
- Pre-emptive evacuation order = mandatory move to safety
- Bring **GO BAG** (3-day food, water, ID, medicines, clothes, important documents)

### Step 2 — Register at the evacuation center
Bring (if available — don't risk safety to retrieve):
- **Valid government ID**
- **Barangay ID** or **Cedula**
- List of **all household members**
- Note **special needs** (medications, pregnancy, elderly, PWD)

### Step 3 — Provide household information
At the **validation desk** you'll be asked:
- **Name of household head**
- **Address and barangay**
- **Number of family members** (with names and ages)
- **Contact number**
- **Damage / impact details** (house, livelihood, injuries)
- **IDs** (if available)

### Step 4 — Wait for distribution schedules
- Distributions happen on **announced schedules** — don't crowd the queue
- **Prioritized lines** for seniors, PWDs, pregnant women, families with infants
- **Receipts and validation slips** are issued for each release
- **Avoid duplicate registration** — penalties may apply for fraud

### Step 5 — Apply for additional cash aid
After the initial distribution:
- Visit **CSWDO at City Hall** for AICS application
- Bring barangay validation slip + IDs
- Social worker will conduct interview
- Cash aid is released via **bank transfer, GCash, or cashier**

### Step 6 — Document recovery progress
For house damage / livelihood loss:
- **Photos of damage** (before and after)
- **List of lost items** (with estimated value)
- **Barangay damage assessment** report
- **Hospital bills** (for injuries)

## During the emergency — quick checklist

1. **Stay safe first** — Life > belongings
2. **Follow CDRRMO and barangay instructions**
3. **Register at the evacuation center**
4. **Stay with your family** — children/elderly nearby
5. **Identify special needs** at registration
6. **Wait for official distribution** schedules
7. **Report any safety concerns** to barangay or PNP

## Important notes

- **Relief is FREE** — no payment, no fixers, no fees
- **One household, one registration** — duplicates lead to disqualification
- **Document damage and losses** with photos for follow-up claims
- **Senior / PWD / pregnant** priority is mandated
- **Stay informed** via barangay, CDRRMO, and city Facebook page
- **DSWD AICS** continues to provide cash aid weeks after the disaster
- **Don't return to damaged homes** until cleared by authorities
