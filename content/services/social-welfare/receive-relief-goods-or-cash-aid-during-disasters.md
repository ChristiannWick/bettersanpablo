# Receive Relief Goods or Cash Aid During Disasters - San Pablo City

Relief distribution is usually coordinated by the barangay, CDRRMO, CSWDO, and other city offices after validation of affected households.

## During an Emergency

1. Follow barangay and CDRRMO evacuation instructions.
2. Register at the evacuation center or barangay validation desk.
3. Keep your family members together and report seniors, PWDs, pregnant residents, children, or medical needs.
4. Wait for official distribution schedules and avoid duplicate registration.

## Common Information Requested

- name of household head
- address and barangay
- number of family members
- contact number
- damage or impact details
- IDs, if available
