# Apply for Senior Citizen, Solo Parent, or PWD Assistance - San Pablo City

San Pablo City's services portal lists **Social Welfare Programs** for senior citizen benefits, persons with disability assistance, and social amelioration programs. The portal entry is currently marked **Coming Soon**, so residents should verify current steps with the barangay or City Hall.

## Common Documents

- valid government ID
- proof of San Pablo City residency
- barangay certificate or certificate of indigency, if required
- senior citizen ID, PWD ID, or solo parent ID, if already issued
- medical abstract or disability documents for PWD-related assistance, if requested
- contact number and emergency contact

## Steps

1. Ask your barangay hall or CSWDO focal person which assistance is currently open.
2. Confirm eligibility, requirements, deadline, and payout or release schedule.
3. Submit complete documents only through official channels.
4. Keep the receiving copy, claim stub, or reference number.
5. Attend validation or interview if scheduled.
