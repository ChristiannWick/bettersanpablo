# Join Livelihood or Skills Training Programs — San Pablo City

A guide to livelihood, skills training, and employment programs offered by San Pablo City through CSWDO, PESO, TESDA, and DTI.

## What

San Pablo City partners with national agencies to offer **free or subsidized livelihood, skills training, and employment programs** for residents:

- **Skills Training** — Free TVET courses (TESDA-accredited)
- **Livelihood Starter Kits** — Tools, equipment, capital goods
- **MSME Support** — Business registration help, seminars, trade fairs
- **Employment Referral** — Job matching to local and overseas employers
- **Tourism Livelihood** — Lake-based livelihood programs (raft guiding, eco-tourism)
- **Women's Livelihood** — Skills + enterprise programs for marginalized women
- **4Ps Livelihood Component** — Sustainable livelihood for 4Ps households

## Who can join

Open to **San Pablo City residents** who are:

- **18 years or older**
- **Out-of-school youth** (for TESDA / livelihood programs)
- **Unemployed or underemployed**
- **Indigent families** (for livelihood starter kits)
- **MSMEs** wanting to grow their business
- **Women, PWDs, solo parents, seniors** (priority for special programs)
- **4Ps beneficiaries** (DSWD livelihood component)
- **Tourism stakeholders** (lake livelihood, food vendors, eco-tour operators)

## When programs are offered

| Type | Frequency / Schedule |
|---|---|
| **TESDA short courses** | Continuous enrollment (varies by course) |
| **CSWDO livelihood kits** | Announced quarterly or per program funding cycle |
| **PESO Job Fairs** | Quarterly + special events (Labor Day, Independence Day) |
| **DTI MSME seminars** | Monthly at Negosyo Center |
| **Tourism livelihood training** | Pre-summer (March–April) and pre-Coco Festival (December) |
| **4Ps livelihood orientation** | Per cohort (DSWD-scheduled) |

## Why participate

- **Free or subsidized** — Most programs cost nothing to join
- **TESDA certification** — Recognized nationwide and abroad (NC II, NC III)
- **Direct income generation** — Livelihood kits = immediate income source
- **Connects you to employers** — PESO job-matching saves time
- **Government-backed** — Safer than private "training schools" / scams
- **Community building** — Network with fellow participants
- **Lifts families out of poverty** — Especially for 4Ps beneficiaries

## How to apply

### Step 1 — Watch for program announcements
- City Hall bulletin boards
- Barangay halls
- Official city Facebook page
- TESDA Laguna scheduled training calendar
- Negosyo Center San Pablo announcements

### Step 2 — Choose the right office
- **For skills training (TVET):** TESDA or CSWDO
- **For job placement:** PESO San Pablo
- **For MSME / business:** DTI Negosyo Center
- **For livelihood kits:** CSWDO
- **For tourism livelihood:** City Tourism Office

### Step 3 — Prepare required documents
- **Valid government ID**
- **Proof of San Pablo residency** (utility bill, barangay clearance)
- **Barangay clearance** (current year)
- **Certificate of Indigency** (for kit / starter programs)
- **NSO/PSA birth certificate** (for some TESDA programs)
- **Resume / bio-data** (for PESO job referral)
- **Business profile** (for DTI / MSME track)

### Step 4 — Submit your application
Apply through the office running the specific program. Most accept walk-ins; some require online registration.

### Step 5 — Attend orientation / training
Most livelihood and training programs include:
- **Orientation session** on rules and expectations
- **Training schedule** (full-time or weekend formats)
- **Skills assessment** for TESDA NC certification
- **Distribution event** for livelihood kit recipients

### Step 6 — Apply your skills / receive your kit
After completing training:
- **TESDA certificate** can be used for jobs locally and abroad
- **Livelihood kit recipients** start their micro-enterprise
- **PESO referrals** lead to interviews / job placements
- **MSME graduates** can apply for DTI loans / market access

## Important notes

- **Beware of fixers** — All legitimate programs are FREE through official channels
- **TESDA training** is typically free for qualified residents
- **Bring originals + photocopies** of all documents
- **Tourism livelihood** programs work closely with the City Tourism Office (Seven Lakes operations)
- Check eligibility requirements (income limits, residency) before applying
- Some programs reserve **slots for women, PWDs, seniors, solo parents**

## Tips

- Maintain a **portfolio** of TESDA certificates and training completions
- Keep your **PESO record updated** with new skills
- Attend **DTI seminars** even if not yet running a business
- Use livelihood kits responsibly — many programs follow up
