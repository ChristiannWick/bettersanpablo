# Request Access to Barangay Water Supply Systems - San Pablo City

For water connection, billing, water quality, and service concerns, start with the San Pablo City Water District or your barangay if the area uses a barangay-managed water system.

## San Pablo City Water District

The water district website lists services for new service application online appointment, new service connection requirements, water rates, senior citizen discount requirements, and downloadable forms.

## Steps

1. Confirm whether your property is served by SPCWD or a barangay water system.
2. Prepare proof of identity, proof of address, and ownership or occupancy documents.
3. Check SPCWD's online appointment or new service connection requirements.
4. Ask about inspection, fees, meter installation, and expected activation date.
