# Request Access to Barangay Water Supply Systems — San Pablo City

A guide to applying for water connection, billing, water quality, and service concerns through the San Pablo City Water District (SPCWD) and barangay-managed systems.

## What

For water connection, billing, water quality, and service concerns, the **San Pablo City Water District (SPCWD)** is the primary water utility serving most of the city. Some barangays operate **independent water systems**.

Services include:

- **New water connection** for residences and businesses
- **Reconnection** of disconnected services
- **Water quality concerns**
- **Billing and payments**
- **Repairs and leak reports**
- **Senior citizen discounts**
- **Bulk and industrial supply**

## Who can apply

| Service | Eligibility |
|---|---|
| **New connection** | Property owners, lessees with owner consent |
| **Reconnection** | Account holders with cleared arrears |
| **Senior discount** | Senior citizens 60+ with valid ID |
| **Industrial supply** | Businesses with proper permits |
| **Bulk water hauling** | Construction sites, special events |

## When to apply

| Situation | Timeframe |
|---|---|
| **New construction** | Apply during construction phase |
| **Move-in to existing structure** | Before moving in |
| **Service disruption / leak** | Report immediately |
| **Renewal of senior discount** | Annually |
| **Disputed billing** | Within billing period |

## Why apply

- **Reliable water supply** — Essential for daily living
- **Compliance** — Required for occupancy permits and business operations
- **Quality assurance** — Treated water meets DOH standards
- **Affordable rates** — Subsidized for senior citizens
- **Customer service** — Centralized billing and complaints
- **Emergency support** — During water shortages, partner with SPCWD

## How to apply

### Step 1 — Confirm coverage area
- Check whether your property is served by **SPCWD** or a **barangay water system**
- Most central San Pablo barangays are served by SPCWD
- Outer barangays may have community-based systems

### Step 2 — Prepare required documents
| Document | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Valid government ID** | Identity verification |
| **Proof of residency** | Utility bill, barangay clearance |
| **Proof of ownership / occupancy** | Land title, lease contract, or owner's authorization |
| **Building Permit / Occupancy Permit** | For new structures |
| **Old SPCWD bill** | If transferring service |
| **Senior citizen ID** | For senior discount |

### Step 3 — Apply for new connection (SPCWD)
1. **Visit SPCWD office** or use online appointment system
2. **Submit application** with required documents
3. **Pay application fees** at SPCWD cashier
4. **Schedule site inspection**
5. **Wait for approval and meter installation**

### Step 4 — Site inspection
- SPCWD inspector visits the property
- Assesses connection feasibility
- Determines pipe routing
- Provides cost estimate for installation

### Step 5 — Pay installation fees
- Pay at SPCWD cashier
- Get official receipt
- Confirm meter installation date

### Step 6 — Meter installation
- SPCWD technician installs the water meter
- Connects pipes to your property
- Tests water flow
- Provides initial reading
- Account is activated

### Step 7 — Manage your account
- **Pay bills on time** to avoid disconnection
- **Apply for senior discount** if eligible
- **Report leaks promptly** to avoid wasted water
- **Update contact info** when changing addresses
- **Request reconnection** if service is interrupted

## For service concerns

### Reporting leaks or no water
1. Call **SPCWD:** (049) 562-9955
2. Provide:
   - **Account number** or address
   - **Type of issue** (no water, leak, low pressure)
   - **Duration** of the issue
   - **Contact number** for follow-up

### Billing disputes
1. Visit SPCWD office with bill
2. Request **investigation**
3. Submit **dispute form**
4. Wait for **investigation results**
5. Pay corrected amount

### Water quality concerns
1. Contact SPCWD with description
2. Request **testing**
3. Avoid drinking until cleared
4. Use bottled water or boil water

## Important notes

- **SPCWD is a separate entity** from City Hall
- **Pay only at SPCWD office** or authorized partners
- **Senior discount** requires valid Senior Citizen ID and annual renewal
- **Meter tampering** is a serious offense
- **Conserve water** — especially during dry season
- **Boil water** if quality is uncertain
