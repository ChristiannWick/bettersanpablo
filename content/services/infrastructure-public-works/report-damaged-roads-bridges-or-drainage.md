# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage — San Pablo City

Report damaged roads, blocked drainage, open manholes, unsafe bridges, fallen branches, landslide risks, or road hazards to your barangay and the City Engineering Office. Early reporting helps prevent accidents and allows the city to prioritize repairs.

## What to Report

| Problem | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| **Road damage** | Potholes, collapsed shoulders, road erosion, damaged pavement |
| **Bridge issues** | Cracked or damaged bridge structures, missing railings, weight limits exceeded |
| **Drainage problems** | Clogged canals, overflowing drainage, broken manhole covers |
| **Hazards** | Fallen trees or branches blocking roads, landslide-prone slopes, flooding chokepoints |
| **Street lighting** | Broken streetlights in public roads and barangay paths |

## What to Include in Your Report

- **Exact location** — barangay, street name, and nearest landmark
- **Photos or video** — if safe to take
- **Type of problem** — describe the hazard clearly
- **Impact** — whether emergency vehicle access, schools, or markets are affected
- **Your contact number** — for follow-up

## How to Report

1. **Contact your Barangay Hall first** — barangays coordinate local road and drainage concerns and can escalate urgent issues to the city.
2. **Call the City Engineering Office** via the City Hall main line: (049) 562-1234
3. **Call the City Information Office:** (049) 561-1483
4. **For emergencies** (flooding, collapsed road, unsafe hazard) — call CDRRMO at 0998 540 7171 or 911.

## After Reporting

- Ask for a reference number or confirmation that the report was received.
- Follow up with the same office if the hazard remains unaddressed after a reasonable time.
- For major infrastructure concerns, you may also raise the issue with your Barangay Council during barangay sessions.
