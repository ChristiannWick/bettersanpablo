# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage - San Pablo City

Report damaged roads, blocked drainage, open manholes, unsafe bridges, fallen branches, landslide risks, or road hazards to your barangay and the city government.

## What to Include

- exact location, barangay, and nearest landmark
- photos or video if safe
- type of problem: pothole, collapsed shoulder, clogged canal, broken cover, flooding, erosion, obstruction
- whether vehicles, pedestrians, schools, or emergency access are affected
- your contact number for follow-up

## Contacts
