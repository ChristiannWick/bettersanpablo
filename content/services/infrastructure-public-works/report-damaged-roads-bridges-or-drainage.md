# Report Damaged Roads, Bridges, or Drainage — San Pablo City

A guide to reporting damaged infrastructure, hazards, and repair needs to the City Engineering Office and barangays in San Pablo City.

## What

Report **damaged roads, blocked drainage, open manholes, unsafe bridges, fallen branches, landslide risks, or road hazards** to your barangay and the City Engineering Office. Early reporting helps prevent accidents and allows the city to prioritize repairs.

Reportable issues include:

- **Road damage** — Potholes, collapsed shoulders, road erosion, damaged pavement
- **Bridge issues** — Cracked or damaged structures, missing railings, weight limit violations
- **Drainage problems** — Clogged canals, overflowing drainage, broken manhole covers
- **Hazards** — Fallen trees, branches, landslide-prone slopes, flooding chokepoints
- **Street lighting** — Broken streetlights on public roads and barangay paths
- **Sidewalk damage** — Broken pavements, obstructions
- **Traffic signage** — Missing or damaged signs

## Who can report

- **Any San Pablo City resident** witnessing infrastructure damage
- **Drivers and motorists** noticing road hazards
- **Pedestrians** observing safety risks
- **Tourists** experiencing dangers
- **Workers** reporting safety hazards
- **Concerned citizens** wanting to prevent accidents

## When to report

| Situation | Urgency |
|---|---|
| **Collapsed road / blocked path** | URGENT — call CDRRMO immediately |
| **Major flooding** | Same day — CDRRMO |
| **Open manhole** | Same day — barangay + city engineer |
| **Pothole hazard** | Within 1–3 days |
| **Broken streetlight** | Within 1 week |
| **Fallen tree** | Same day if blocking road |
| **Drainage backup** | During / after flooding |
| **Bridge damage** | URGENT — same day |

## Why report

- **Prevent accidents** — Early action saves lives
- **Save lives** — Major hazards can cause serious injuries
- **Faster repairs** — Reports prioritize maintenance
- **Insurance support** — Documented hazards can support insurance claims
- **Civic duty** — Every report helps maintain the city
- **Accountability** — Officials track and respond to citizen reports
- **Community safety** — Especially for schools, hospitals, markets

## What to include in your report

- **Exact location** — barangay, street name, nearest landmark
- **Photos or video** — if safe to take
- **Type of problem** — describe the hazard clearly
- **Impact** — emergency vehicle access, schools, markets affected?
- **Your contact number** — for follow-up
- **Date / time noticed**
- **Severity / risk level**

## How to report

### Step 1 — Document the problem safely
- Take **photos / video** ONLY if safe
- **Don't risk your safety** to document
- **Keep distance** from collapsed structures, downed wires
- **Note key details** in writing

### Step 2 — Identify the responsible office
- **Barangay roads** → Barangay Hall first
- **City roads / major infrastructure** → City Engineering Office
- **Emergencies** → CDRRMO + 911

### Step 3 — Contact the right office
For non-emergency issues:
1. **Contact your Barangay Hall first** — barangays coordinate local concerns and escalate urgent issues
2. **Call the City Engineering Office** via City Hall main line: (049) 562-0111
3. **Call the City Information Office:** (049) 561-1483

For emergencies (flooding, collapsed road, unsafe hazard):
- **CDRRMO:** 0998 540 7171
- **National Emergency:** 911

### Step 4 — Provide complete information
When making a report, include:
- **Exact location** (barangay, street, landmark)
- **Type of damage**
- **Photos** (if safe to provide)
- **Your contact number**
- **Affected area description**
- **Whether emergency vehicles or schools are affected**

### Step 5 — Get a reference / follow up
- **Ask for reference number**
- **Note who received your report**
- **Date / time of report**
- **Follow up after a reasonable period** if not resolved
- **Escalate** to City Information Office if barangay doesn't act

### Step 6 — For ongoing or recurring issues
- **Document over time** — multiple reports
- **Coordinate with neighbors** — group reports get more attention
- **Raise at Barangay Council** sessions
- **File complaint** with City Information Office or hotline 8888

## After reporting

- **Ask for a reference number** or confirmation
- **Follow up** with the same office if hazard remains unaddressed
- **For major infrastructure concerns**, raise the issue with your **Barangay Council during sessions**
- **Don't ignore unresolved issues** — escalate

## Important notes

- **Document safely** — never risk your safety
- **Don't try to fix** large infrastructure yourself
- **Stay clear** of collapsed structures, downed wires
- **Multiple reports** for same issue help prioritize
- **Anonymous reports** are accepted
- **Persistent reporting** ensures action
