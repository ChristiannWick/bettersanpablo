# Rent Stalls in Public Markets or Use LGU-Managed Cemeteries — San Pablo City

A guide to renting market stalls and using LGU-managed cemeteries in San Pablo City.

## What

San Pablo City manages several **public markets and cemeteries**:

- **Public market stalls** — for businesses, food vendors, dry goods sellers
- **Public cemetery lots** — burial spaces in city-managed cemeteries
- **Niches and vaults** — alternative burial options
- **Memorial services** — coordination for funerals

These are public facilities operated by the **City Market Administrator** (markets) and the **City Engineering Office** (cemeteries), with policies that follow the Local Government Code (RA 7160).

## Who can avail

| Service | Eligibility |
|---|---|
| **Market stall rental** | Filipino citizens 18+, with valid business documents |
| **Cemetery lot purchase** | Family of deceased, individuals planning ahead |
| **Niche / vault rental** | Authorized representatives with valid documents |
| **Memorial services** | Family of deceased with proper documentation |

## When to apply

| Situation | When |
|---|---|
| **Market stall (when available)** | When city announces vacancies or bidding |
| **Annual market stall renewal** | Per city schedule |
| **Cemetery — pre-need** | Anytime |
| **Cemetery — at time of death** | Coordinate with City Engineering Office immediately |
| **Funeral services** | Coordinate with chosen mortuary + city office |

## Where to apply

| Service | Office |
|---|---|
| **Market stalls** | City Market Administrator at City Hall |
| **Cemetery transactions** | City Engineering Office at City Hall |
| **Funeral coordination** | Chosen funeral parlor + city office |
| **Forms hub** | [sanpablocity.gov.ph/forms](https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph/forms) |

## Why use LGU-managed facilities

- **Government-regulated rates** — Standardized fees
- **Verified location** — In approved zones, complies with city plans
- **Public oversight** — Standardized policies and inspections
- **Affordable** — Generally lower cost than private alternatives
- **Community-based** — Familiar local network
- **Reliable services** — Maintained by the city

## How to apply

### 🛒 For Market Stall Inquiries

#### Step 1 — Determine availability
Contact the **City Market Administrator** at City Hall. Ask:
- Available stalls in your preferred section
- Stall sizes and rental rates
- Bidding requirements (if applicable)
- Permit prerequisites

#### Step 2 — Prepare required documents
- **Valid government ID**
- **Barangay certificate or proof of residency**
- **Business permit or DTI registration** (if required)
- **Product or business description**
- **Contact number**
- **Photos / sketch** of intended stall setup

#### Step 3 — Submit application
- Submit to City Market Administrator
- Pay any applicable fees at City Treasurer's Office
- Wait for approval
- Sign lease / contract upon approval

#### Step 4 — Begin operations
- Display Mayor's Permit and stall assignment
- Comply with sanitation, garbage, hours rules
- Pay rent and other fees on schedule

### ⚱️ For Cemetery Inquiries

#### Step 1 — Identify needs
- **Pre-need (planning ahead)** — for future use
- **At time of death** — for immediate burial
- **Lot, niche, or vault** — different options available

#### Step 2 — Prepare required documents
- **Valid ID of requester**
- **Death certificate or burial documents** (if applicable)
- **Proof of relationship or authorization**
- **Lot, niche, or cemetery details** (if already known)
- **Cremation permit** (if cremation is involved)

#### Step 3 — Visit City Engineering Office
- Submit documents
- Choose available lot / niche
- Receive assessment of fees
- Pay at City Treasurer's Office

#### Step 4 — Complete burial / interment
- Coordinate with chosen funeral parlor
- Schedule burial date
- Witness interment
- Receive **certificate of interment**
- Maintain records for future reference

## Important notes

### For markets:
- **Sub-leasing stalls is prohibited**
- **Sanitation and food safety** rules are strict
- **Display permits visibly** in your stall
- **Pay rent on time** to avoid eviction
- **Beware of fixers** offering guaranteed slots

### For cemeteries:
- **Pre-need purchase** is allowed and often recommended
- **Family records** are important for future reference
- **Maintenance fees** may apply for niches and vaults
- **Cemetery rules** include visiting hours and decor restrictions
- **Coordinate with funeral parlor** for smooth proceedings
