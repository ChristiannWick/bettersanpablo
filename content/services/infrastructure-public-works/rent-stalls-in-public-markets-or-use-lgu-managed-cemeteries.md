# Rent Stalls in Public Markets or Use LGU-Managed Cemeteries - San Pablo City

Market stall and cemetery transactions may require in-person coordination with the relevant city office while the official forms portal is still under development.

## What to Prepare for Market Stall Inquiries

- valid ID
- barangay certificate or proof of residency
- business permit or DTI registration, if required
- product or business description
- contact number

## What to Prepare for Cemetery Inquiries

- valid ID of requester
- death certificate or burial documents, when applicable
- proof of relationship or authorization
- lot, niche, or cemetery details if already known
