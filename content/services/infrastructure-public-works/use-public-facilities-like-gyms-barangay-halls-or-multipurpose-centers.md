# Use Public Facilities Like Gyms, Barangay Halls, or Multipurpose Centers - San Pablo City

Public facility use is usually managed by the barangay, school, city office, or venue administrator that controls the site.

## Common Facilities

- barangay halls and covered courts
- multipurpose halls
- public school gyms or grounds, when allowed
- city-managed venues and parks
- lake-area spaces subject to safety and environmental restrictions

## How to Request

1. Identify who manages the facility.
2. Submit a letter with event purpose, date, time, expected attendance, and organizer contact details.
3. Ask about fees, security, clean-up, sound system limits, traffic, parking, and curfew.
4. Secure written approval before advertising the event.
5. Clean the venue and settle any post-event requirements.
