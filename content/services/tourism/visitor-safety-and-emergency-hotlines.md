## Lake Safety Reminders

- **Do not swim alone** in any of the Seven Lakes. Many lake areas have no lifeguards, and the depth and currents can be unpredictable.
- **Check weather conditions** before heading to outdoor lake sites. During typhoon warnings, stay away from lake shores and elevated areas.
- **Use life vests** during bamboo raft tours and boat activities. Ask your boatman or guide before boarding.
- **Do not fish inside fishpens** or in areas marked as restricted without authorization.
- **Keep a safe distance** from the lake edge, especially in areas without railings or barriers.

## Trekking and Nature Safety

For treks near the lakes, Mt. Banahaw, or surrounding hillsides:

- Inform someone of your route and expected return time.
- Bring enough water, food, and a fully charged phone.
- Do not trek in unfamiliar areas without a local guide.
- Do not enter private property without permission.

## Weather Alerts

Stay informed about typhoons, heavy rain, and flooding advisories through:

- **PAGASA (Philippine Atmospheric, Geophysical and Astronomical Services Administration):** https://www.pagasa.dost.gov.ph
- **CDRRMO San Pablo City:** 0998 540 7171
- **Local barangay announcements** in lake-adjacent communities

## Travel Tips

- Carry a printed or downloaded map of the lake routes. Mobile signal may be weak near some of the outer lakes.
- Inform your accommodation or host of your itinerary, especially for remote lake visits.
- Respect local residents and do not enter private properties surrounding the lakes.

## Medical Assistance

For medical emergencies in San Pablo City:

- **San Pablo City Hospital** — Contact through City Hall: (049) 562-1234
- **Nearest barangay health center** — Ask your barangay or accommodation for the address of the nearest health center
