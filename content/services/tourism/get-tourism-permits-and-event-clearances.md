# Get Tourism Permits and Event Clearances — San Pablo City

A complete guide to securing tourism permits, event clearances, and accreditations for tourism activities in San Pablo City.

## What

Businesses and individuals engaged in tourism activities, events at lake sites, and tourism-related enterprises in San Pablo City must secure **appropriate permits and clearances** from the City Tourism Office, BPLO, CENRO, and barangay halls. Permits cover:

- **Tourism Business Accreditation**
- **Mayor's Business Permit**
- **Lake Activity Permits**
- **Event Clearances**
- **Environmental Compliance**
- **Barangay Clearance**

## Who needs these permits

| Operator / Activity | Permits Required |
|---|---|
| **Resorts and guesthouses** | Tourism accreditation + Mayor's Permit + barangay clearance + sanitary permit |
| **Tour guides** | Tourism accreditation + DOT-accredited training |
| **Travel agencies** | Tourism accreditation + DTI/SEC registration |
| **Bamboo raft operators** | Lake Activity Permit + barangay clearance |
| **Boat operators** | Lake Activity Permit + safety inspection |
| **Tourism-related events** | Event Clearance + barangay clearance |
| **Restaurants near lakes** | Mayor's Permit + sanitary + environmental |
| **Tourism-related businesses** | Mayor's Permit + tourism endorsement |

## When to apply

| Permit | Timing |
|---|---|
| **Tourism Business Accreditation** | Before opening or annually |
| **Mayor's Business Permit** | Annually (Jan 1–20 renewal) |
| **Lake Activity Permit** | Annually + per event |
| **Event Clearance** | At least 30 days before event |
| **Environmental Compliance** | Before construction/operation |
| **Barangay Clearance** | Annually + per business |

## Where to apply

| Permit | Where |
|---|---|
| **Tourism Business Accreditation** | City Tourism Office at City Hall |
| **Mayor's Business Permit** | BPLO at City Hall |
| **Lake Activity Permit** | City Tourism Office + CENRO |
| **Event Clearance** | City Mayor's Office / Tourism Office |
| **Environmental Compliance** | CENRO (City Environment & Natural Resources Office) |
| **Barangay Clearance** | Local Barangay Hall |
| **Online (BPLO)** | [bplo.sanpablocity.gov.ph](https://bplo.sanpablocity.gov.ph) |

## Why secure permits

- **Legal compliance** — Operating without permits is a violation
- **Safety standards** — Permits enforce visitor safety
- **Environmental protection** — Lakes are protected ecosystems
- **Insurance coverage** — Most insurers require accredited operators
- **Tourist confidence** — Visitors prefer accredited operators
- **Government support** — Marketing, training, financial assistance
- **Avoid penalties** — Operating without permits = fines + closure

## Permit types in detail

### 🏨 Tourism Business Accreditation
- Required for resorts, guesthouses, tour guides, travel agencies
- Ensures DOT and City Tourism Office service standards
- Eligible for promotional support and training
- Renewed annually

### 📋 Mayor's Business Permit
- Required for ALL commercial tourism establishments
- Applied through BPLO
- Renewed annually (Jan 1–20)
- Tied to local business tax (LBT) payments

### 🛶 Lake Activity Permit
- Required for bamboo raft operators, boat services, lake activities
- Coordinated through City Tourism + CENRO
- Includes safety equipment requirements (life vests)
- Subject to environmental compliance

### 🎉 Event Clearance
- Required for outdoor events at tourism sites
- Submit event proposal **30 days before** event
- Include: description, attendance, waste plan, emergency contacts
- Barangay clearance also required

### 🌳 Environmental Compliance
- Required for resorts/businesses near the lakes
- CENRO conducts site inspections
- Ensures protection of Seven Lakes ecosystem
- May require ECC (Environmental Compliance Certificate) from DENR

### 🏘️ Barangay Clearance
- Required from the barangay where business / event is located
- Confirms barangay endorsement
- Renewed annually for businesses; per-event for activities

## How to apply (general process)

### Step 1 — Identify required permits
Use the table above based on your activity. Some businesses need multiple permits.

### Step 2 — Secure barangay clearance first
- Visit the **Barangay Hall** where your business / event is located
- Pay barangay fees
- Get the official barangay clearance

### Step 3 — Apply at the City Tourism Office (for tourism accreditation)
Bring:
- Filled-out application form
- Barangay clearance
- DTI/SEC registration
- Valid IDs
- Proof of property ownership/lease
- Photos of establishment

### Step 4 — Apply for Mayor's Business Permit at BPLO
- Use [bplo.sanpablocity.gov.ph](https://bplo.sanpablocity.gov.ph) or visit BPLO
- Submit unified application form
- Submit fire safety + sanitary clearances
- Pay assessed fees at City Treasurer's Office

### Step 5 — Coordinate with CENRO (for lake activities)
- Submit Lake Activity Application
- Comply with environmental requirements
- Receive site inspection
- Receive Lake Activity Permit

### Step 6 — Apply for Event Clearance (for events)
- Submit event proposal at least **30 days before** event
- Include all required documentation
- Coordinate with affected barangays
- Receive city clearance

### Step 7 — Display all permits
- Display permits prominently at your establishment
- Carry copies during operations
- Renew before expiration

## Common requirements

For most permits:
- **Filled-out application form** (per permit)
- **Barangay clearance** (current year)
- **Valid government IDs** of owner / authorized representative
- **DTI/SEC/CDA registration**
- **Proof of ownership or lease**
- **Fire safety inspection certificate** (BFP)
- **Sanitation permit** (City Health Office)
- **BIR registration / TIN**
- **Photos** of establishment
- **Tax declarations** if applicable
- **Environmental compliance** for lake-area operations

## Important notes

- **Apply early** — Permits take time to process; events need 30+ days lead time
- **Pay only through official channels** — beware of fixers
- **Display permits prominently** at your establishment
- **Keep originals safe** — carry photocopies for daily operations
- **Renew on time** — late renewals incur penalties
- **Lake operations** require strict environmental compliance
- **Coordinate with affected barangays** for events
