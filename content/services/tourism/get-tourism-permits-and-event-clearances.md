# Get Tourism Permits and Event Clearances in San Pablo City

Businesses and individuals engaged in tourism activities, events at lake sites, and tourism-related enterprises in San Pablo City must coordinate with the City Tourism Office and other relevant city departments for the appropriate permits and clearances.

## Types of Permits and Clearances

| Permit / Clearance                 | Who Needs It                                                         | Where to Apply                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tourism Business Accreditation** | Resorts, guesthouses, tour guides, travel agencies                   | City Tourism Office                                 |
| **Mayor's Business Permit**        | All commercial tourism establishments                                | Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)        |
| **Lake Activity Permit**           | Operators of bamboo raft tours, boat services, lake activities       | City Tourism Office / CENRO                         |
| **Event Clearance**                | Organizers of outdoor events, festivals, gatherings at tourism sites | City Mayor's Office / City Tourism Office           |
| **Environmental Compliance**       | Resorts, campsites, or businesses near the lakes                     | City Environment & Natural Resources Office (CENRO) |
| **Barangay Clearance**             | All businesses and events in specific barangays                      | Barangay Hall of the relevant barangay              |

## General Steps for Tourism Business Permits

1. **Secure a barangay clearance** from the barangay where your business or activity is located.
2. **File an application** at the City Tourism Office for tourism accreditation or at the BPLO for a Mayor's Permit.
3. **Submit required documents** — Typically includes: completed application form, barangay clearance, valid IDs, proof of ownership or lease, fire safety inspection certificate, and sanitation permit.
4. **Pay the applicable fees** at the City Treasurer's Office.
5. **Await inspection and approval** — City inspectors may visit the establishment or site.
6. **Receive your permit or clearance** and display it prominently at your establishment.

## For Lake Activity Operators

Operators running bamboo raft tours, fishing tours, or other lake activities must coordinate with:

- **City Tourism Office** — For tourism activity registration
- **CENRO (City Environment & Natural Resources Office)** — For environmental compliance near protected lake areas
- **Barangay where the lake activity is based** — For barangay-level clearance

## Event Clearances

For events planned at lake sites, parks, or city tourism areas:

- Submit an event proposal to the **City Mayor's Office** or **City Tourism Office** at least 30 days before the event.
- Include: event description, expected attendance, waste management plan, and emergency contact details.
- Obtain barangay clearance from the relevant barangay.
