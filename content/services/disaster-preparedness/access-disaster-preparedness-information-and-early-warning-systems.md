# Access Disaster Preparedness Information and Early Warning Systems - San Pablo City

Use official city, CDRRMO, and barangay advisories for warnings about heavy rain, floods, road closures, lake-area restrictions, fire incidents, earthquakes, and evacuation orders.

## Where to Monitor

- City of San Pablo website: https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph
- City Information Office: (049) 561-1483
- CDRRMO emergency: 0998 540 7171
- CDRRM Office: (049) 800-0405
- barangay announcement boards and official barangay pages

## What to Verify During an Alert

- affected barangays or roads
- evacuation area and transport instructions
- whether classes, markets, or public offices are suspended
- whether electricity or water service is affected
- relief distribution location and schedule

Do not rely on forwarded screenshots alone. Confirm urgent instructions through official city or barangay channels.
