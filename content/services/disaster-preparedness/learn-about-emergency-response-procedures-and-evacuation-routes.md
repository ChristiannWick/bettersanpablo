# Learn About Emergency Response Procedures and Evacuation Routes - San Pablo City

Emergency routes and evacuation sites are usually assigned by barangay and city disaster teams. Confirm your route before typhoon season and after major road or drainage works.

## What to Ask Your Barangay

- nearest evacuation center
- primary and alternate route from your home
- pick-up point for rescue or transport
- warning signals used by the barangay
- contact person for seniors, PWDs, pregnant residents, and children
- where to bring pets or livestock if evacuation is needed

## Household Checklist

- IDs and photocopies
- medicines and maintenance prescriptions
- drinking water and ready-to-eat food
- phone, charger, power bank, flashlight, batteries
- clothes, hygiene kit, face masks, and basic first aid
- emergency contact list
