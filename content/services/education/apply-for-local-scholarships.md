# Apply for Local Scholarships - San Pablo City

Scholarship and educational assistance programs may come from the city government, barangays, schools, CHED, TESDA, DSWD, private foundations, or congressional/provincial offices. Application windows change, so always verify the current call before preparing documents.

## San Pablo Education Channels

| Channel | Details |
| --- | --- |
| City services portal | Lists **Education Programs** for scholarship programs, educational assistance, and public school enrollment information, marked "Coming Soon" on the official portal |
| DepEd San Pablo City | Division website, issuances, Citizen's Charter, document tracking, and hotline |
| Pamantasan ng Lungsod ng San Pablo (PLSP) | Local higher education institution in Brgy. San Jose |

## Common Requirements

- accomplished application form
- proof of San Pablo City residency
- valid student ID or school registration form
- certificate of enrollment or assessment form
- grades or transcript of records
- certificate of indigency, if required
- parent or guardian ID and contact number
- applicant's bank or payout details, if required

## Steps

1. Identify which scholarship or assistance program is currently open.
2. Read the eligibility rules carefully, including income, grade, course, and residency requirements.
3. Prepare scanned and photocopied documents.
4. Submit through the announced office, school, online form, or payout partner.
5. Keep the receiving copy, tracking number, or confirmation message.
6. Watch for interview, validation, orientation, or release schedules.

Never pay a fixer or submit original documents unless the official instructions require it.
