# Learn About Supplementary Activities for Schools in Your Area - San Pablo City

Students, parents, and teachers can find school enrichment activities through their school, DepEd San Pablo City, barangay youth councils, and city announcements.

## Activities to Watch For

- journalism, arts, sports, science, and academic competitions
- leadership programs through schools and SK councils
- reading, library, and learning recovery activities
- disaster preparedness, health, nutrition, and environmental campaigns
- career talks, job fairs, and SPES announcements
- cultural and tourism-related activities connected to San Pablo's heritage and Seven Lakes

## Where to Check

- your school adviser or school Facebook page
- DepEd San Pablo City: https://www.depedsanpablo.com
- DepEd hotline: 049 521 0645
- barangay and SK announcements
- City of San Pablo website: https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph
- City Information Office: (049) 561-1483
- PLSP announcements: https://plsp.edu.ph

## Tips for Parents and Students

- Ask your school for official memoranda before joining an off-campus activity.
- Keep consent forms and emergency contacts updated.
- Check whether uniforms, fees, transportation, or parent attendance are required.
- Save screenshots of official schedules and venue changes.

## Local Context

San Pablo City's official profile highlights cultural heritage, tourism, and natural wonders. School and youth activities may connect to the Seven Lakes, environmental protection, disaster preparedness, public health, and local history.
