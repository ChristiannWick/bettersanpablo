# Learn About Supplementary Activities for Schools in Your Area — San Pablo City

A guide to school enrichment, extracurricular, and supplementary educational activities available to San Pablo City students, parents, and teachers.

## What

San Pablo City schools, DepEd, the SK (Sangguniang Kabataan), and city offices coordinate **supplementary educational activities** that go beyond the classroom curriculum:

- **Academic competitions** — math, science, language, journalism
- **Sports activities** — Palarong Pambansa qualifications, citywide meets
- **Arts and culture** — choir, dance, theater, visual arts
- **Leadership programs** — student government, SK youth leadership
- **Reading and library activities** — book clubs, learning recovery
- **Career talks and job fairs** — for graduating students
- **SPES (Special Program for Employment of Students)** — DOLE summer employment
- **Cultural and tourism activities** — tied to San Pablo's heritage and Seven Lakes
- **Disaster preparedness, health, nutrition, environmental campaigns**

## Who can participate

Open to:

- **Students** in elementary, secondary, vocational, and college levels
- **Parents and guardians** for parent involvement programs
- **Teachers and faculty** for professional development
- **Out-of-school youth** for ALS and TESDA programs
- **SK officers and members** for youth leadership programs
- **Volunteers** for tutoring, reading, and outreach

| Activity | Typical Period |

## Why participate

- **Develops well-rounded students** — Beyond academics
- **Strengthens portfolios** — Helps with scholarships, college applications, jobs
- **Network building** — Meet peers, mentors, recruiters
- **Free or subsidized** — Most public school activities cost little to nothing
- **Earn credits / certificates** — Some activities count for academic credit
- **Civic engagement** — Connect with community and government
- **Career exposure** — Job fairs and SPES introduce real-world work
- **Cultural appreciation** — Connect to San Pablo's heritage and natural wonders

## How to join activities

### Step 1 — Stay informed
- Follow your **school's Facebook page** and adviser announcements
- Check **DepEd San Pablo City** for division memoranda
- Listen to **barangay loudspeaker** announcements
- Bookmark city websites

### Step 2 — Sign up promptly
- Some activities have **limited slots**
- **Submit consent forms** if minor
- **Provide emergency contact** information

### Step 3 — Prepare based on activity type
- **Competitions** — Practice, study materials, supplies
- **Sports** — Uniforms, equipment, signed parental consent
- **Field trips** — Allowance, snacks, IDs
- **SPES** — Resume, transcript, valid ID, application form

### Step 4 — Coordinate with school adviser
- Get **official memorandum** before joining off-campus activities
- Know **transportation arrangements**
- Confirm **fees** (if any) and **inclusions**

### Step 5 — Document your participation
- **Photos** for portfolios
- **Certificates of participation / completion**
- **Updated record** of activities for college applications

## Tips for parents and students

- **Ask for official memos** before joining off-campus activities
- **Update consent forms and emergency contacts** annually
- Know if **uniforms, fees, transportation** are required
- **Save screenshots** of official schedules and venue changes
- **Maintain academic standing** — extracurriculars complement, not replace, studies
- **Balance participation** — avoid overload

## Local context

San Pablo City's official profile highlights:
- **Cultural heritage** — historic churches, ancestral homes
- **Tourism** — Seven Lakes (Sampaloc, Palakpakin, Bunot, Pandin, Yambo, Muhikap, Calibato)
- **Coco Festival** — annual January 9–15 celebration
- **Natural wonders** — Mt. Cristobal, Mt. Banahaw

School and youth activities often connect to these themes — environmental protection, disaster preparedness, heritage promotion, and tourism livelihood.
