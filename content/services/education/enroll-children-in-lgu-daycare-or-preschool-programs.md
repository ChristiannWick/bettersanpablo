# Enroll Children in LGU Daycare or Preschool Programs - San Pablo City

San Pablo City families can ask their barangay, daycare center, or the City Social Welfare and Development Office about daycare and early childhood care schedules. Public kindergarten enrollment is coordinated through DepEd San Pablo City and public schools.

## Who Can Ask

- parents or guardians of preschool-age children
- families looking for barangay daycare or child development services
- parents preparing a child for public kindergarten

## Common Requirements

- child's birth certificate or PSA copy
- parent or guardian valid ID
- proof of residence in San Pablo City or the barangay
- immunization record, if requested
- 1x1 or 2x2 photo, if requested by the center

## Steps

1. Ask your barangay hall where the nearest daycare or child development center is located.
2. Confirm the age requirement, enrollment period, class schedule, and available slots.
3. Prepare the required documents.
4. Submit the application to the daycare worker, barangay focal person, or school.
5. Attend orientation and keep the contact number of the teacher or daycare worker.

## For Public Kindergarten

Contact your nearest public elementary school or DepEd San Pablo City for enrollment instructions and official school memos.
