# Enroll Children in LGU Daycare or Preschool Programs — San Pablo City

A guide to enrolling children in barangay daycare centers, child development centers, and public kindergarten in San Pablo City.

## What

San Pablo City offers **early childhood care and education** through:

- **Barangay Daycare Centers** — for ages 3–4 (typically managed by CSWDO + barangays)
- **Child Development Centers (CDCs)** — for ages 3–5 (DSWD-supervised)
- **Public Kindergarten** — for ages 5+ (DepEd-supervised, mandatory under RA 10157)
- **Supplementary Feeding Program** — nutrition support during preschool

These services prepare children for **Grade 1** by developing social skills, basic literacy, numeracy, and motor skills.

## Who can enroll

Open to **San Pablo City families** with:

- **Daycare ages 3–4** — for nearest barangay daycare
- **Kindergarten age 5** — for public kindergarten (mandatory)
- **Children with special needs** — referred to special programs (DepEd Inclusive Education)
- **Indigent families** — priority for daycare slots
- **4Ps beneficiaries** — daycare attendance is a compliance requirement

## When to enroll

| Program | Enrollment Period |
|---|---|
| **Barangay Daycare** | Pre-school year (May–June) — limited slots |
| **Public Kindergarten** | DepEd Early Registration (January–February) |
| **CDC enrollment** | Per CSWDO / DSWD announcement |
| **Late enrollment** | DepEd accepts late enrollees per LGU policy |

## Where to enroll

| Program | Where |
|---|---|
| **Barangay Daycare Center** | Your barangay hall (ask your Punong Barangay) |
| **Child Development Center** | CSWDO at City Hall (refers to nearest CDC) |
| **Public Kindergarten** | Nearest public elementary school in your barangay |
| **DepEd information** | [depedsanpablo.com](https://www.depedsanpablo.com) — (049) 521-0645 |
| **Document tracking** | [edt.depedsanpablo.com](https://edt.depedsanpablo.com) |

San Pablo has **80 barangays**, each with at least one daycare or CDC.

## Why enroll

- **Free** — Public daycare and kindergarten cost nothing
- **Mandatory by law** — Kindergarten is required under RA 10157
- **School readiness** — Critical for transition to elementary
- **Nutrition support** — Supplementary Feeding Program
- **Socialization** — Develop friendships, communication, sharing
- **Working parents support** — Daycare allows parents to work
- **4Ps compliance** — Required for cash transfer beneficiaries

## How to enroll

### Step 1 — Identify the right program for your child's age
- **Ages 3–4** → Barangay Daycare or CDC
- **Age 5** → Public Kindergarten (school year before Grade 1)
- **Special needs** → Coordinate with DepEd Inclusive Education

### Step 2 — Visit your barangay hall
- Ask the **Punong Barangay** or **Daycare Worker** about:
  - Nearest daycare or CDC location
  - Available slots
  - Class schedules and hours
  - Required documents

### Step 3 — Prepare required documents
- **Child's PSA Birth Certificate** (or local civil registry copy)
- **Parent / guardian valid ID**
- **Proof of residency** (barangay clearance, utility bill)
- **Immunization record** (often requested)
- **1×1 or 2×2 photo** of the child
- **Medical certificate** (some centers require)

### Step 4 — Submit the application
- For daycare → Submit to **daycare worker / barangay focal person**
- For kindergarten → Submit to **public elementary school**
- Sign **enrollment forms** and **parent agreement**

### Step 5 — Attend orientation
Most programs include an orientation covering:
- **Class schedule** (typically half-day for kindergarten)
- **What to bring** (snacks, materials)
- **Parent involvement** (PTA, parenting sessions)
- **Health and safety** rules

### Step 6 — Maintain attendance
- **Regular attendance** is critical for development
- Notify the daycare worker of **absences**
- Keep child's **vaccination card** updated
- Stay engaged with **PTA / parent activities**

## For Public Kindergarten — extra notes

Public kindergarten is **mandatory** under the Kindergarten Education Act (RA 10157). All 5-year-olds must enroll by the school year before Grade 1.

- DepEd usually conducts **Early Registration** in January–February
- Enrollment is at the **nearest public elementary school**
- For school memos and policies, see DepEd Citizen's Charter

## Important notes

- **Public daycare and kindergarten are FREE** — no fees should be charged
- **Bring all original documents** for verification
- **Photocopies stay with the school / center**
- **Attendance is monitored** — daycare often has strict policies
- **Special needs children** can request modified accommodations
