# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU - San Pablo City

Educational support may include school supplies, student assistance, youth employment, public school coordination, and referrals to national education programs. Availability depends on city budgets, school-year schedules, and official announcements.

## Possible Support Channels

- barangay education or youth desks
- City Social Welfare and Development Office referrals
- PESO programs such as SPES or job fairs for students
- DepEd San Pablo City school memoranda
- DSWD, CHED, TESDA, and other national programs
- PLSP announcements for local college admissions, academic programs, and student advisories

## What to Prepare

- student ID or enrollment proof
- proof of residency
- barangay certificate or certificate of indigency, when requested
- parent or guardian ID
- grades, assessment form, or school recommendation
- contact number that stays active

## How to Apply

1. Check the official announcement for the exact program name and deadline.
2. Confirm whether applications are accepted through the school, barangay, city office, or online form.
3. Submit complete documents before the deadline.
4. Keep your claim stub or confirmation.
5. Attend validation, orientation, or payout schedules if selected.
