# Access Educational Support Programs from the LGU — San Pablo City

A guide to educational assistance, school supplies, student programs, and youth employment opportunities for San Pablo City residents.

## What

San Pablo City coordinates **educational support programs** through the LGU, DepEd, partner colleges (PLSP), and national agencies (CHED, TESDA, DSWD). Support may include:

- **School supplies and uniforms** — for indigent students
- **Educational assistance grants** — for tuition, fees, materials
- **Special Program for Employment of Students (SPES)** — DOLE summer / break employment
- **Public school coordination** — DepEd memoranda and policies
- **Career talks, job fairs, internships** — youth employment
- **Referrals** to national programs (DSWD, CHED, TESDA, OWWA)

## Who can apply

Open to **San Pablo City residents** who are:

- **Currently enrolled** students (elementary, secondary, college, vocational)
- **Parents or guardians** of students in financial need
- **Indigent families** with school-age children
- **Out-of-school youth** for TESDA / livelihood programs
- **Students aged 15–30** for SPES (Republic Act 9547)
- **High-performing students** for merit-based programs

## When to apply

| Program | Typical Window |
|---|---|
| **School supplies / uniform aid** | Pre-enrollment (April–May) |
| **Tuition assistance** | Per semester (June, November) |
| **SPES summer program** | March–April registration |
| **TESDA scholarships** | Continuous (varies by course) |
| **DSWD educational aid** | Year-round (per crisis) |
| **PLSP admissions** | February–April per academic year |
| **CHED scholarships** | Per CHED memo schedules |

## Why apply

- **Reduces educational expenses** — Stay in school despite financial constraints
- **Earn while studying** — SPES + TESDA stipends
- **Network access** — Connect to schools, agencies, employers
- **Free or subsidized** — Most programs cost nothing
- **National safety net** — DSWD AICS for emergency cases
- **Career pathways** — Internships and job fairs for graduating students

## How to apply

### Step 1 — Identify the right program
Use this guide:
- **Tuition / fees support** → CSWDO + DSWD
- **School supplies / uniforms** → CSWDO + barangay
- **Summer employment** → PESO San Pablo (SPES)
- **Vocational training** → TESDA
- **College education** → PLSP, CHED, private foundations
- **Public school enrollment** → DepEd San Pablo City

### Step 2 — Watch for announcements
- Barangay halls' bulletin boards
- DepEd San Pablo City website
- City Hall and CSWDO bulletins
- City Facebook page
- School memos

### Step 3 — Prepare documents
- **Student ID or proof of enrollment**
- **Proof of residency** (utility bill, barangay clearance)
- **Barangay Certificate of Indigency** (for need-based programs)
- **Parent/guardian ID**
- **Grades, assessment forms, or school recommendation**
- **Application form** (per program)
- **Photos** (1×1 or 2×2)
- **Active contact number**

### Step 4 — Submit before the deadline
Applications may be accepted through:
- The school directly
- Barangay endorsement
- City office (CSWDO / PESO)
- Online form (some programs)
- DepEd Citizen's Charter portal

### Step 5 — Track your application
- Keep your **claim stub or confirmation receipt**
- Use **DepEd document tracking** for school records
- Follow up with the issuing office

### Step 6 — Attend validation / orientation
If selected:
- Attend **interview / validation**
- Attend **orientation** for program rules
- Receive **assistance** (cash, kit, scholarship release)
- Comply with **continuing requirements** (grades, attendance, reports)

## Important notes

- **Never pay fixers** — All legitimate programs are FREE
- **Don't submit originals** unless required by official instructions
- **Save screenshots** of online applications and confirmations
- **Verify deadlines** — Late applications are usually rejected
- **Continuing requirements** — Maintain grades to keep scholarships
