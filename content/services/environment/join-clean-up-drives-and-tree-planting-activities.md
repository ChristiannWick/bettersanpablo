# Join Clean-up Drives and Tree Planting Activities — San Pablo City

A guide to joining environmental clean-up drives, tree planting, and watershed protection activities in San Pablo City.

## What

San Pablo City's identity as the **City of Seven Lakes** makes clean-up drives, tree planting, and watershed protection especially important. The Seven Lakes — **Sampaloc, Pandin, Bunot, Yambo, Palakpakin, Calibato, and Muhikap (Mohicap)** — are **protected ecological zones** central to the city's environment and livelihood.

Activities include:

- **Lake clean-up drives**
- **Coastal and waterway clean-up**
- **Tree planting (Arbor Day and ongoing)**
- **Barangay clean-up drives**
- **School environmental programs**
- **Seven Lakes conservation activities**

## Who can participate

Open to:

- **All San Pablo City residents** (any age)
- **Students and schools** (especially through Brigada Eskwela)
- **Civic and community organizations**
- **Religious organizations**
- **Workplaces** for CSR activities
- **Visitors and tourists** who want to give back
- **Cooperative members**
- **Senior citizen groups** for accessible activities
- **PWDs** for adapted volunteer roles

## Why participate

- **Protect the Seven Lakes** from pollution and sedimentation
- **Reduce flooding** by clearing drainage and waterways
- **Increase tree cover** and watershed health
- **Fulfill environmental commitments** under RA 9003 (ESWM Act)
- **Build community** with neighbors and fellow volunteers
- **Educate young people** about civic responsibility
- **Improve city image** and tourism appeal
- **Climate action** at local level
- **Wildlife protection** in lake and forest ecosystems

## Types of activities

### 🌊 Lake clean-up drives
- Removing litter, aquatic weeds, waste from lakeshores
- Lake surface debris removal
- **Most impact-heavy** — directly protects the Seven Lakes
- Coordinated by CENRO and barangays

### 🚤 Coastal and waterway clean-up
- Clearing drainage canals
- Rivers and creek cleaning
- Flood-prone area maintenance
- Critical for preventing flooding

### 🌳 Tree planting (Arbor Day)
- Annual national event (June 25)
- City and school programs throughout the year
- Helps watershed and air quality
- Plants native and culturally appropriate species

### 🏘️ Barangay clean-up drives
- Community-organized general cleaning
- Public spaces, sidewalks, parks
- Weekly or monthly schedules
- Often combined with **Bayanihan** spirit

### 🎓 School environmental programs
- DepEd and city-coordinated activities
- Brigada Eskwela (pre-school year)
- Adopt-a-Tree programs
- Eco-club activities

### 🌿 Seven Lakes conservation
- CENRO-led watershed protection
- Buffer zone protection
- Native species restoration
- Monitoring lake health

## How to join

### Step 1 — Watch for announcements
Check these regularly:
- **City Information Office** bulletins
- **Barangay Hall** announcements
- **City Government Facebook page**
- **School memos** (for students/parents)
- **Civic organization** social media
- **CENRO** scheduled drives

### Step 2 — Register through the organizer
- Confirm **meeting point, time, materials** to bring
- Provide your name and contact for tracking
- Confirm if **transportation** is provided
- Note **return time**

### Step 3 — Prepare what to bring
- **Gloves** (work gloves)
- **Drinking water** (1L+)
- **Sun protection** (hat, sunscreen, sunglasses)
- **Reusable sacks** (if requested)
- **Closed-toe shoes** or boots
- **Insect repellent**
- **Towel and change of clothes**
- **Snacks** for energy
- **Personal first aid** (band-aids, pain relievers)
- **Cash** for incidental costs

### Step 4 — Attend the activity
- **Arrive on time**
- **Listen to safety briefing**
- **Follow leader's instructions**
- **Stay with your group**
- **Report any injury** immediately

### Step 5 — Follow safety rules
- **Don't enter unsafe areas** (slopes, deep water)
- **Don't lift heavy objects** alone
- **Stay hydrated** in hot weather
- **Be mindful** of glass, sharp objects, biological hazards
- **Use proper protective gear**

### Step 6 — Segregate waste properly
- **Biodegradable** in green bags
- **Recyclable** in blue bags
- **Residual** in black bags
- **Special waste** kept separate
- **Don't burn** any collected waste

### Step 7 — Document and share
- **Photos** for your records
- **Share** on social media to inspire others
- **Provide feedback** to organizers
- **Stay engaged** for future activities
