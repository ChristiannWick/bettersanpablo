# Join Clean-up Drives and Tree Planting Activities - San Pablo City

San Pablo City's identity as the **City of Seven Lakes** makes clean-up drives, tree planting, and watershed protection especially important. The seven lakes are Sampaloc, Palakpakin, Mohicap, Yambo, Pandin, Calibato, and Bunot.

## How to Join

1. Watch city, barangay, school, and civic group announcements for clean-up and planting schedules.
2. Register through the organizer and confirm the meeting point.
3. Bring gloves, drinking water, sun protection, and reusable sacks if requested.
4. Follow waste segregation instructions after the activity.
5. Do not enter lake or slope areas marked unsafe or temporarily restricted.
