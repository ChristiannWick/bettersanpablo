# Join Clean-up Drives and Tree Planting Activities — San Pablo City

San Pablo City's identity as the **City of Seven Lakes** makes clean-up drives, tree planting, and watershed protection especially important. The seven lakes — **Sampaloc, Pandin, Bunot, Inya, Palakpakin, Calibato, and Mabini** — are protected ecological zones and central to the city's environment and livelihood.

## Why Participate

- Protect the Seven Lakes from pollution and sedimentation
- Reduce flooding by keeping drainage and waterways clear
- Contribute to the city's tree cover and watershed health
- Fulfill environmental commitments under RA 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act)

## Types of Activities

| Activity | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Lake clean-up drives** | Removing litter, aquatic weeds, and waste from lakeshores and lake surfaces |
| **Coastal and waterway clean-up** | Clearing drainage canals, rivers, and flood-prone areas |
| **Tree planting (Arbor Day)** | Annual national tree planting; additional city and school programs |
| **Barangay clean-up drives** | Community-organized general cleaning of public spaces |
| **School environmental programs** | DepEd and city-coordinated activities involving students |
| **Seven Lakes conservation activities** | CENRO-led programs protecting the lake watersheds and buffer zones |

## How to Join

1. **Watch for announcements** from the city government, your barangay hall, schools, civic groups, and the City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO).
2. **Register through the organizer** and confirm the meeting point, time, and materials to bring.
3. **Prepare what to bring:** gloves, drinking water, sun protection, reusable sacks if requested, closed-toe shoes.
4. **Follow waste segregation instructions** after the activity.
5. **Do not enter lake or slope areas** marked unsafe or temporarily restricted.

## Organizations Running Environmental Programs

- **City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)** — lake protection, tree planting coordination
- **Barangay Environmental Officers** — local clean-up and drainage maintenance
- **DepEd San Pablo City** — school-based environmental programs
- **Civic and community organizations** — Rotary, Lions, youth groups, church organizations
