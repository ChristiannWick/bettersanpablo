# Report Illegal Logging, Quarrying, or Fishing - San Pablo City

Report activities that may damage San Pablo City's lake areas, waterways, slopes, roads, farms, and residential communities.

## What to Report

- tree cutting without visible authority
- quarrying, hauling, or excavation in unsafe or unauthorized areas
- fish kills, illegal fishing methods, or waste entering a lake
- dumping, burning, or chemical discharge near the Seven Lakes
- blocked drainage, erosion, or landslide risk after works

## How to Report

1. Take photos or video only if safe.
2. Record the date, time, location, barangay, and nearest landmark.
3. Note vehicle plate numbers, business names, or visible identifiers.
4. Report to the barangay hall for immediate local action.
5. Escalate to the City Information Office or emergency hotlines for urgent hazards.
