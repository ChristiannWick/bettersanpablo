# Freedom of Information (FOI) — San Pablo City

San Pablo City supports residents' right to access public records. This page explains the local FOI process and who to contact.

## Legal basis

- **Executive Order No. 2 (s. 2016)** — Established the FOI Program for the executive branch
- **DILG Memorandum Circular 2010-83** — Full Disclosure Policy
- **Local Government Code (RA 7160)** — Section 5(c) on people's right to information
- **Bill of Rights, Article III Sec. 7** — Constitutional right to public information

## How to file an FOI request in San Pablo City

### Step 1 — Identify what you need
Be specific. Include the document title, year, and originating department if possible. Vague requests slow down processing.

### Step 2 — Submit a written request
You can submit through any of the following:

- **In person** at the Records Office, City Hall (Mabini Extension, San Pablo City, Laguna 4000)
- **By phone:** (049) 562-1234 loc 205 (Records Office) or (049) 561-1483 (City Information Office)
- **At the City Administrator's Office:** (049) 521-0307

### Step 3 — Provide required information
Your request should include:

- Full name and contact details
- Description of the document(s) requested
- Purpose of the request (helpful but not always required)
- Preferred format (printed copy, electronic file, or both)

### Step 4 — Await response
Under the FOI Manual standard, agencies should respond within **15 working days**, extendable for complex requests. The Records Office will confirm receipt and advise on copying fees, if any.

## What you can request

- City ordinances, resolutions, and executive orders
- Approved annual budget and budget execution reports
- COA audit findings on San Pablo City
- Procurement documents (Bid bulletins, Notices of Award)
- Project contracts and accomplishment reports
- Statistics, demographic data, plans (CLUP, CDP, etc.)
- Department organizational charts

## What may be exempt

Per Memorandum from the Office of the Executive Secretary (s. 2016), exempted information includes:

- National security and foreign affairs matters
- Records covered by **RA 10173** (Data Privacy Act)
- Trade secrets and commercial information
- Records subject to attorney-client privilege
- Drafts of orders, resolutions, or decisions still under deliberation
- Information that may endanger personal safety

If a request is denied, the agency must give you a **written explanation**. You may file an administrative appeal.

## Tips for faster responses

- Cite a specific ordinance number, resolution, or year
- Use the official channels — not social media DMs
- Follow up politely if no response is received within 15 working days
- If multiple documents, group them in a single request
