# Freedom of Information (FOI)

San Pablo City supports the public's right to access government information. The FOI process allows residents to request records not proactively published.

## How to submit an FOI request
1. **Identify the document** — Be specific: include title, date, and department if possible.
2. **Contact the City Secretary or FOI desk** — Call City Hall main line (049) 562-1234 or email the City Information Office.
3. **Submit a written request** — Provide your name, contact details, and the documents requested.
4. **Await response** — The city will review and provide available records or explain any legal exemptions.

## What can you request?
- Ordinances, resolutions, executive orders
- Budget documents, audit reports, procurement records
- Project contracts and bid documents
- Public program performance reports

## Exemptions and privacy
Some records may be withheld due to privacy, national security, or other legal exemptions. If a request is denied, you may ask for a written explanation and appeal following local procedures.

## Tips for quicker responses
- Be precise with the document name and timeframe
- Attach supporting information (e.g., report number) if available
- Use official contact channels and follow up politely
