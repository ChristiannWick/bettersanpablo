## Saan galing ang revenue ng San Pablo

- **National Tax Allotment (NTA)** — dating IRA; pinakamalaking pinagmumulan ng kita
- **Local Business Tax (LBT)** — mula sa BPLO permit at renewal collections
- **Real Property Tax (RPT)** — kinokolekta ng City Treasurer's Office (online portal: [realpropertytax.sanpablocity.gov.ph](https://realpropertytax.sanpablocity.gov.ph))
- **Service fees at iba pang income** — Civil registry, market stall rentals, regulatory fees
- **Special allocations** — Bayanihan, BARMM transitions, disaster funding

## Saan nag-iibon ang San Pablo

Mga karaniwang kategorya sa budget execution reports:

- **Personnel Services (PS)** — Sahod at benefits ng city employees
- **Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses (MOOE)** — Day-to-day operations
- **Capital Outlay (CO)** — Mga kalsada, drainage, public buildings, equipment
- **20% Development Fund** — Mandated para sa local infrastructure at livelihood
- **5% Local Disaster Risk Reduction Fund (LDRRF)** — CDRRMO-managed

## Paano ina-audit ang San Pablo

Nagsasagawa ang COA Provincial Office para sa Laguna ng annual audits at naglalabas ng:

1. **Audit Observation Memoranda (AOMs)** — Inilalabas sa fieldwork
2. **Annual Audit Report (AAR)** — Komprehensibong taunang report
3. **Audit Opinion** sa financial statements:
   - **Unqualified (Clean)** — Walang material misstatement
   - **Qualified** — May ilang specific exceptions
   - **Adverse / Disclaimer** — May significant issues

## Paano basahin ang COA audit report

May tatlong bahagi ang typical AAR:

1. **Part I — Audited Financial Statements** na may audit opinion
2. **Part II — Observations at Recommendations** (audit findings)
3. **Part III — Status of Implementation of Prior Year's Recommendations**

Maaaring gamitin ng residente ang Part II para i-check ang compliance issues at Part III para i-track kung umaksyon ang lungsod.
