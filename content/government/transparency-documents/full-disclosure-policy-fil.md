# Full Disclosure Policy — Lungsod ng San Pablo

Sumusunod ang San Pablo sa **Full Disclosure Policy (FDP)** ng Department of the Interior and Local Government (DILG) sa ilalim ng Memorandum Circular 2010-83 at subsequent issuances, na nag-aatas sa LGUs na publicly post ng specific financial documents.

## Legal basis

- **DILG Memorandum Circular 2010-83** — Original FDP issuance (2010)
- **DILG Memorandum Circular 2013-140** — Updated documents
- **DILG Memorandum Circular 2017-87** — FDP Portal compliance
- **Republic Act 11032** — Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act
- **Local Government Code of 1991** (RA 7160) — Transparency provisions

## Mga dokumentong kailangang i-disclose

Sa ilalim ng FDP, kailangang i-publish ng San Pablo:

### Quarterly disclosures
- **Statement of Receipts and Expenditures (SRE)**
- **Quarterly Report of Income**
- **Statement of Cash Flow**
- **Bid results / Notices of Award**

### Annual disclosures
- **Annual Budget** (proposed at approved)
- **Annual Procurement Plan (APP)**
- **Annual Audit Report** (mula sa COA)
- **Trust Fund (PDAF) utilization** (kung applicable)
- **Manpower Complement / Plantilla**
- **Statement of Debt Service**
- **20% Component of IRA Utilization**
- **GAD (Gender and Development) Plan and Budget**
- **SK / Local Youth Development Plan**

## Saan makikita physically

Per DILG MC 2010-83, ang FDP documents ay dapat din i-post sa **hindi bababa sa tatlong (3) conspicuous public places** sa loob ng LGU. Sa San Pablo, ito:

- **City Hall main bulletin board** (Mabini Extension)
- **City Information Office** display
- **Public market** community bulletin board

## Paano i-verify ang FDP compliance

1. I-check ang [DILG FDP Portal](https://fdpp.dilg.gov.ph) at hanapin ang "San Pablo City" o "Laguna"
2. Cross-reference ang posted dates sa required disclosure schedule
3. I-verify ang document completeness sa FDP checklist
4. I-report ang missing documents sa DILG Region IV-A o sa Citizen's Complaint Hotline (8888)

## Pag-request ng dokumentong hindi pa nai-publish

Kung may required FDP document na missing o outdated, maaari kayong:

1. **Mag-request sa FOI** — tingnan ang [Freedom of Information](freedom-of-information)
2. **Tumawag sa City Information Office** para sa paliwanag
3. **Mag-file ng reklamo** sa DILG Provincial Office o sa Hotline 8888
