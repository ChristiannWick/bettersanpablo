# Ordinances and Resolutions

This page provides access to local legislation passed by the Sangguniang Panlungsod of San Pablo City.

## About ordinances and resolutions

**Ordinances** are city laws enacted by the Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Council) that govern various aspects of city life, such as taxation, public order, health, business regulation, and land use.

**Resolutions** are formal decisions made by the City Council that direct specific city government actions, allocate resources, or express the will of the council on important matters.

Both ordinances and resolutions are published to ensure public transparency and awareness of local government decisions.

## How ordinances become city law

1. **Proposal** — A councilor introduces a proposed ordinance or resolution.
2. **Committee Review** — The proposal is assigned to the appropriate standing committee for study and recommendation.
3. **City Council Session** — The proposal is discussed and debated in a regular or special session.
4. **Vote** — If approved by a majority of councilors, the ordinance or resolution proceeds to the Mayor.
5. **Mayoral Action** — The Mayor may sign (approve), veto, or allow the ordinance to lapse without signature.
6. **Veto Override** — If vetoed, the City Council can override the veto with a two-thirds majority vote.
7. **Publication** — Once approved, the ordinance is published and becomes effective.

## Key areas of San Pablo city legislation

Recent ordinances and resolutions cover topics including:

- **Business and Trade Regulation** — Permits, licensing, trade regulations, and market operations
- **Health and Sanitation** — Public health, waste management, environmental protection, and disease prevention
- **Public Order and Safety** — Peace and order, fire safety, disaster risk reduction, and emergency management
- **Infrastructure and Public Works** — Road construction, maintenance, bridge projects, and public utilities
- **Education and Social Services** — Support for schools, scholarships, social welfare programs, and livelihood assistance
- **Finance and Taxation** — City budget, local business taxes (LBT), property taxes, and revenue ordinances
- **Land Use and Development** — Zoning, land classification, subdivision regulations, and urban planning

## Transparency and accountability

San Pablo City is committed to open and transparent government. The publication of ordinances, resolutions, and executive orders supports democratic participation and accountability. Residents are encouraged to:

- Review city legislation to understand rules and regulations affecting their community
- Attend public hearings and consultations on proposed ordinances
- Provide feedback and suggestions to the City Council through official channels
