# Full Disclosure Policy

San Pablo City follows principles of open and transparent governance to ensure public access to information about city programs, budgets, and decisions.

## What is a Full Disclosure Policy?
A Full Disclosure Policy describes the local government's commitment to proactively publish public documents and data to promote accountability, reduce corruption, and improve citizen engagement. Typical disclosures include budgets, audit reports, procurement notices, ordinances, resolutions, and official decisions.

## Proactive disclosure in San Pablo City
San Pablo City publishes many documents through the City Website and its Disclosure Portal. Common proactive disclosures include:

- Annual budgets and budget execution reports
- Financial statements and COA audit findings
- Procurement notices, bids, and awards
- Ordinances, resolutions, and executive orders
- Organizational charts and contact details for departments

## Where to find San Pablo's disclosures
- Official website: https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph
- Disclosure Portal (if available): https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph/disclosure-portal
- City Budget Office / City Accounting Office — contact via City Hall main line: (049) 562-1234

## How to request additional information
If a document is not proactively published, residents may request records through the City Secretary's Office or the Freedom of Information (FOI) process described on this site. See the Freedom of Information page for request steps and contact points.

## Helpful tips
- Check the Disclosure Portal or official site first — many records are published there.
- Prepare a clear request: include document title, year, and any identifying details.
- Use official contact channels: City Secretary's Office or City Information Office.

For more details see the `Freedom of Information` page and the `Financial Records and Audit Reports` document on this site.
