# Full Disclosure Policy — San Pablo City

San Pablo City complies with the Department of the Interior and Local Government's (DILG) **Full Disclosure Policy (FDP)** under Memorandum Circular 2010-83 and subsequent issuances, which require LGUs to publicly post specific financial documents.

## Legal basis

- **DILG Memorandum Circular 2010-83** — Original FDP issuance (2010)
- **DILG Memorandum Circular 2013-140** — Updated documents to be disclosed
- **DILG Memorandum Circular 2017-87** — FDP Portal compliance
- **Republic Act 11032** — Ease of Doing Business and Efficient Government Service Delivery Act
- **Local Government Code of 1991** (RA 7160) — Transparency provisions

## Documents required to be disclosed

Under the FDP, San Pablo City is required to publish:

### Quarterly disclosures
- **Statement of Receipts and Expenditures (SRE)**
- **Quarterly Report of Income**
- **Statement of Cash Flow**
- **Bid results / Notices of Award**

### Annual disclosures
- **Annual Budget** (proposed and approved)
- **Annual Procurement Plan (APP)**
- **Annual Audit Report** (issued by COA)
- **Trust Fund (PDAF) utilization** (if applicable)
- **Manpower Complement / Plantilla**
- **Statement of Debt Service**
- **20% Component of the IRA Utilization**
- **GAD (Gender and Development) Plan and Budget**
- **SK / Local Youth Development Plan**

## Where to find them physically

Per DILG MC 2010-83, FDP documents must also be posted in **at least three (3) conspicuous public places** within the LGU. In San Pablo City, these include:

- **City Hall main bulletin board** (Mabini Extension)
- **City Information Office** display
- **Public market** community bulletin board

## How to verify FDP compliance

1. Check the [DILG FDP Portal](https://fdpp.dilg.gov.ph) and search "San Pablo City" or "Laguna"
2. Cross-reference posted dates with the required disclosure schedule
3. Verify document completeness against the FDP checklist
4. Report missing documents to DILG Region IV-A or via the Citizen's Complaint Hotline (8888)

If a required FDP document is missing or outdated, you can:

1. **Request through FOI** — see the [Freedom of Information](freedom-of-information) page
2. **Contact the City Information Office** for explanation
3. **File a complaint** with the DILG Provincial Office or via Hotline 8888
