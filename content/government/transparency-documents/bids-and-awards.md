# Bids and Awards

This page provides information about public procurement, bidding processes, and contract awards for San Pablo City government projects.

## What is public procurement?

**Public procurement** is the process by which the San Pablo City government purchases goods, services, and conducts public works projects. Procurement follows the **Government Procurement Reform Act (GPRA)** and related laws to ensure:

- **Transparency** — All procurement activities are publicly announced and documented
- **Fairness** — Bidding is open to qualified suppliers and contractors
- **Efficiency** — Resources are used effectively to serve the public
- **Economy** — The government obtains best value for public funds

## Bidding process in San Pablo City

The City Government of San Pablo follows a standardized bidding process:

1. **Procurement Planning** — City departments identify needs and plan procurements
2. **Public Announcement** — Bidding opportunities are advertised publicly
3. **Bidding Period** — Interested suppliers and contractors submit bids
4. **Bid Opening** — Submitted bids are opened publicly and evaluated
5. **Post-Qualification** — Winning bidders are verified for qualifications
6. **Award** — Contract is awarded to the most responsive and qualified bidder
7. **Contract Execution** — The city and contractor sign the procurement contract

## Types of procurement

San Pablo City conducts various types of procurement:

- **Goods Procurement** — Supplies, equipment, and materials for city operations
- **Infrastructure and Public Works** — Roads, bridges, water systems, public buildings
- **Services** — Consulting, maintenance, janitorial, security, and professional services
- **Lease Agreements** — Office space, equipment rentals, and facility usage

## Transparency in procurement

San Pablo City is committed to transparent and competitive procurement practices. Benefits of open bidding include:

- **Lower costs** — Competition among bidders reduces procurement costs
- **Quality** — Multiple suppliers ensures quality standards
- **Equal opportunity** — Small and large businesses can participate
- **Public trust** — Transparency builds confidence in government spending

## How to participate in San Pablo City bidding

### Suppliers and contractors who are interested in bidding should:

1. **Register with the City** — Complete supplier registration at the City Finance Office
2. **Monitor announcements** — Check the city website and PhilGEPS for bidding opportunities
3. **Review bid documents** — Obtain and carefully read the bidding documents and specifications
4. **Submit bids** — Submit completed bids before the deadline during the specified bidding period
5. **Participate in bid opening** — Attend the public bid opening session
6. **Respond to inquiries** — Answer post-qualification questions if selected as the lowest bid

## Integrity and dispute resolution

San Pablo City maintains strict procurement integrity standards. Disputes or concerns regarding the bidding process may be addressed through:

- **Bids and Awards Committee (BAC)** — For procurement process issues
- **City Finance Office** — For payment and contract performance concerns
- **City Legal Office** — For disputes and legal matters
- **Official channels** — Submit complaints to the City Administrator or Mayor's office
