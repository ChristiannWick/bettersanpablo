# Local Regulations — San Pablo City

This page summarizes key local regulations in San Pablo City that residents and businesses should be aware of. For the full and legally binding text of ordinances and resolutions, visit the **Sangguniang Panlungsod Secretariat** or the official city website.

## Key Regulatory Areas

### Business and Trade Regulation

San Pablo City regulates business operations through:

- **Mayor's Permit / Business Permit** — All businesses operating within the city must secure an annual Mayor's Permit from the BPLO.
- **Zoning compliance** — Businesses must operate in zones appropriate to their classification per the city's Comprehensive Land Use Plan (CLUP).
- **Market regulations** — Public market stall holders and vendors are governed by city market ordinances.
- **Ambulant vending** — Street vending is regulated in designated areas; unauthorized vending in prohibited zones is subject to penalties.

### Environmental Regulations

San Pablo City enforces environmental rules especially around the Seven Lakes:

- **Anti-littering ordinance** — Throwing garbage in public spaces, waterways, and lake areas is prohibited and subject to fines.
- **Waste segregation** — Residents and establishments must follow mandatory waste segregation per RA 9003 (Ecological Solid Waste Management Act) and city ordinances.
- **Lake protection** — Dumping of waste, chemicals, or untreated sewage into the Seven Lakes is strictly prohibited.
- **Tree-cutting regulation** — Cutting of trees, especially within the lake buffer zones, requires a permit from the City Environment & Natural Resources Office (CENRO).
- **Quarrying regulation** — Quarrying activities are regulated by the city and require appropriate environmental clearances.

### Public Order and Safety

- **Anti-noise ordinance** — Excessive noise, particularly late at night, is regulated to protect residential areas.
- **Liquor and public drinking regulations** — Public consumption of alcohol in non-designated areas may be regulated by local ordinance.
- **Public safety regulations** — Fireworks, open burning, and other hazardous activities in public spaces are subject to city and national regulations.

### Building and Land Use

- **Building permits** — Any construction, renovation, or demolition of structures requires a **Building Permit** from the City Engineering Office.
- **Zoning and land use** — Development activities must comply with the city's zoning regulations as administered by the City Planning and Development Office (CPDO).
- **Subdivision regulations** — Real estate developments must secure clearances from city and national agencies.

### Health and Sanitation

- **Food establishment sanitation** — Restaurants, eateries, and food businesses must secure a **Sanitation Permit** from the City Health Office.
- **Slaughterhouse regulations** — Slaughter of animals must be done at authorized facilities under city health supervision.
- **Public health ordinances** — Anti-smoking zones, mandatory health protocols in specific areas, and disease prevention ordinances apply throughout the city.
