# Citizen Guides — San Pablo City

This page provides step-by-step guidance for common government transactions in San Pablo City. Residents can use these guides to prepare for visits to City Hall, barangay offices, and government service windows.

## Common City Hall Transactions

### Business Permit (New Application)

1. Secure a **Barangay Clearance** from the barangay where your business is located.
2. Go to the **Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)** at San Pablo City Hall.
3. Fill out the application form. Submit together with: DTI/SEC registration, barangay clearance, valid ID, and proof of business address.
4. Pay the applicable fees at the **City Treasurer's Office**.
5. Undergo fire and sanitation inspections if required.
6. Receive your **Mayor's Permit / Business Permit**.

### Business Permit Renewal

1. Prepare: previous year's business permit, barangay clearance, and proof of tax payments.
2. Go to the **BPLO** during the annual renewal period (typically January 1–20).
3. Pay renewal fees at the **City Treasurer's Office**.
4. Receive updated Mayor's Permit.

### Request for Barangay Clearance

1. Visit your **Barangay Hall** during office hours.
2. State your purpose (business permit, employment, etc.).
3. Present a valid ID and proof of residence.
4. Pay the barangay clearance fee (varies per barangay).
5. Receive your Barangay Clearance.

### Civil Registry Documents (Birth, Marriage, Death Certificates)

1. Go to the **City Civil Registrar's Office** at San Pablo City Hall.
2. Fill out the request form and state the purpose of the document.
3. Present a valid ID.
4. Pay the applicable fee.
5. Receive the certified copy (processing time varies; may take a few days for older records).

Alternatively, PSA-authenticated civil registry documents are available from the **Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA)** outlets or online at https://psa.gov.ph.

### Real Property Tax (RPT) Payment

1. Check your **Real Property Tax (RPT) assessment** from the City Assessor's Office.
2. Go to the **City Treasurer's Office** or pay online through the city's e-payment channels (if available).
3. Pay your RPT before January 31 to avail of annual discounts; or before the quarterly due dates to avoid surcharges.
4. Keep your official receipt.

### Freedom of Information (FOI) Request

1. Prepare a written request describing the document or information you need.
2. Submit to the relevant city office or file through the city's FOI process.
3. The city will respond within the timeframe specified by local FOI guidelines.
