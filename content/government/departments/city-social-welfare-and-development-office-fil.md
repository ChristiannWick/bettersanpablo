# City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) — Lungsod ng San Pablo

Ang **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** ng San Pablo ay namamahala sa social protection programs, assistance services, at community development initiatives para sa vulnerable at marginalized residents sa 80 barangay ng lungsod.

## Tungkol sa CSWDO

Ipinapatupad ng CSWDO ang mga program na mandato ng DSWD at local government, nagseserbisyo sa residenteng nangangailangan dahil sa kahirapan, kapansanan, edad, krisis, o iba pang pagkasugatan.

## Mga Serbisyo

| Serbisyo | Sinusuportahan |
|---|---|
| **AICS (Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations)** | Mga residenteng nasa krisis na nangangailangan ng financial assistance sa medikal, libing, edukasyon, o pagkain |
| **4Ps (Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program) coordination** | Mga registered 4Ps beneficiary household |
| **Senior citizen support** | 100-year-old centenarian cash gift, social pension coordination |
| **PWD (Persons with Disability) assistance** | ID processing, referrals, at assistance programs |
| **Solo parent support** | Solo Parent ID processing at kaugnay na benefits |
| **Child welfare services** | Child protection, day-care programs, early childhood care |
| **Women and family services** | VAWC (Violence Against Women and Children) referrals at support |
| **Livelihood assistance** | Skills training, capital assistance, livelihood program referrals |
| **Disaster relief at rehabilitation** | Relief distribution sa kalamidad at post-disaster support |

## Day-care at Early Childhood Care

Pinamamahalaan ng CSWDO ang **Day-care Program** sa mga barangay ng San Pablo, nagbibigay ng early childhood education at care para sa mga batang 3–5 taong gulang. Ang day-care centers ay pinatatakbo sa barangay level na may suporta mula sa city government.

## Mga Susuporta Program

### Para sa Senior Citizens
- Social pension para sa indigent senior
- Centenarian cash gift (RA 10868) — ₱100,000 para sa 100-year-old
- Senior Citizen ID processing (sa pamamagitan ng OSCA)

### Para sa PWDs
- PWD ID processing
- Referral sa medical at vocational rehabilitation
- Educational assistance

### Para sa Solo Parents
- Solo Parent ID processing
- Educational scholarship referrals
- Counseling at livelihood support

### Para sa 4Ps Beneficiaries
- Compliance verification
- Family Development Sessions
- Livelihood at education monitoring
