# Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO) — San Pablo City

The **Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)** of San Pablo City is the primary office for all business registration, permit issuance, licensing, and regulatory compliance requirements for businesses operating in the city.

## About the BPLO

The BPLO processes business permit applications, renewals, and amendments for all business establishments in San Pablo City. It coordinates with other city departments — including the City Health Office, City Engineering Office, and Bureau of Fire Protection — to ensure businesses meet safety and regulatory standards before permits are issued.

## Services Offered

| Service                            | Description                                                      |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **New business permit**            | Registration and Mayor's Permit for newly established businesses |
| **Annual business permit renewal** | Yearly renewal of Mayor's Permit (January deadline)              |
| **Business permit amendment**      | Changes in business information, location, or activity           |
| **Business closure**               | Official retirement or closure of registered business            |
| **Business permit certification**  | Certification of business permit status for official purposes    |
| **Zoning clearance coordination**  | Coordination with CPDO for zoning compliance                     |

## General Requirements for New Business Permit

- Accomplished application form
- DTI registration (sole proprietors), SEC registration (corporations/partnerships), or CDA registration (cooperatives)
- Barangay clearance from the barangay where the business is located
- Proof of business address (lease contract or title)
- Valid government ID of the owner or authorized representative
- Fire safety inspection certificate (from Bureau of Fire Protection)
- Sanitation permit (from City Health Office)
- Other clearances depending on business type

## Annual Renewal

Business permit renewal is due every year, typically from **January 1 to January 20**. Late renewals are subject to surcharges and penalties. Renewing businesses must present:

- Previous year's Mayor's Permit
- Latest barangay clearance
- Proof of updated tax payments

## Steps for New Business Registration

1. Secure a barangay clearance from the barangay where your business will operate.
2. Register your business name with DTI, SEC, or CDA.
3. Submit a completed application form at the BPLO counter or through the online portal.
4. Undergo the required inspections (fire, sanitation, zoning).
5. Pay the assessed fees at the **City Treasurer's Office**.
6. Receive your **Mayor's Permit**.
