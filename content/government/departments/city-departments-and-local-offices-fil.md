# Mga City Department at Local Office — Lungsod ng San Pablo

Ang pahinang ito ay naglalahad ng mga key na departamento at local service offices ng City Government ng San Pablo, Laguna, at nagpapaliwanag kung paano nakikipag-ugnayan ang mga residente sa government programs sa city at barangay levels.

## Mga City Department na nagseserbisyo sa residente

Inoorganisa ng San Pablo ang public services sa pamamagitan ng mga city department at office na sumusuporta sa pagpaplano, kalusugan, kaligtasan, negosyo, social welfare, at environmental management.

Mga karaniwang city office:

- Office of the Mayor
- Office of the Vice Mayor
- Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Council)
- City Health Office (CHO)
- Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)
- City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)
- City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)
- City Planning and Development Office (CPDO)
- City Treasurer's Office
- City Assessor's Office
- City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)
- City Agriculture Office
- City Engineering Office
- City Tourism Office
- City Legal Office
- City Budget Office
- City Civil Registrar

## Paano nakakatulong ang departamento sa mga residente

Bawat departamento ay may responsibilidad sa set ng services at regulatory functions:

| Departamento | Tungkulin |
|---|---|
| **CHO** | Mga programa sa kalusugan, vaccination, maternal care, sanitation |
| **BPLO** | Business registration, permit renewal, licensing |
| **CSWDO** | Senior citizen, PWD, solo parent, AICS, 4Ps |
| **CDRRMO** | Disaster preparedness, emergency response, evacuation |
| **CPDO** | Land use, zoning, urban planning |
| **City Treasurer** | Real Property Tax, business tax collection |
| **City Assessor** | Property valuation, tax declaration |
| **CENRO** | Environment, waste, lake protection |
| **City Agriculture** | Farm support, livestock, fisheries |
| **City Engineering** | Roads, drainage, building permits |
| **City Tourism** | Seven Lakes, tourism promotion, heritage |
| **City Legal** | Legal opinions, contracts |
| **City Budget** | Budget preparation, fiscal management |
| **Civil Registrar** | Birth, death, marriage certificates |

## Mga Barangay-level Office

Bawat isa sa **80 barangay** ng San Pablo ay may:

- **Punong Barangay** — Barangay Captain
- **Sangguniang Barangay** — 7 elected members
- **SK Chairperson** — Youth representative
- **Barangay Tanod** — Local peace and order
- **Barangay Health Worker (BHW)** — Frontline health worker
- **Barangay Nutrition Scholar (BNS)** — Nutrition programs

## Paano mag-access ng serbisyo

### Hakbang 1 — Tukuyin ang tamang opisina
Gamitin ang table sa itaas o tumawag sa City Information Office para sa guidance.

### Hakbang 2 — Maghanda ng mga dokumento
Kadalasang kailangan:
- Valid government ID
- Proof of residency
- Barangay clearance
- Mga specific document depende sa serbisyo

### Hakbang 3 — Bumisita o tumawag
- **In-person:** San Pablo City Hall, Mabini Extension (Lun–Biy 8 AM – 5 PM)
- **Telepono:** (049) 562-0111 (main line)
- **Online:** [sanpablocity.gov.ph](https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph)

### Hakbang 4 — Magbayad sa awtorisadong channel
- City Treasurer's Office (cashier)
- Authorized partner banks (kung available)
