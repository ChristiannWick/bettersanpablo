# City Health Office — San Pablo City

The **City Health Office (CHO)** of San Pablo City oversees public health programs, disease prevention and control, maternal and child health, sanitation, and health promotion across the city's 80 barangays.

## About the City Health Office

The CHO implements health programs aligned with the Department of Health (DOH) national guidelines and the health priorities of the local government. It coordinates with Rural Health Units (RHUs), Barangay Health Centers, and partner hospitals to deliver health services to San Pablo City residents.

## Services Offered

| Service                        | Description                                                                  |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Free consultations**         | Primary health consultations at health centers and RHUs                      |
| **Vaccination / Immunization** | Routine childhood vaccines, rabies vaccines, and mass immunization campaigns |
| **Maternal care**              | Prenatal, delivery, and postnatal care coordination                          |
| **Nutrition programs**         | Child nutrition monitoring, supplemental feeding, and Vitamin A distribution |
| **TB-DOTS program**            | Tuberculosis diagnosis, directly-observed treatment, and follow-up           |
| **Dengue surveillance**        | Vector control, fogging operations, and dengue case monitoring               |
| **Sanitation inspections**     | Food establishment sanitation permits and environmental health monitoring    |
| **PhilHealth Konsulta**        | Referral and enrollment assistance for PhilHealth Konsulta packages          |
| **Health certificates**        | Medical certificates and health clearances                                   |

## Barangay Health Centers

San Pablo City has health centers distributed across its 80 barangays. These are the first point of contact for:

- General consultations and check-ups
- Immunization and vaccination schedules
- Prenatal care and maternal health services
- Referrals to hospitals and specialists

## Key Health Programs

- **Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI)** — Routine vaccines for children
- **National Tuberculosis Control Program (NTP)** — TB-DOTS
- **Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI)** — Child health protocols
- **Dengue Prevention and Control** — Surveillance and vector control
- **Nutrition Program** — Addressing malnutrition and stunting
- **Non-communicable Disease (NCD) Prevention** — Hypertension, diabetes screening
