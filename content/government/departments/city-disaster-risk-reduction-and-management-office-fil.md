# City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO) — Lungsod ng San Pablo

Ang **City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)** ng San Pablo ay responsable para sa disaster risk reduction, emergency preparedness, response operations, at post-disaster rehabilitation at recovery para sa lahat ng residente sa 80 barangay ng lungsod.

## Tungkol sa CDRRMO

Ang CDRRMO ay umooperate sa ilalim ng Republic Act 10121 (Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010) at nakikipag-coordinate sa national NDRRMC, provincial PDRRMC, at barangay BDRRMC para tiyakin ang coordinated approach sa disaster preparedness at response sa San Pablo.

## Mga Serbisyo at Tungkulin

| Serbisyo | Description |
|---|---|
| **Emergency response** | Rescue, evacuation, at emergency operations sa kalamidad |
| **Early warning systems** | Weather alerts, flood warnings, typhoon advisories |
| **Evacuation management** | Designated evacuation centers at evacuation planning para sa high-risk areas |
| **Disaster drills** | Community evacuation drills at emergency response exercises |
| **Hazard mapping** | Pag-identify ng flood-prone, landslide-prone areas |
| **Damage assessment** | Post-disaster validation ng pinsala |
| **Relief coordination** | Coordination sa CSWDO at NGO partners |
| **Public information** | Disaster awareness campaigns at education |

## Mga Pangunahing Banta sa San Pablo

| Banta | Affected Areas |
|---|---|
| **Bagyo at malakas na ulan** | Citywide |
| **Baha** | Lake-adjacent at low-lying barangays |
| **Landslide** | Hillsides, Mt. Cristobal foothills |
| **Lindol** | Citywide (West Valley Fault malayo) |
| **Volcanic activity** | Mt. Banahaw monitoring |
| **Sunog** | Urban areas, palengke |

## Mga Aktibidad at Programa

### Pre-Disaster
- Hazard at vulnerability assessment
- Mga barangay disaster drills
- Public awareness campaigns
- Maintenance ng evacuation centers
- Pre-positioning ng relief goods
- Training ng barangay tanod at responders

### Sa Panahon ng Disaster
- 24/7 emergency operations center
- Rescue at evacuation
- First aid at medical response
- Pakikipag-coordinate sa hospital
- Pakikipag-ugnayan sa PAGASA, PHIVOLCS, NDRRMC

### Post-Disaster
- Damage assessment
- Relief distribution coordination
- Rehabilitation planning
- Pakikipag-coordinate sa NHA para sa relocation
- Mental health support referrals

## Mga Legal Framework

- **RA 10121** — Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010
- **RA 9514** — Revised Fire Code of the Philippines
- **PD 1566** — Mandatory disaster preparedness
- **DILG-DSWD-NDRRMC Joint Memorandum** — Camp coordination
