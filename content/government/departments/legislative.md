# Legislative (City Council) — San Pablo City

The **Sangguniang Panlungsod** (City Council) of San Pablo is the legislative body of the City Government. It enacts ordinances, approves the annual budget, and ensures legal compliance of city programs.

## What the Council Does

### Legislative
- Enacts city **ordinances** (local laws)
- Passes **resolutions** on local concerns
- Reviews and approves the **annual budget** submitted by the Mayor
- Approves contracts above the Mayor's spending threshold
- Conducts **public hearings** for proposed ordinances

### Oversight
- Reviews implementation of city programs
- Investigates complaints against city officials
- Approves appointments requiring council confirmation
- Conducts inquiries in aid of legislation

### Representation
- Represents constituents in policy discussions
- Sponsors legislation responding to barangay needs
- Holds barangay consultations
- Coordinates with the Mayor and national agencies

## Standing Committees (typical structure)

The Sangguniang Panlungsod typically organizes committees to handle specific areas. Each councilor chairs or sits in multiple committees:

- **Committee on Finance, Budget & Appropriations**
- **Committee on Health & Sanitation**
- **Committee on Education & Culture**
- **Committee on Infrastructure & Public Works**
- **Committee on Trade, Commerce & Tourism**
- **Committee on Agriculture & Fisheries**
- **Committee on Public Order & Safety**
- **Committee on Environmental Protection**
- **Committee on Social Services & Women's Welfare**
- **Committee on Transportation & Communication**
- **Committee on Youth & Sports**
- **Committee on Senior Citizens & PWD Affairs**

> Specific committee assignments and chairmanships are issued by Council resolution at the start of each term.

## How a City Ordinance Becomes Law

1. **Drafting** — Sponsor councilor or committee drafts the ordinance
2. **First Reading** — Title is read; referred to committee
3. **Committee Hearings** — Public consultation, amendments
4. **Second Reading** — Substantive debate
5. **Third Reading** — Final vote
6. **Mayor's Action** — Sign, veto, or pocket-approve (10-day window)
7. **Publication** — Posted at City Hall and 3 conspicuous places
8. **Effectivity** — Typically 15 days after publication
