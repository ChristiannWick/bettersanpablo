## Barangay Officials

San Pablo City has **80 barangays**. Each barangay has a **Punong Barangay** (Barangay Captain), 7 elected Sangguniang Barangay members, and an SK Chairperson. For your specific barangay's officials, contact your **Barangay Hall** directly or visit [sanpablocity.gov.ph](https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph).

---

## How to Reach an Official

1. **In person** — Visit the appropriate office at City Hall during office hours (Mon–Fri, 8 AM – 5 PM).
2. **By phone** — Call **(049) 562-0111** and ask to be routed.
3. **By letter** — Address mail to the official's office c/o San Pablo City Hall, Mabini Extension, San Pablo City, Laguna 4000.
4. **For document requests** — File an FOI request through the **Records Office: (049) 562-1234 loc 205**.
5. **For press / public info** — Contact the **City Information Office: (049) 561-1483**.
