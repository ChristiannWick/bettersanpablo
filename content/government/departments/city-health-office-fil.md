# City Health Office — Lungsod ng San Pablo

Ang **City Health Office (CHO)** ng San Pablo ay nag-oversee sa mga programa sa pampublikong kalusugan, disease prevention at control, maternal at child health, sanitation, at health promotion sa 80 barangay ng lungsod.

## Tungkol sa City Health Office

Ipinapatupad ng CHO ang mga health program na naaayon sa DOH national guidelines at health priorities ng local government. Nakikipag-coordinate ito sa Rural Health Units (RHUs), Barangay Health Centers, at partner hospital para maghatid ng serbisyo sa mga residente.

## Mga Serbisyo

| Serbisyo | Description |
|---|---|
| **Libreng konsultasyon** | Primary health consultations sa mga health center at RHU |
| **Vaccination / Immunization** | Routine childhood vaccines, rabies vaccines, at mass immunization campaigns |
| **Maternal care** | Prenatal, panganganak, postnatal care coordination |
| **Nutrition programs** | Child nutrition monitoring, supplemental feeding, Vitamin A distribution |
| **TB-DOTS program** | Tuberculosis diagnosis, directly-observed treatment, at follow-up |
| **Dengue surveillance** | Vector control, fogging operations, dengue case monitoring |
| **Sanitation inspections** | Food establishment sanitation permits at environmental health monitoring |
| **PhilHealth Konsulta** | Referral at enrollment assistance para sa PhilHealth Konsulta packages |
| **Health certificates** | Medical certificates at health clearances |

## Mga Barangay Health Center

May health center ang San Pablo na nakakalat sa 80 barangay. Ito ang first point of contact para sa:

- General consultations at check-ups
- Mga iskedyul ng pagpapabakuna
- Prenatal care at maternal health
- Mga referral sa ospital at specialist

## Mga Key Health Program

- **Expanded Program on Immunization (EPI)** — Regular na bakuna para sa bata
- **National Tuberculosis Control Program (NTP)** — TB-DOTS
- **Integrated Management of Childhood Illness (IMCI)** — Child health protocols
- **Dengue Prevention and Control** — Surveillance at vector control
- **Nutrition Program** — Pagtatakda ng malnutrisyon at stunting
- **Non-communicable Disease (NCD) Prevention** — Screening sa hypertension, diabetes
