# City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO) — San Pablo City

The **City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)** of San Pablo City is responsible for disaster risk reduction, emergency preparedness, response operations, and post-disaster rehabilitation and recovery for all residents across the city's 80 barangays.

## About the CDRRMO

The CDRRMO operates under Republic Act 10121 (Philippine Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Act of 2010) and coordinates with the national NDRRMC, provincial PDRRMC, and barangay BDRRMC units to ensure a coordinated approach to disaster preparedness and response in San Pablo City.

## Hazards Relevant to San Pablo City

San Pablo City faces specific disaster risks including:

- **Flooding** — Low-lying areas, particularly near the Seven Lakes and river systems, are prone to flooding during heavy rain
- **Typhoons** — San Pablo is within the typhoon belt and can experience strong wind and rain from tropical cyclones
- **Earthquake** — Proximity to fault lines requires earthquake preparedness for all residents
- **Landslides** — Hilly and mountainous areas near Mt. Banahaw and surrounding terrain are at risk during heavy rain

## What to Do During a Disaster

1. **Follow official warnings** — Monitor PAGASA advisories and CDRRMO announcements.
2. **Know your evacuation center** — Ask your barangay hall for the nearest designated evacuation center.
3. **Prepare a go bag** — Keep emergency supplies ready: water, food, documents, medicines, flashlight, and phone charger.
4. **Evacuate early** — Do not wait until the last minute if evacuation is recommended.
5. **Call for help** — Contact CDRRMO at **0998 540 7171** or **911** for rescue and emergency assistance.

## Evacuation Centers

San Pablo City has designated evacuation centers across its 80 barangays. Contact your **Barangay Hall** or the **CDRRMO** for the evacuation center designated for your area.

## Barangay DRRM Committees (BDRRMC)

Each of San Pablo's 80 barangays has a **Barangay Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Committee (BDRRMC)** that coordinates local preparedness and response. Contact your barangay captain or barangay hall for barangay-level DRRM information.
