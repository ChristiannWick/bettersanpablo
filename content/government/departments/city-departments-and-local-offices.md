# City Departments & Local Offices

This page highlights the key departments and local service offices of the City Government of San Pablo, Laguna, and explains how residents can engage with government programs across city and barangay levels.

## City departments that serve residents
San Pablo City organizes public services through city departments and offices that support planning, health, safety, business, social welfare, and environmental management.

Common city offices include:

- Office of the Mayor
- Office of the Vice Mayor
- Sangguniang Panlungsod (City Council)
- City Health Office
- Business Permits and Licensing Office (BPLO)
- City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)
- City Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Office (CDRRMO)
- City Planning and Development Office (CPDO)
- City Treasurer's Office
- City Assessor's Office
- City Environment and Natural Resources Office (CENRO)
- City Agriculture Office
- City Engineering Office
- City Tourism Office
- City Legal Office
- City Budget Office
- City Civil Registrar

## How departments work for San Pablo residents
Each department is responsible for a set of services and regulatory functions:

- Health, vaccination, and public sanitation programs through the City Health Office.
- Business permits, licensing, and regulatory clearances through BPLO.
- Social assistance and community protection programs through CSWDO.
- Disaster preparedness, emergency response, and risk reduction through CDRRMO.
- Public works, roads, drainage, and infrastructure through the City Engineering Office.
- Land use, zoning, and development planning through CPDO.
- Environmental protection and natural resource management through CENRO.

Residents should use the official San Pablo City website and City Hall hotlines to find current service hours, application forms, and requirements.

## Barangay-level local offices
San Pablo City is composed of 80 barangays. Each barangay is led by a Barangay Captain (Punong Barangay) and a Sangguniang Barangay.

Barangay halls are the first point of contact for:

- community certificates and barangay clearances
- local assistance programs
- neighborhood dispute resolution
- vaccination and health outreach services
- emergency notification and evacuation support

For barangay services, residents should contact their barangay hall directly and follow announcements from the City Government of San Pablo.

## Getting help from city government
To reach city government offices, use the San Pablo City official web portal and published hotlines. Common contact channels include:

- Official website: https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph
- City Hall main line: (049) 562-1234
- City Information Office: (049) 561-1483
- City Admin's Office: (049) 521-0307
- CDRRMO emergency line: 0998 540 7171

City services and offices are designed to help residents access permits, social programs, disaster support, health services, and business information across the city and barangays.
