# City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO) — San Pablo City

The **City Social Welfare and Development Office (CSWDO)** of San Pablo City administers social protection programs, assistance services, and community development initiatives for vulnerable and marginalized residents across the city's 80 barangays.

## About the CSWDO

The CSWDO implements programs mandated by the Department of Social Welfare and Development (DSWD) and the local government, serving residents who need support due to poverty, disability, age, crisis situations, or other vulnerabilities.

## Services Offered

| Service                                                    | Who It Serves                                                                                    |
| ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **AICS (Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations)**  | Residents in crisis needing financial assistance for medical, burial, educational, or food needs |
| **4Ps (Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program) coordination** | Registered 4Ps beneficiary households                                                            |
| **Senior citizen support**                                 | 100-year-old centenarian cash gift, social pension coordination                                  |
| **PWD (Persons with Disability) assistance**               | ID processing, referrals, and assistance programs                                                |
| **Solo parent support**                                    | Solo Parent ID processing and related benefits                                                   |
| **Child welfare services**                                 | Child protection, day-care programs, and early childhood care                                    |
| **Women and family services**                              | VAWC (Violence Against Women and Children) referrals and support                                 |
| **Livelihood assistance**                                  | Skills training, capital assistance, and livelihood program referrals                            |
| **Disaster relief and rehabilitation**                     | Relief distribution during calamities and post-disaster support                                  |

## Day-care and Early Childhood Care

The CSWDO oversees the **Day-care Program** across San Pablo City's barangays, providing early childhood education and care for children 3–5 years old. Day-care centers are operated at the barangay level with support from the city government.

## How to Access CSWDO Services

1. Visit the **CSWDO office** at San Pablo City Hall with a valid ID and supporting documents for your request.
2. For barangay-level concerns, approach your **Barangay Social Worker** or Barangay Captain first.
3. For 4Ps concerns, visit the **CSWDO 4Ps desk** or contact your assigned social worker.
4. For emergency assistance (AICS), go directly to the CSWDO with documentation of your crisis situation.

## Common Documents Needed

- Valid government ID
- Barangay certificate or clearance
- Medical records (for medical assistance requests)
- Death certificate (for burial assistance)
- For PWD ID: PWD medical assessment form from a licensed physician
