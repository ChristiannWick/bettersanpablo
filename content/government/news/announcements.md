# Public Announcements and Advisories

The City Government of San Pablo issues public announcements and advisories to keep residents informed about service schedules, emergency situations, community programs, and important city government notices.

## Where to Find Announcements

| Source                        | How to Access                                                    |
| ----------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Official city website**     | https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph                                  |
| **City Information Office**   | (049) 561-1483                                                   |
| **Barangay halls**            | Posted on community bulletin boards and announced via PA systems |
| **City Hall bulletin boards** | Physical notices posted at San Pablo City Hall                   |

## Types of Announcements

### Service advisories

Notices about changes in service schedules, office hours, and availability of city services such as:

- Business permit renewal schedules and deadlines
- Health center vaccination schedules
- Market and public facility operation adjustments during holidays
- Water service interruptions from SPCWD (San Pablo City Water District)

### Emergency advisories

During typhoons, flooding, or other emergencies, the city issues advisories on:

- Evacuation orders and designated evacuation centers
- Road closures and traffic rerouting
- Suspended classes and work (per IATF or city executive orders)
- Emergency relief distribution schedules

### Social welfare and assistance programs

Announcements about:

- 4Ps (Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program) updates
- Senior citizen benefit release schedules
- AICS (Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations) applications
- Livelihood training and registration windows

### Events and activities

Notices about:

- City-sponsored events, festivals, and community programs
- Public hearings and consultations
- City government ceremonies and commemorations
