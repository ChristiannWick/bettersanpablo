# Mga Pampublikong Anunsyo at Advisory

Naglalabas ang City Government ng San Pablo ng public announcements at advisories upang panatilihing informed ang mga residente sa service schedules, emergency situations, community programs, at importanteng city government notices.

## Saan Maghahanap ng Mga Anunsyo

| Source | Paano Mag-access |
|---|---|
| **Opisyal na city website** | https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph |
| **City Information Office** | (049) 561-1483 |
| **Mga Barangay Hall** | Naka-post sa community bulletin board at inanunsyo sa PA systems |
| **City Hall bulletin boards** | Mga physical notice na naka-post sa San Pablo City Hall |

## Mga Uri ng Anunsyo

### Service advisories

Mga abiso tungkol sa pagbabago sa service schedule, office hours, at availability ng city services tulad ng:

- Iskedyul ng business permit renewal at deadlines
- Iskedyul ng vaccination sa health center
- Adjustments sa palengke at public facility sa holidays
- Water service interruptions mula sa SPCWD (San Pablo City Water District)

### Emergency advisories

Sa panahon ng bagyo, baha, o iba pang emergency, naglalabas ang lungsod ng advisory tungkol sa:

- Evacuation orders at designated evacuation centers
- Road closures at traffic rerouting
- Suspended classes at trabaho (per IATF o city executive orders)
- Iskedyul ng emergency relief distribution

### Mga Social welfare at assistance programs

Mga anunsyo tungkol sa:

- 4Ps (Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program) updates
- Iskedyul ng release ng senior citizen benefits
- AICS (Assistance to Individuals in Crisis Situations) applications
- Mga windows para sa livelihood training at registration

### Mga Event at Aktibidad

Mga abiso tungkol sa:

- City-sponsored events, pista, at community programs
- Public hearings at consultations
- City government ceremonies at paggunita
