# City News and Updates

This page directs residents to official news sources from the City Government of San Pablo, Laguna. The city regularly publishes updates on public programs, infrastructure projects, city governance activities, and community events.

## Where to Find Official City News

The City Government of San Pablo publishes updates through its official channels:

| Channel                                | What You'll Find                                          |
| -------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Official City Website**              | Press releases, program announcements, event schedules    |
| **Official Facebook Page**             | Real-time updates, photo documentation, public advisories |
| **City Information Office**            | Press releases and media coordination                     |
| **Barangay halls and bulletin boards** | Community-level announcements and barangay-specific news  |

## Official Online Channels

- **Official website:** https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph
- **City Information Office:** (049) 561-1483
- **City Hall main line:** (049) 562-1234

## Types of News Published

The City Government of San Pablo regularly publishes news on:

- **Infrastructure and public works** — Road repairs, new facilities, drainage projects
- **Health programs** — Vaccination drives, health outreach activities, disease prevention campaigns
- **Social welfare activities** — Assistance distributions, livelihood programs, senior citizen support
- **Disaster preparedness and response** — Typhoon advisories, flood response, evacuation updates
- **Economic development** — Business permit drives, trade events, livelihood opportunities
- **Environmental programs** — Lake clean-up drives, tree planting, Seven Lakes conservation
- **Tourism and culture** — Festival announcements, city events, heritage activities

## City Social Media Reminder

Always verify news and updates through official city government channels. Unverified information circulated on personal accounts or unofficial groups may be inaccurate.

For media and press inquiries, contact the **City Information Office** at (049) 561-1483.
