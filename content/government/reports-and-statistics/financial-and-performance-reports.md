# Financial and Performance Reports — San Pablo City

San Pablo City is a **1st Class Component City**, one of the highest income-classified local government units in Laguna Province. The following financial data is sourced from the Bureau of Local Government Finance (BLGF) and official city reports.

---

## City Government Financials (FY 2022)

| Item | Amount |
| ---- | ------ |
| Total Revenue | ₱1,960,000,000 |
| Total Expenditure | ₱1,747,000,000 |
| Total Assets | ₱3,948,000,000 |
| Total Liabilities | ₱1,047,000,000 |
| Net Assets | ₱2,901,000,000 |

- Annual Regular Revenue (2016): ₱933,220,870.43 (source: BLGF)
- Income Classification: **1st Class** (highest LGU income tier in the Philippines)

*Note: FY 2023 and FY 2024 figures will be published in this portal as official reports become available.*

## Annual Accomplishment Reports

The City Government of San Pablo publishes annual accomplishment reports summarizing programs, projects, and activities across all departments. These are made available through:

- The **Full Disclosure Policy (FDP) Portal** at [fdpp.blgs.gov.ph](https://fdpp.blgs.gov.ph/)
- The **Official City Website** at [www.sanpablocity.gov.ph](https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph)

Key 2024–2025 accomplishments include:
- Inauguration of the San Pablo City Central Terminal (2023)
- Guinness World Record recognition for mass coconut planting activity (2025)
- Successful conduct of the 30th Coco Festival and Coco Trade Fair (January 2025)
- City transition to new administration under Mayor Arcadio Gapangada Jr. (May 2025)
