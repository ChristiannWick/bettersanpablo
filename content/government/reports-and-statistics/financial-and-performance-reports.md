# Financial and Performance Reports

This page provides access to San Pablo City financial reports, audit results, and government performance metrics.

## What are financial and performance reports?

San Pablo City government publishes financial and performance reports to demonstrate:

- **Fiscal Responsibility** — How public funds are collected and used
- **Transparency** — Full disclosure of city finances and budget execution
- **Accountability** — Results achieved with public resources
- **Governance Quality** — Effectiveness and efficiency of city government operations

## Types of financial reports

### City budget documents

The **Annual City Budget** details how San Pablo City allocates resources across:

- **Personnel Services** — Salaries and benefits of city employees
- **Maintenance and Operating Expenses** — Day-to-day operations of city departments
- **Capital Outlay** — Infrastructure projects and asset purchases
- **Debt Service** — Payments on city loans and obligations

### Financial statements

City financial statements include:

- **Statement of Income** — City revenues from taxes and other sources
- **Statement of Assets and Liabilities** — City property, equipment, and obligations
- **Cash Flow Statement** — Money received and spent during the fiscal year
- **Budget Execution Report** — Actual spending compared to budget

### Audit reports

The **Commission on Audit (COA)** examines city finances and publishes:

- **Annual Audit Report** — Comprehensive review of all city finances
- **Opinion on Financial Statements** — Whether financial reports are accurate
- **Observations and Recommendations** — Issues found and suggested improvements

## Revenue sources for San Pablo City

San Pablo City generates revenue from:

### Local tax revenues

- **Business Tax (LBT)** — Tax on business operations and trade
- **Property Tax (Real Property Tax)** — Tax on land and buildings
- **Documentary Stamp Tax** — Tax on legal documents and contracts

### National government share

- **Internal Revenue Allotment (IRA)** — Automatic share of national taxes
- **Special Allocation** — Additional funds for specific programs

### Other revenues

- **Service fees** — Fees for city services and permits
- **Rentals** — Income from city-owned property and facilities
- **Donations** — Contributions from private organizations and individuals

## City spending and expenditures

San Pablo City budgets allocate funds for:

- **Social services** — Education, health, welfare, and housing
- **Infrastructure development** — Roads, water systems, public buildings
- **Public safety** — Police, fire, and emergency services
- **Environmental management** — Parks, water quality, waste management
- **Government operations** — Administration, personnel, equipment
- **Public utilities** — Water, sewage, and sanitation services

## Performance metrics and accomplishments

San Pablo City government tracks and reports:

### Service delivery

- **Health services** — Clinics, vaccination programs, disease prevention activities
- **Education support** — Scholarships, school infrastructure, literacy programs
- **Social programs** — Assistance to vulnerable populations, livelihood projects
- **Infrastructure projects** — Roads constructed, bridges built, systems improved

### Development indicators

- **Economic growth** — Business registrations, job creation, investment
- **Community development** — Barangay projects, livelihood programs, skills training
- **Environmental initiatives** — Tree-planting, waste reduction, water conservation
- **Quality of life** — Health indicators, education outcomes, community satisfaction

## Fiscal transparency initiatives

San Pablo City is committed to fiscal transparency through:

- **Regular publication** of financial statements and reports
- **Online accessibility** of city budget and financial data
- **Public hearings** on budget preparation and significant expenditures
- **Open data initiatives** to share government information with the public
- **Audit compliance** addressing COA recommendations and findings

## Understanding city finances

City government revenues and expenditures are governed by:

- **Republic Act No. 7160** (Local Government Code of 1991)
- **Budget laws and executive orders**
- **Sangguniang Panlungsod ordinances and resolutions**
- **Commission on Audit regulations**
- **Bureau of Local Government Finance standards**

## How to request financial information

Residents and organizations can request financial information by:

2. **Contacting city offices** — Call or visit relevant departments
3. **Filing a request** — Use Freedom of Information requests for specific documents
4. **Attending public hearings** — Participate in budget and financial discussions
