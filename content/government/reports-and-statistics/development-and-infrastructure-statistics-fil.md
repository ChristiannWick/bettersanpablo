# Development at Infrastructure Statistics

Nagbibigay ang pahinang ito ng impormasyon tungkol sa infrastructure, development projects, at public works accomplishments ng Lungsod ng San Pablo.

## Overview ng Infrastructure ng San Pablo

Bilang isang 1st-class component city na may 80 barangay, may iba-ibang infrastructure ang San Pablo kasama ang:

- **Road network** — City roads, barangay roads, at provincial connections
- **Water systems** — Water distribution, irrigation systems, at water supply facilities
- **Sanitation** — Wastewater treatment, drainage systems, at solid waste management
- **Public buildings** — Eskuwelahan, health center, palengke, at government offices
- **Utilities** — Electric lines, communication networks, at utility infrastructure

## Mga Major Infrastructure Systems

### Transportasyon at kalsada

May San Pablo ng:

- **National highways** — Mga major routes na nag-uugnay sa Laguna at ibang rehiyon
- **City roads** — Primary, secondary, at tertiary roads sa loob ng lungsod
- **Bridges** — Mga crossings sa pitong lake at river systems
- **Public transportation** — Mga jeepney, traysikel, at bus routes

Kasama sa infrastructure development:

- Road widening at improvement projects
- Bridge construction at rehabilitation
- Traffic management systems
- Public transport facilities

### Water supply at sanitation

Kasama sa water infrastructure:

- **Water treatment plants** — Purification facilities para sa ligtas na inuming tubig
- **Distribution networks** — Mga tubo na naghahatid ng tubig sa mga tahanan at negosyo
- **Wastewater treatment** — Pagproseso ng sewage at wastewater
- **Drainage systems** — Storm water management at flood control

### Ang Seven Lakes

Kasama sa kakaibang infrastructure ng San Pablo:

- **Lake management systems** — Conservation at utilization facilities
- **Fisheries infrastructure** — Aquaculture at fishing support facilities
- **Recreation facilities** — Parke, docks, at tourism amenities
- **Water quality monitoring** — Mga sistema para mapanatili ang lake health

## Pag-report ng Infrastructure Status

### Paano mag-report ng infrastructure problems

Maaaring mag-report ang residente ng issues tulad ng:

- **Sirang kalsada** — Mga butas, bitak, o pagkasira
- **Water system problems** — Mga leak, mababang pressure, o contamination
- **Drainage issues** — Pag-clog, overflow, o maintenance needs
- **Public facility damage** — Structural damage o safety concerns

**Para mag-report:**

- Tumawag sa **City Engineering Office:** (049) 562-0111
- Tumawag sa inyong **Barangay Captain o Barangay Council**
- Bumisita sa **City Hall** para mag-file ng formal request
- Mag-email sa **engineering@sanpablocity.gov.ph**
