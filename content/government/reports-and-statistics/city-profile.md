# City Profile — San Pablo City, Laguna

**The City of Seven Lakes** — San Pablo City is a 1st Class Component City in the province of Laguna, CALABARZON (Region IV-A), Philippines. It is the largest city in Laguna by land area and is nationally known for its seven volcanic crater lakes.

## History

San Pablo City traces its origins to **1586**, when it was established as a parish by the Franciscan missionaries. It was renamed **San Pablo de los Montes** in honor of St. Paul the First Hermit.

In **1647**, the settlement was formally elevated to a municipality. After centuries of growth, **Commonwealth Act No. 520** was signed on **May 7, 1940**, granting San Pablo its city charter — making it one of the oldest chartered cities in the Philippines. The city was officially inaugurated on **March 30, 1941**.

In **1967**, the **Roman Catholic Diocese of San Pablo** was formally established, with the Cathedral of Saint Paul the First Hermit as its seat.

Today, San Pablo City is a regional center for commerce, tourism, education, and agriculture, anchored by its iconic Seven Lakes and strong cultural heritage.

## The Seven Lakes

San Pablo is known worldwide for its cluster of seven volcanic maar lakes, formed by phreatomagmatic eruptions along the San Pablo Volcanic Field — a rift zone between Mt. Makiling and Mt. Banahaw.

| Lake | Notes |
| ---- | ----- |
| Sampaloc | Largest of the seven lakes; at the city center; lakeside promenade |
| Palakpakin | Accessible for fishing and nature visits |
| Bunot | Quiet lake in a forested setting |
| Pandin | Known for bamboo-raft eco-tourism; community-managed |
| Yambo | Twin lake of Pandin; calm waters and scenic views |
| Muhikap | Smaller lake; accessible via trekking |
| Calibato | Southernmost; surrounded by farmland and forest |

Hiking trails connecting the lakes were officially opened in **2005** by the Rotary Club of San Pablo City.

## Key Festivals

### Coco Festival (Coconut Festival)
- **Dates:** January 9–15 annually
- **Culmination:** January 15 (Feast Day of St. Paul the First Hermit)
- **First held:** 1996 (400th anniversary of the parish)
- **Features:** Street dancing with coconut-themed costumes, float parade, Mutya at Lakan ng San Pablo pageant, Coco Trade Fair
- **Awards:** DOT and ATOP "Best Tourism Event — Festival Category, City Level"

### City Foundation Day
- **Date:** May 7 (anniversary of the signing of Commonwealth Act No. 520 in 1940)

## Notable People

**Chief Justice Alexander G. Gesmundo**
27th Chief Justice of the Supreme Court of the Philippines. Born in San Pablo City on November 6, 1956.

**Gerry Alanguilan (Komikero)**
Acclaimed Filipino comic book artist, writer, and architect. Born and raised in San Pablo, Laguna. Founded the Komikero Komiks Museum in the city. His works were internationally recognized, including inking for Marvel and DC Comics. Passed away in January 2019.

**Erik Spoelstra**
Head coach of the Miami Heat (NBA) and coach of USA Basketball (2025). His mother, Elisa Celino, is a native of San Pablo, Laguna.
