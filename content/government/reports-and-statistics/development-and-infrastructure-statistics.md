# Development and Infrastructure Statistics

This page provides information about San Pablo City's infrastructure, development projects, and public works accomplishments.

## San Pablo City infrastructure overview

As a 1st-class component city with 80 barangays, San Pablo has diverse infrastructure including:

- **Road network** — City roads, barangay roads, and provincial connections
- **Water systems** — Water distribution, irrigation systems, and water supply facilities
- **Sanitation** — Wastewater treatment, drainage systems, and solid waste management
- **Public buildings** — Schools, health centers, markets, and government offices
- **Utilities** — Electric lines, communication networks, and utility infrastructure

## Major infrastructure systems

### Transportation and roads

San Pablo City has:

- **National highways** — Major routes connecting Laguna to other regions
- **City roads** — Primary, secondary, and tertiary roads within the city
- **Bridges** — Crossings over the seven lakes and river systems
- **Public transportation** — Jeepneys, tricycles, and bus routes

Infrastructure development includes:

- Road widening and improvement projects
- Bridge construction and rehabilitation
- Traffic management systems
- Public transport facilities

### Water supply and sanitation

Water infrastructure includes:

- **Water treatment plants** — Purification facilities for safe drinking water
- **Distribution networks** — Pipes and systems delivering water to homes and businesses
- **Wastewater treatment** — Processing of sewage and wastewater
- **Drainage systems** — Storm water management and flood control

### The Seven Lakes

San Pablo's distinctive infrastructure includes:

- **Lake management systems** — Conservation and utilization facilities
- **Fisheries infrastructure** — Aquaculture and fishing support facilities
- **Recreation facilities** — Parks, docks, and tourism amenities
- **Water quality monitoring** — Systems to maintain lake health

## Public facilities and institutions

### Educational institutions

San Pablo City has:

- **Public elementary schools** — Community-based primary education
- **Public high schools** — Secondary education serving diverse communities
- **Technical schools** — Vocational and technical training centers
- **Higher education institutions** — Universities and colleges offering degree programs

### Health facilities

Health infrastructure includes:

- **City Health Office** — Primary health administration
- **Rural Health Units (RHU)** — Community health services at the barangay level
- **Hospital services** — Medical facilities for hospitalization and emergency care
- **Vaccination centers** — Disease prevention and immunization programs

### Market and commercial facilities

Public facilities for commerce include:

- **Public markets** — Traditional markets with vendor stalls
- **Commercial centers** — Establishments supporting business activities
- **Business development centers** — Support for small and medium enterprises
- **Tourism facilities** — Hotels, restaurants, and lake-based attractions

## Infrastructure challenges and solutions

### Common challenges

- **Road maintenance** — Keeping large road network in good condition
- **Water supply** — Meeting demand in growing urban areas
- **Traffic congestion** — Managing vehicle flow in urban centers
- **Flood management** — Drainage systems during heavy rainfall
- **Solid waste** — Sustainable waste management and disposal

### City government response

The San Pablo City government addresses infrastructure challenges through:

- **Maintenance programs** — Regular repair and upkeep of facilities
- **Capital projects** — Investment in new infrastructure
- **Public-private partnerships** — Collaboration for facility development
- **Community involvement** — Local participation in barangay projects
- **Sustainability initiatives** — Long-term solutions for environmental challenges

## Reporting infrastructure status

### How to report infrastructure problems

Residents can report infrastructure issues such as:

- **Damaged roads** — Holes, cracks, or deterioration
- **Water system problems** — Leaks, low pressure, or contamination
- **Drainage issues** — Clogging, overflow, or maintenance needs
- **Public facility damage** — Structural damage or safety concerns

**To report issues:**

- Call the **City Engineering Office:** (049) 562-1234
- Contact your **Barangay Captain or Barangay Council**
- Visit **City Hall** to file a formal request
- Email **engineering@sanpablocity.gov.ph**
