# City Budget Summary (San Pablo City)

This summary provides an overview of how the City of San Pablo organizes its annual budget and where residents can find budget details.

## Budget structure
The city's budget is typically broken down into:
- **Personnel Services** (salaries, benefits)
- **Maintenance and Other Operating Expenses** (MOOE)
- **Capital Outlay** (infrastructure and capital projects)
- **Debt Service** (loan repayments)

## How to find the official budget numbers
- Visit the San Pablo City official website: https://www.sanpablocity.gov.ph
- Look for the Budget or Financial Reports section, or the Disclosure Portal
- Contact the City Budget Office for official schedules and annexes: (049) 562-1234

## Using budget documents
- Compare the approved budget to budget execution reports to evaluate spending performance
- Review capital outlay lists to see infrastructure priorities
- Check operating budgets of departments to understand resource allocation

For detailed figures, refer to the City's published Annual Budget and Budget Execution Reports available on the official site or at the City Budget Office.
