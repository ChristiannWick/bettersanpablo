# Public Consultations and Community Participation

The City Government of San Pablo is required by the Local Government Code (Republic Act 7160) to conduct public consultations and hearings on significant matters affecting the community, including budget preparation, ordinance legislation, and major development projects.

## What Are Public Consultations?

Public consultations are formal opportunities for residents, community organizations, and stakeholders to provide input, ask questions, and share feedback on city government decisions and policies. They are a key part of participatory governance in the Philippines.

## Types of Public Consultations in San Pablo City

| Consultation Type                     | Purpose                                                                           | Who Can Participate                       |
| ------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Annual Budget Consultations**       | Residents provide input on city spending priorities before the budget is approved | All residents, barangay officials, CSOs   |
| **Ordinance Public Hearings**         | Required before major city ordinances are enacted                                 | Affected parties, residents, stakeholders |
| **CLUP / Zoning Consultations**       | Input on Comprehensive Land Use Plans and zoning changes                          | Residents, businesses, landowners         |
| **Development Project Consultations** | For major infrastructure or development proposals                                 | Affected communities, barangay officials  |
| **Barangay-level Consultations**      | Community meetings organized by barangay offices                                  | Barangay residents                        |

## How to Participate

1. **Watch for announcements** — Public consultations are announced through the city website, official Facebook page, barangay halls, and City Hall bulletin boards.
2. **Register to speak** — Some consultations require pre-registration for those who wish to speak formally. Contact the organizing office for details.
3. **Attend the hearing** — Bring a valid ID. Consultations are usually held at City Hall, the Sangguniang Panlungsod session hall, or barangay halls.
4. **Submit written comments** — If you cannot attend, submit written feedback to the organizing office before the consultation deadline.
5. **Follow up** — The city is required to record and consider public inputs. Follow official channels for updates on how your feedback was considered.

## Civil Society Organization (CSO) Accreditation

Registered CSOs and people's organizations in San Pablo City can participate formally in Local Special Bodies such as:

- **Local Development Council (LDC)**
- **Local Health Board**
- **Local School Board**
- **Local Peace and Order Council**

For CSO accreditation, contact the **City Mayor's Office** or the **Sangguniang Panlungsod Secretariat**.

## Your Rights as a Resident

Under the Local Government Code and the Freedom of Information Executive Order:

- You have the right to **attend and participate** in public hearings and consultations.
- You have the right to **access public documents** related to city budget and programs.
- You have the right to **file FOI requests** for information about city government decisions.
